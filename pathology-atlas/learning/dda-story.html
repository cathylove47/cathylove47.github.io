回到源头：用扩散驱动适配应对测试时的破坏

Back to the Source: Diffusion-Driven Adaptation to Test-Time Corruption

Jin Gao* · Jialing Zhang* · Xihui Liu · Trevor Darrell · Evan Shelhamer† · Dequan Wang†

测试时适配的常规做法是更新模型：用目标域数据继续训练。这篇论文反过来更新数据——用一个只在源域上训练、之后冻结的扩散模型，把每个测试输入投影回源域，再交给源域分类器。整条链路逐样本独立，所以不怕小批量、有序数据或多重破坏混在一起。

读之前，先把这条链路分清

这一段是译者注，不是原文。它只做一件事：把论文里反复出现的「适配谁」和「用多少」两个轴讲清楚，避免把 DDA 和 Tent/MEMO 混成一类方法。

  1. 模型适配（model adaptation）：拿到目标域数据后更新分类器（或其一小部分参数），再预测。Tent 用熵最小化更新批归一化统计与参数，MEMO 对单张图做增广后更新再重置，BUFR 无源地改特征直方图。它们都改「模型」。
  2. 输入适配（input adaptation）：模型一个参数都不动，把输入本身变换成更像源域的样子再预测。DDA 与 DiffPure 属于这一类，区别在条件与集成方式。
  3. DDA 的三步：前向加噪把输入推向噪声侧 → 逆向去噪若干步生成 x0gx^g_0x0g​ → 每一步用低通滤波后的源输入做结构引导，让「身份/类别」别丢。最后把原图与生成图的预测按置信度平均。
  4. 为什么便宜：一个源域扩散模型服务所有目标域，没有逐域的再训练；每个输入独立处理，所以批量大小、样本顺序、破坏类型混合都不影响结果。代价是单样本推理要跑扩散，时间明显高于一次分类。

术语约定：corruption 译「破坏」，distribution shift 译「分布偏移」，test-time adaptation 译「测试时适配」，self-ensembling 译「自集成」，guidance 译「引导」，diffusion range 译「扩散范围」。方法名 DDA、DiffPure、Tent、MEMO、BUFR、ILVR 保留英文。

摘要

English original
Abstract

测试时适配利用测试输入本身，去提升一个在源数据上训练好的模型在分布偏移的目标数据上的准确率。多数方法是更新源模型——在每个目标域上重新训练。再训练固然有帮助，但它对数据的数量与顺序、以及优化的超参数很敏感。我们改为更新目标数据，用一个生成式扩散模型把所有测试输入投影回源域。我们的扩散驱动适配（diffusion-driven adaptation，DDA）方法在所有域之间共享同一个分类模型与同一个生成模型：两者都只在源域上训练，随后冻结用于全部目标域，从而避免昂贵的逐域再训练。我们用图像引导和分类器自集成来增强扩散，让它自动决定要适配多少。在 ImageNet-C 基准上，跨越多种破坏、多种模型与多种数据情形，DDA 的输入适配都比模型适配更稳健。借助逐输入的更新，DDA 在模型适配会退化的场合依然有效：数据太少（小批量）、数据相关（有序）、数据混合（多种破坏）。

English original

Test-time adaptation harnesses test inputs to improve the accuracy of a model trained on source data when tested on shifted target data. Most methods update the source model by (re-)training on each target domain. While re-training can help, it is sensitive to the amount and order of the data and the hyperparameters for optimization. We update the target data instead, and project all test inputs toward the source domain with a generative diffusion model. Our diffusion-driven adaptation (DDA) method shares its models for classification and generation across all domains, training both on source then freezing them for all targets, to avoid expensive domain-wise re-training. We augment diffusion with image guidance and classifier self-ensembling to automatically decide how much to adapt. Input adaptation by DDA is more robust than model adaptation across a variety of corruptions, models, and data regimes on the ImageNet-C benchmark. With its input-wise updates, DDA succeeds where model adaptation degrades on too little data (small batches), on dependent data (correlated orders), or on mixed data (multiple corruptions).

English original
1 Introduction

深度网络在视觉识别上取得了当前最好的性能（He 等，2016；Dosovitskiy 等，2021；Liu 等，2021；Liu 等，2022），但当用于测试的源数据与目标数据之间存在偏移时仍会失灵（Quionero-Candela 等，2009）。偏移可能来自破坏（Hendrycks 与 Dietterich，2019；Mintun 等，2021）、对抗攻击（Goodfellow 等，2015），也可能是仿真与真实之间、不同地点与时间之间的自然差异（Peng 等，2017；Koh 等，2021）。为应对偏移，适配与稳健性技术会更新预测，以提高目标数据上的准确率。本文考察适配的两个基本轴：适配什么——模型还是输入；以及适配多少——是否采用这次更新。我们提出一种由生成式扩散模型驱动的测试时输入适配方法，用来对抗图像破坏造成的偏移。

English original

Deep networks achieve state-of-the-art performance for visual recognition (He et al., 2016; Dosovitskiy et al., 2021; Liu et al., 2021; Liu et al., 2022), but can still falter when there is a shift between the source data and the target data for testing (Quionero-Candela et al., 2009). Shift can result from corruption (Hendrycks and Dietterich, 2019; Mintun et al., 2021); adversarial attack (Goodfellow et al., 2015); or natural shifts between simulation and reality, different locations and times, and other such differences (Peng et al., 2017; Koh et al., 2021). To cope with shift, adaptation and robustness techniques update predictions to improve accuracy on target data. In this work, we consider two fundamental axes of adaptation: what to adapt—the model or the input—and how much to adapt—using the update or not. We propose a test-time input adaptation method driven by a generative diffusion model to counter shifts due to image corruptions.

适配的主导范式是在源域与目标域上联合优化来训练模型（Saenko 等，2010；Torralba 与 Efros，2011；Ganin 等，2016；Tzeng 等，2017；Hoffman 等，2018）。然而训练时适配面临一个关键问题：不知道测试期间数据会怎样不同。训练时的更新可以应对已知的偏移，但部署时若出现新的、不同的偏移呢？这时就需要测试时更新，使模型能够（1）不使用源数据、（2）不中断推理地完成适配。无源适配（source-free adaptation；Sun 等，2020；Varsavsky 等，2020；Iwasawa 与 Matsuo，2021；Kundu 等，2020；Li 等，2020；Liang 等，2020）通过在新的目标上重新训练模型而无需访问源域，满足条件（1）。测试时适配（Schneider 等，2020；Sun 等，2020；Wang 等，2021；Zhang 等，2021b）在推理过程中迭代更新模型，同时满足（1）与（2）。尽管更新模型可以提升稳健性，这些更新本身也有代价与风险。模型更新在计算上可能过于昂贵，无法扩展到很多目标域（每个目标都需要自己的模型）；它们还可能对目标数据的数量与顺序敏感，从而产生无益、甚至损害稳健性的噪声更新。总之，多数方法更新源模型，但这并不能改善所有部署。

English original

The dominant paradigm for adaptation is to train the model by joint optimization over the source and target (Saenko et al., 2010; Torralba and Efros, 2011; Ganin et al., 2016; Tzeng et al., 2017; Hoffman et al., 2018). However, train-time adaptation faces a crucial issue: not knowing how the data may differ during testing. While train-time updates can cope with known shifts, what if new and different shifts should arise during deployment? In this case, test-time updates are needed to adapt the model (1) without the source data and (2) without halting inference. Source-free adaptation (Sun et al., 2020; Varsavsky et al., 2020; Iwasawa and Matsuo, 2021; Kundu et al., 2020; Li et al., 2020; Liang et al., 2020) satisfies (1) by re-training the model on new targets without access to the source. Test-time adaptation (Schneider et al., 2020; Sun et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2021; Zhang et al., 2021b) satisfies (1) and (2) by iteratively updating the model during inference. Although updating the model can improve robustness, these updates have their own cost and risk. Model updates may be too computationally costly, which prevents scaling to many targets (as each needs its own model), and they may be sensitive to different amounts or orders of target data, which may result in noisy updates that do not help or even hinder robustness. In summary, most methods update the source model, but this does not improve all deployments.

我们提出改为更新目标数据。我们的扩散驱动适配方法 DDA 在训练期间于源数据上学习一个扩散模型，然后在测试期间把所有目标域的输入投影回源域。图 1 展示了一个源扩散模型如何在无需模型适配那种昂贵再训练的情况下，对多个目标完成适配。DDA 训练一个扩散模型来替代源数据，从而实现无源适配；并在做预测的同时适配目标输入，从而实现测试时适配。图 2 展示了 DDA 如何先适配输入，再由源分类器直接预测，而无需更新模型。

English original

We propose to update the target data instead. Our diffusion-driven adaptation method, DDA, learns a diffusion model on the source data during training, then projects inputs from all targets back to the source during testing. Figure 1 shows how just one source diffusion model enables adaptation on multiple targets. DDA trains a diffusion model to replace the source data, for source-free adaptation, and adapts target inputs while making predictions, for test-time adaptation. Figure 2 shows how DDA adapts the input then applies the source classifier without model updates.

我们的实验在破坏稳健性上对比输入更新与模型更新。对输入更新，我们评估并消融 DDA，并与对抗防御中的先进扩散方法 DiffPure（Nie 等，2022）比较。对模型更新，我们评估熵最小化方法（Tent（Wang 等，2021）与 MEMO（Zhang 等，2021b）），它们是在线与逐样本（episodic）测试时更新的先进方法；以及 BUFR（Eastwood 等，2021），它是无源离线更新的先进方法。在 ImageNet-C 上，DDA 的稳健性高于 DiffPure 与 MEMO；在 Tent 因数据有限、有序或混合而退化的地方，DDA 仍然有效。DDA 通过适配输入而与模型无关，在标准架构（ResNet-50）以及最新的卷积（ConvNeXt；Liu 等，2022）与注意力（Swin Transformer；Liu 等，2021）架构上都能在无需重新调参的情况下带来提升。

English original

Our experiments compare and contrast input and model updates on robustness to corruptions. For input updates, we evaluate and ablate our DDA and compare it to DiffPure (Nie et al., 2022), the state-of-the-art in diffusion for adversarial defense. For model updates, we evaluate entropy minimization methods (Tent (Wang et al., 2021) and MEMO (Zhang et al., 2021b)), the state-of-the-art for online and episodic test-time updates, and BUFR (Eastwood et al., 2021), the state-of-the-art for source-free offline updates. DDA achieves higher robustness than DiffPure and MEMO across ImageNet-C and helps where Tent degrades due to limited, ordered, or mixed data. DDA is model-agnostic, by adapting the input, and improves across standard (ResNet-50) and state-of-the-art convolutional (ConvNeXt (Liu et al., 2022)) and attentional (Swin Transformer (Liu et al., 2021)) architectures without re-tuning.

我们的贡献：

  • 我们提出 DDA，这是第一个基于扩散的、面向破坏的测试时适配方法，并给出一种新的自集成方案来决定要适配多少。
  • 我们识别并经实验确认了在线模型更新的薄弱点——小批量、有序数据、混合目标——并说明输入更新如何应对这些自然却当前棘手的场景。
  • 我们在 ImageNet-C 基准上实验，表明 DDA 在各种破坏、模型与数据情形下都优于现有的测试时适配方法。
English original

Our contributions:

  • We propose DDA as the first diffusion-based method for test-time adaptation to corruption and include a novel self-ensembling scheme to choose how much to adapt.
  • We identify and empirically confirm weak points for online model updates—small batches, ordered data, and mixed targets—and highlight how input updates address these natural but currently challenging regimes.
  • We experiment on the ImageNet-C benchmark to show that DDA improves over existing test-time adaptation methods across corruptions, models, and data regimes.

2 Related Work

模型适配在目标数据上更新源模型以提高准确率。我们聚焦无源适配——适配时不需要源数据——以及测试时适配——边适配边预测——因为 DDA 是无源且测试时的方法。

English original

Model Adaptation updates the source model on target data to improve accuracy. We focus on source-free adaptation—not needing the source while adapting—and on test-time adaptation—making predictions while adapting—because DDA is a source-free and test-time method.

无源适配（Li 等，2020；Kundu 等，2020；Liang 等，2020；Sun 等，2020）使方法能够尊重部署时对计算、带宽与隐私的现实约束。不过，多数方法通过改动训练（Li 等，2020；Kundu 等，2020；Liang 等，2020；Sun 等，2020；Dubey 等，2021）而带来一定复杂度与计算量，并在每个目标上离线再训练其模型（Li 等，2020；Kundu 等，2020；Liang 等，2020；Eastwood 等，2021；Pandey 等，2021），从而打断测试。DDA 是无源的，它用源扩散建模替代源数据；但它不同之处在于更新数据而非模型，而且不改变分类器的训练，因为扩散模型是独立训练的。通过保持模型固定，DDA 可以处理多个目标而无需为模型再训练而中断测试——这是无源模型适配做不到的。

English original

Source-free adaptation (Li et al., 2020; Kundu et al., 2020; Liang et al., 2020; Sun et al., 2020) makes it possible to respect practical deployment constraints on computation, bandwidth, and privacy. Nevertheless, most methods involve a certain amount of complexity and computation by altering training (Li et al., 2020; Kundu et al., 2020; Liang et al., 2020; Sun et al., 2020; Dubey et al., 2021) and interrupt testing by re-training their model(s) offline on each target (Li et al., 2020; Kundu et al., 2020; Liang et al., 2020; Eastwood et al., 2021; Pandey et al., 2021). DDA is source-free, as it replaces the source data with source diffusion modeling. However, it differs by updating the data rather than the model(s). Furthermore, it does not alter the training of the classifier, as the diffusion model is trained on its own. By keeping its models fixed, DDA handles multiple targets without halting testing for model re-training, as source-free model adaptation does.

测试时适配（Sun 等，2020；Schneider 等，2020；Wang 等，2021；Zhang 等，2021a；Niu 等，2022；Zhou 与 Levine，2021；Tang 等，2023；Niu 等，2023）同时进行更新与预测。

English original

Test-time adaptation (Sun et al., 2020; Schneider et al., 2020; Wang et al., 2021; Zhang et al., 2021a; Niu et al., 2022; Zhou and Levine, 2021; Tang et al., 2023; Niu et al., 2023) simultaneously updates and predicts.

这类测试时模型更新可能对优化超参数、以及测试数据的大小、顺序与多样性敏感。相反，DDA 更新数据，因而在输入之间彼此独立，也就不受测试数据的批量、顺序或混合方式影响。与测试时模型适配不同，DDA 甚至可以对单个测试输入完成适配，且不需要数据增广。

English original

Such test-time model updates can be sensitive to their optimization hyperparameters along with the size, order, and diversity of the test data. On the contrary, DDA updates the data, which makes it independent across inputs, and thereby invariant to batches, orders, or mixtures of the test data. DDA can even adapt to a single test input, without augmentation, unlike test-time model adaptation.

输入适配在源域与目标域之间转换数据。DDA 通过测试时扩散把输入由目标域适配到源域。此前的方法有的在测试时适配，但目的与技术不同；有的在训练时适配，却无法在测试时处理新的目标域。

English original

Input Adaptation translates data between source and target. DDA adapts the input from target to source by test-time diffusion. Prior methods adapt during testing, but differ in their purpose and technique, or adapt during training, but cannot handle new target domains during testing.

在测试期间，从源到目标的转换使得只依赖源域训练的模型也能使用。DiffPure（Nie 等，2022）是与 DDA 最接近的方法，因为它用扩散来防御对抗偏移。然而 DiffPure 与 DDA 分别针对对抗偏移与自然偏移，场景不同，技术也因此不同。DDA 的不同在于其对扩散更新的条件方式，以及在适配前后对预测做自集成。

English original

During testing, translation from source to target enables the use of a source-only model. DiffPure (Nie et al., 2022) is the closest method to DDA because it applies diffusion to defense against the adversarial shift. However, DiffPure and DDA differ in their settings of adversarial and natural shift respectively, and as a result differ in their techniques. DDA differs in its conditioning of the diffusion updates and its self-ensembling of predictions before and after adaptation.

在训练期间，从源到目标的转换可提供额外数据或辅助损失。训练时转换包括风格迁移（Reinhard 等，2001；Pitié 等，2007；Li 等，2018；Yoo 等，2019）、条件图像合成（Zhu 等，2017；Hoffman 等，2018；Huang 等，2018；Park 等，2019；Park 等，2020；Jiang 等，2020；Richter 等，2022），或面向偏移稳健性的对抗生成（Rusak 等，2020）。CyCADA（Hoffman 等，2018）通过 CycleGAN（Zhu 等，2017）生成，在源与目标之间转换来适配。CyCADA 与 DDA 都是生成式的，但 CyCADA 训练时需要成对的源与目标数据，且测试时无法适配多个目标。DDA 只在源上训练一个模型，就能适配多个目标。

English original

During training, translation from source to target provides additional data or auxiliary losses. Train-time translation includes style transfer (Reinhard et al., 2001; Pitié et al., 2007; Li et al., 2018; Yoo et al., 2019), conditional image synthesis (Zhu et al., 2017; Hoffman et al., 2018; Huang et al., 2018; Park et al., 2019; Park et al., 2020; Jiang et al., 2020; Richter et al., 2022), or adversarial generation (Rusak et al., 2020) for robustness to shift. CyCADA (Hoffman et al., 2018) adapts by translating between source and target via generation with CycleGAN (Zhu et al., 2017). While CyCADA and DDA are generative, CyCADA needs paired source and target data for training, and cannot adapt to multiple targets during testing. DDA only trains one model on source to adapt to multiple targets.

扩散建模扩散（Sohl-Dickstein 等，2015；Rombach 等，2022；Song 与 Ermon，2019；Song 与 Ermon，2020；Nichol 与 Dhariwal，2021；Song 等，2021）是近期很强的生成建模路线，它通过迭代细化输入来采样。本质上，扩散学习「反转」噪声，用关于输入的梯度更新来生成图像。噪声的类型很关键，标准扩散依赖高斯噪声。本文研究一个强扩散模型如何把被破坏的目标数据投影到源数据分布附近，即使面对高度非高斯的破坏。

English original

Diffusion Modeling Diffusion (Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Rombach et al., 2022; Song and Ermon, 2019; Song and Ermon, 2020; Nichol and Dhariwal, 2021; Song et al., 2021) is a strong, recent approach to generative modeling that samples by iteratively refining the input. In essence, diffusion learns to ``reverse'' noise to generate an image by gradient updates w.r.t. the input. The type of noise matters, and standard diffusion relies on Gaussian noise. In this work, we investigate how a strong diffusion model can project corrupted target data toward the source data distribution, even on corruptions that are highly non-Gaussian.

我们在这个新角色——扩散驱动适配——中使用去噪扩散概率模型（denoising diffusion probabilistic model，DDPM；Ho 等，2020）。引导式扩散模型通过在类别标签（Dhariwal 与 Nichol，2021；Ho 与 Salimans，2021）、文本（Nichol 等，2022；Liu 等，2023）与图像（Choi 等，2021）上的条件优化来改善生成，但测试时适配恰恰缺少这些条件所需的数据。DDA 在朴素地应用扩散的基础上做了改进，从而在测试时获得更高的抗破坏稳健性。

English original

We apply the denoising diffusion probabilistic model (DDPM) (Ho et al., 2020) in this new role of diffusion-driven adaptation. Guided diffusion models improve generation by optimization conditioned on class labels (Dhariwal and Nichol, 2021; Ho and Salimans, 2021), text (Nichol et al., 2022; Liu et al., 2023), and images (Choi et al., 2021), but test-time adaptation denies the data needed for their use as-is. DDA improves on the straightforward application of diffusion to achieve higher robustness to corruption during testing.

3 面向破坏的扩散驱动适配

5 Discussion

DDA 用扩散建模做测试时输入适配，从而缓解偏移。我们在 ImageNet-C 上的实验确认，把目标数据扩散回源域能提升稳健性。与可能在稀缺、有序、混合数据上挣扎的测试时模型适配不同，我们的方法在这些情形下能可靠地提升准确率。与可能需要为每个目标再训练的无源模型适配不同，我们通过保持源模型固定，自由地把适配扩展到多个目标。这些实际差异源于我们概念上的转变：从模型适配转向输入适配，并采用扩散建模。

DDA mitigates shift by test-time input adaptation with diffusion modeling. Our experiments on ImageNet-C confirm that diffusing target data back to the source domain improves robustness. In contrast to test-time model adaptation, which can struggle with scarce, ordered, and mixed data, our method is able to reliably boost accuracy in these regimes. In contrast to source-free model adaptation, which can require re-training to each target, we are able to freely scale adaption to multiple targets by keeping our source models fixed. These practical differences are due to our conceptual shift from model adaptation to input adaptation and our adoption of diffusion modeling.

Limitations. The strengths and weaknesses of input adaptation complement those of modal adaptation. Although our method can adapt to a single target input, it must adapt from scratch on each input, and so its computation cannot be amortized across the deployment. In contrast, model adaptation by TTT (Sun et al., 2020) or Tent (Wang et al., 2021) can update on each batch while cumulatively adapting the model more and more. Although diffusion can project many targets to the source data, and does so without expensive model re-training, it can fail on certain shifts. If these shifts arise gradually, then model adaptation could gradually update too (Kumar et al., 2020), but our fixed diffusion model cannot.

我们依赖扩散，因此也受制于扩散生成的质量。扩散确实有失效模式，尽管我们的正面结果表明了它当下的用途与未来的潜力。特别是，鉴于扩散模型的容量很大，它可能不仅转换域属性，还会转换其他图像内容。我们对图像引导的使用有助于避免这一点，但代价是限制了对某些破坏的适配能力。新的扩散架构，或专为适配设计的新引导技术，有望解决这些不足。

New diffusion architectures or new guidance techniques specific to adaption could address these shortcomings.

DDA forces the model to preserve the global structural information to avoid semantic drift, leading to a drawback that it may fail to adapt images from certain domains. While DDA performs well when projecting most high-frequency/local corruptions (e.g., Gaussian noise, impulse noise, jpeg encoding, ...), it fails for a few low-frequency/global corruptions (e.g., frost, fog, brightness adjustment, ...). However, our self-ensembling scheme effectively detects these cases to avoid significant drops in accuracy.
