CVPR 2026 · IMAGE RESTORATION / HALLUCINATION SYNTHESIS

HalluGen：为评估图像复原而合成真实、可控的幻觉

HalluGen: Synthesizing Realistic and Controllable Hallucinations for Evaluating Image Restoration

Seunghoi Kim1,2 · Henry F. J. Tregidgo1,2 · Chen Jin4 · Matteo Figini1,3 · Daniel C. Alexander1,3（1UCL Hawkes Institute；2UCL 医学物理与生物医学工程系；3UCL 计算机科学系；4AstraZeneca 英国 DS&AI 人工智能中心。通讯：seunghoi.kim.17@ucl.ac.uk）

评估「幻觉」需要带标签的数据，而幻觉本身就模糊、难以标注——这是一个循环依赖。HalluGen 不再去标注幻觉，而是主动把幻觉造出来：在扩散后验采样（DPS）的迭代里对数据一致性项做分区域的梯度扰动，控制幻觉的类型（内在／外在）、位置（图块）与严重度，同时保留扩散先验让结果看上去真实。作者用它在低场 MRI 增强上造出 4,350 张带图块级标注的图像，并用它给质量指标排队、训练无参考幻觉检测器。

读之前，先把这条链路分清

这一段是译者注，不是原文。它只做一件事：让你在读公式 (1)–(11) 时知道每个量在做什么。

  1. 逆问题：复原写成 y = 𝒜(x) + n。𝒜 是退化算子（低场 MRI 里是「gamma 变换 → 4 倍下采样 → 模糊」），y 是能测到的退化观测，x 是我们想要的干净图像。因为 𝒜 不可逆，解不唯一，生成模型就会在「观测允许、但真实答案不唯一」的地方自己编内容。
  2. 两类幻觉：内在幻觉违反测量一致性，即 𝒜(x̂) ≠ 𝒜(xgt)，把观测里没有的东西硬塞进去；外在幻觉保持测量一致，但落在 𝒜 的「不可见」方向（类似零空间）上，测量看不出来，语义却已经错了。
  3. DPS 的更新：每一步先让模型预测均值 μθ(xt, t)，再沿「测量误差 ‖y − 𝒜(x̂0)‖²」的负梯度走一步（梯度下降＝更贴数据），最后加噪声。HalluGen 的关键改动只有一处：在掩膜 m 内把这一步的方向反过来（梯度上升），于是模型被推离测量一致性，幻觉就被「种」进了指定图块。
  4. 为什么还像真的：扰动只作用在数据一致性项上，去噪网络仍然把样本拉回数据流形附近；再加上后几步把上升权重清零，边界会被扩散先验修平，于是得到「看着合理、其实错」的结构。
  5. 验证模块 HVM：造完一张图要检查它是否真的符合所属类型（内在的要测量域偏离够大，外在的要测量域偏离小、图像域偏离大），用 Cohen's d 衡量，不达标就重采样——这保证了数据集里的标签是「按定义成立」而不是「按人眼猜」。
术语速查（译者注）
测量一致性（measurement consistency）
把重建结果再送进同一个前向算子，是否还能得到原来的测量。内在幻觉破坏它，外在幻觉不破坏。
Tweedie 估计 x̂0(xt)
用当前含噪样本一步预测出的干净图像，是 DPS 里算数据一致性损失的入口。
图块（patch）
幻觉注入的矩形区域；本文用熵挑图块，尺寸 16–24 像素，每张图 1–3 块。
Cohen's d
标准化效应量：(预测均值 − 真值均值) / 合并标准差；HVM 用它做与量纲无关的门槛判断。
SHAFE
本文提出的全参考指标：低通滤波 → 浅层多尺度特征 → 逐图块余弦距离 → 温度 softmax 加权求和。
无参考检测器
只输入（预测 x̂, 测量 y），不需要真值图像，输出「是否有幻觉」的二分类。
效应量 / AUC / SBC
指标基准用四个角度打分：可分性（Cohen's |d|）、可检测性（AUC-ROC）、锐度偏置曲线（对模糊图的胜率曲线下面积）、与严重度的秩相关（Spearman ρ）。

English original
Abstract

生成模型容易产生幻觉：看似合理、但真值中并不存在的结构。在医学影像、工业检测、遥感等安全攸关领域，这类错误会削弱可靠性与信任，因而在图像复原中格外成问题。例如在资源受限地区广泛使用的低场 MRI 中，复原模型对提升低质量扫描至关重要，而幻觉可能导致严重的诊断错误。相关进展一直受制于一种循环依赖：评估幻觉需要标注数据，而这样的标注既昂贵又主观。我们提出 HalluGen，一个基于扩散的框架，能够合成类型、位置与严重度均可控的真实幻觉，产出在感知上真实、语义上错误的输出（分割 IoU 从 0.86 降到 0.36）。借助 HalluGen，我们构建了首个大规模幻觉数据集，包含源自 1,450 张脑部 MR 图像、用于低场增强的 4,350 张带标注图像，从而可以系统地评估幻觉检测与缓解。我们展示了它的两项应用价值：(1) 对图像质量指标进行基准评测，并提出语义幻觉评估（Semantic Hallucination Assessment via Feature Evaluation, SHAFE）——一种带软注意力池化的特征型指标，相比传统指标提升了对幻觉的敏感度；(2) 训练可泛化到真实复原失败案例的无参考幻觉检测器。HalluGen 及其公开数据集共同为安全攸关图像复原中的幻觉评估建立了首个可扩展的基础。录用后我们将公开代码与数据集。

English original
Generative models are prone to hallucinations: plausible but incorrect structures absent in the ground truth. This issue is problematic in image restoration for safety-critical domains such as medical imaging, industrial inspection, and remote sensing, where such errors undermine reliability and trust. For example, in low-field MRI, widely used in resource-limited settings, restoration models are essential for enhancing low-quality scans, yet hallucinations can lead to serious diagnostic errors. Progress has been hindered by a circular dependency: evaluating hallucinations requires labeled data, yet such labels are costly and subjective. We introduce HalluGen, a diffusion-based framework that synthesizes realistic hallucinations with controllable type, location, and severity, producing perceptually realistic but semantically incorrect outputs (segmentation IoU drops from 0.86 to 0.36). Using HalluGen, we construct the first large-scale hallucination dataset comprising 4,350 annotated images derived from 1,450 brain MR images for low-field enhancement, enabling systematic evaluation of hallucination detection and mitigation. We demonstrate its utility in two applications: (1) benchmarking image quality metrics and developing Semantic Hallucination Assessment via Feature Evaluation (SHAFE), a feature-based metric with soft-attention pooling that improves hallucination sensitivity over traditional metrics; and (2) training reference-free hallucination detectors that generalize to real restoration failures. Together, HalluGen and its open dataset establish the first scalable foundation for evaluating hallucinations in safety-critical image restoration. We will make the code and dataset publicly available upon acceptance.

English original
1 Introduction

幻觉——视觉上看似合理、但内容并不正确——是现代生成模型的一种根本失效模式。在图像复原中，模型要从退化的测量里重建缺失信息，这类错误在医学影像、工业检测和遥感等安全攸关领域尤其有害。与对抗攻击不同，幻觉是从生成不确定性中自然涌现的。

English original
Hallucinations, visually plausible yet incorrect content, are a fundamental failure mode of modern generative models. In image restoration, where models reconstruct missing information from degraded measurements, such errors can be especially harmful in safety-critical domains such as medical imaging, industrial inspection, and remote sensing. Unlike adversarial attacks, hallucinations arise naturally from generative uncertainty.

尽管图像复原近年取得了进展 [1]、[2]、[3]、[4]、[5]、[6]，这些模型仍然会产生难以检测、难以度量的幻觉。如图 1（上）所示，进展受制于一种循环依赖：幻觉分析需要标注数据，而幻觉本身既模糊又标注昂贵。度量幻觉并不简单，因为常用指标（PSNR、SSIM、LPIPS [7]）偏好感知锐利度而非正确性，往往会给带有幻觉的输出打出高分。现有评估要么依赖下游任务表现 [8]、[9]、[10]，要么依赖人工标注 [11]、[12]，前者把幻觉与任务准确率混为一谈，后者则局限于简单数据集（如 MNIST [13]、Hands [14]）。

English original
Despite recent advances in image restoration [1]–[6], these models still produce hallucinations that are difficult to detect or measure. As shown in Fig. 1 (top), progress is limited by a circular dependency: hallucination analysis requires labeled data, yet hallucinations are ambiguous and costly to annotate. Measuring hallucinations is non-trivial as common metrics (PSNR, SSIM, LPIPS [7]) favor perceptual sharpness over correctness, often scoring hallucinated outputs highly. Existing evaluations rely on downstream task performance [8]–[10] or manual labels [11], [12], which either conflate hallucinations with task accuracy or are restricted to simple datasets (e.g., MNIST [13], Hands [14]).

为打破这一僵局，我们提出 HalluGen，一个基于扩散、可产生可控且真实幻觉的框架。沿用近期工作 [15]、[9]，我们把幻觉分为 (1) 内在幻觉，它破坏测量一致性；(2) 外在幻觉，它保持测量一致，但语义上是错的。

English original
To break this deadlock, we introduce HalluGen, a diffusion-based framework for controllable and realistic hallucination generation. Following recent work [15], [9], we categorize hallucinations as (1) intrinsic, which break measurement consistency, and (2) extrinsic, which remain consistent but are semantically incorrect.

HalluGen 在基于扩散的逆问题求解器上加入有针对性的梯度扰动，从而可以显式控制幻觉的类型、位置与严重度。可控性至关重要，因为出现在决策关键区域（例如病灶、焊点）的幻觉风险最大。据我们所知，此前没有工作能够为图像复原中的系统性幻觉分析提供可控、且与分类学对齐的幻觉合成能力。我们用扩散后验采样（Diffusion Posterior Sampling）[16] 实例化 HalluGen，不过它与其他扩散表述同样兼容。

English original
HalluGen extends diffusion-based inverse solvers with targeted gradient perturbations, enabling explicit control over hallucination type, location, and severity. Controllability is crucial as hallucinations in decision-critical regions (e.g., lesions, solder joints) pose the greatest risk. To the best of our knowledge, no prior work enables controllable, taxonomically grounded hallucination synthesis for systematic hallucination analysis in image restoration. We instantiate HalluGen with Diffusion Posterior Sampling [16], though it is compatible with other diffusion formulations.

我们的贡献有三点：

  1. 我们提出 HalluGen，一个用于图像复原中可控幻觉生成的扩散框架，可跨医学（HCP）[17]、工业（MVTec AD [18]）与自然（ImageNet [19]）图像泛化。
  2. 借助 HalluGen，我们构建了用于低场 MRI 增强的首个大规模幻觉数据集，包含跨 1,450 张图像的 4,350 个带图块级标注的预测结果。该数据集为系统性幻觉分析奠定了基础。
  3. 利用 HalluGen 生成的数据集，我们展示了两项关键应用：(a) 一个幻觉指标基准，揭示现有指标会忽略幻觉；我们提出语义幻觉评估（SHAFE），一种带软注意力池化的特征型指标，把幻觉检测 AUC 提升 0.25、把假阴性降低 24 个百分点；(b) 在 HalluGen 上训练、在真实复原输出上评估的无参考幻觉检测器，无需真值参考。
English original
Our contributions are threefold: (1) We propose HalluGen, a diffusion-based framework for controllable hallucination generation in image restoration, generalizing across medical (HCP) [17], industrial (MVTec AD [18]), and natural (ImageNet [19]) images. (2) Using HalluGen, we construct the first large-scale hallucination dataset for low-field MRI enhancement, comprising 4,350 predictions with patch-level annotations across 1,450 images. This dataset establishes a foundation for systematic hallucination analysis. (3) Using the HalluGen-generated dataset, we demonstrate two key applications: (a) a hallucination metric benchmark, revealing that existing metrics overlook hallucinations. We develop Semantic Hallucination Assessment via Feature Evaluation (SHAFE), a feature-based metric with soft-attention pooling that improves hallucination detection AUC by 0.25 and reduces false negatives by 24 percentage points; and (b) reference-free hallucination detectors trained on HalluGen and evaluated on real restoration outputs without ground-truth references.

HalluGen 与我们的数据集共同建立了首个用于生成与评估图像复原幻觉的系统性基础，推动安全攸关应用朝着更安全、更可靠的部署前进。

English original
Together, HalluGen and our dataset establish the first systematic foundation for generating and evaluating hallucinations in image restoration, advancing toward safer and more reliable deployment in safety-critical applications.

2 Related Work

2.1 图像复原

English original
2.1 Image Restoration

图像复原的目标是从退化测量中恢复干净、高质量的图像，涵盖超分辨率、去噪、去模糊等任务。现代监督模型为感知质量学习强力的数据驱动先验 [20]、[1]、[2]、[3]、[4]，而近期工作则把预训练扩散模型当作求解逆问题的生成先验 [16]、[21]、[22]、[23]、[24]、[25]、[26]。这些方法已被推广到医学影像，包括压缩感知与低场 MRI 增强 [27]、[28]、[29]、[30]、[31]、[9]、[32]。

English original
Image restoration aims to recover clean, high-quality images from degraded measurements across tasks such as super-resolution, denoising, and deblurring. Modern supervised models learn powerful data-driven priors for perceptual quality [20], [1]–[4], while recent works leverage pre-trained diffusion models as generative priors for inverse problems [16], [21]–[26]. These approaches have been extended to medical imaging, including compressed sensing and low-field MRI enhancement [27]–[32].

然而，强调感知真实感会诱发幻觉——看似合理却不正确的结构——这正是感知–失真权衡的体现 [1]、[33]。在安全攸关领域，幻觉是已知的风险，却基本未被研究，只有少数近期工作 [10]、[9]、[12]、[11] 提出了缓解策略。

English original
However, emphasizing perceptual realism can induce hallucinations, plausible but incorrect structures, reflecting the perception–distortion trade-off [1], [33]. In safety-critical domains, hallucination is a known risk but remains largely unstudied, with only a few recent work [10]–[12] proposing mitigation strategies.

2.2 图像质量指标

English original
2.2 Image Quality Metrics

传统的全参考指标 [34]、[35]、[36] 关注逐像素保真度与全局结构相似性，因而对错位敏感，也难以捕捉局部的、语义上错误的误差。学习型感知指标 [7]、[37] 通过比较深层特征表示提升了与人类感知的相关性，但它们把误差做全局聚合，对空间上稀疏的幻觉仍然不敏感。

English original
Traditional full-reference metrics [34], [35], [36] focus on pixel-wise fidelity and global structural similarity, making them sensitive to misalignment and ineffective at capturing localized or semantically incorrect errors. Learned perceptual metrics [7], [37] improve correlation with human perception by comparing deep feature representations, but they aggregate errors globally and remain insensitive to spatially sparse hallucinations.

在医学图像重建中，近期量化幻觉的努力 [38]、[15] 受限于线性前向模型或特定任务的网络结构，通用性有限。

English original
In medical image reconstruction, recent efforts to quantify hallucinations [38], [15] are restricted to linear forward models or task-specific architectures, limiting generality.

这些局限促成了 SHAFE 指标（第 4.5 节）：它计算局部（例如逐图块）的指标图，再用软注意力权重聚合，从而提高对局部幻觉的敏感度。

English original
These limitations motivate the SHAFE metric (Section 4.5), which computes local (e.g. patch) metric maps and aggregates them using soft-attention weighting, increasing sensitivity to localized hallucinations.

2.3 幻觉基准

English original
2.3 Hallucination Benchmarks

近期针对语言模型与视觉–语言模型（LLM 与 VLM）的基准，评估的是语义幻觉，例如事实错误或无法核实的输出。代表性例子包括用于图文推理的 HaluEval、HalluLens 与 HallusionBench [39]、[40]、[41]，用于物体幻觉的 POPE [42]，以及用于时序幻觉分析的 VideoHallucer [43]。

English original
Recent benchmarks for language and vision-language models (LLMs and VLMs) evaluate semantic hallucinations such as factually incorrect or unverifiable outputs. Examples include HaluEval, HalluLens, and HallusionBench for text–image reasoning [39], [40], [41], POPE for object hallucination [42], and VideoHallucer for temporal hallucination analysis [43].

尽管一些近期工作 [44]、[45]、[46] 尝试合成幻觉以自动化 VLM 的标注，它们对幻觉生成的空间属性（如大小、位置）缺乏控制。据我们所知，目前还没有带空间标注的图像复原幻觉基准，这正是本文填补的空白。

English original
While some recent works [44], [45], [46] attempt to synthesize hallucinations to automate annotation in VLMs, they lack spatial control (e.g., size, location) over how hallucinations are generated. To our knowledge, no benchmark exists for hallucinations in image restoration with spatial annotations, a gap our work addresses.

3.1 问题定义

English original
3.1 Problem Formulation

图像复原的目标是从退化观测 y ∈ ℝM 中恢复高质量图像 x ∈ ℝN，可写成一个逆问题：

English original
Image restoration aims to recover a high-quality image x ∈ ℝN from its degraded observation y ∈ ℝM, formulated as an inverse problem:
公式 (1)
y=A(x)+n,y = \mathcal{A}(x) + n,y=A(x)+n,

其中 𝒜(·) 表示前向退化算子，n 是测量噪声。由于 𝒜 通常不可逆，恢复是不适定的。

English original
where 𝒜(·) denotes the forward degradation operator and n is measurement noise. As 𝒜 is typically non-invertible, recovery is ill-posed.

幻觉分类学。沿用近期关于幻觉的工作 [15]、[9]，我们按幻觉与前向算子 𝒜(·) 的关系对其进行分类。

English original
Hallucination Taxonomy. We categorize hallucinations based on their relationship to the forward operator 𝒜(·), following the recent work on hallucination [15], [9].

内在幻觉违反测量一致性，引入与观测数据不一致的特征：

English original
Intrinsic hallucinations violate measurement consistency, introducing features inconsistent with observed data:
公式 (2)
A(x^)  ≠  A(xgt),\mathcal{A}(\hat{x}) \;\neq\; \mathcal{A}(x_{\text{gt}}),A(x^)=A(xgt​),

其中 x̂ 与 xgt 分别表示重建图像与真值图像。

English original
where x̂ and xgt denote the reconstructed image and ground truth, respectively.

外在幻觉保持测量一致性，但在 𝒜 的逆域中与真值不同：

English original
Extrinsic hallucinations preserve measurement consistency but differ in the inverse domain of 𝒜:
公式 (3)
A(x^)=A(xgt),    but    A−1 ⁣(A(x^))  ≠  A−1 ⁣(A(xgt)).\mathcal{A}(\hat{x}) = \mathcal{A}(x_{\text{gt}}), \;\; \text{but} \;\; \mathcal{A}^{-1}\!\big(\mathcal{A}(\hat{x})\big) \;\neq\; \mathcal{A}^{-1}\!\big(\mathcal{A}(x_{\text{gt}})\big).A(x^)=A(xgt​),butA−1(A(x^))=A−1(A(xgt​)).

这一区分至关重要：内在幻觉可以通过测量一致性检查发现，而外在幻觉需要真值或领域知识。在医学影像中，两类幻觉都构成严重风险，因为它们可能掩盖临床上有意义的细节。理解并系统评估这两类幻觉，正是我们需要可控幻觉生成的原因。

English original
This distinction is crucial: intrinsic hallucinations can be detected through measurement consistency checks, while extrinsic hallucinations require ground truth or domain knowledge. In medical imaging, both pose serious risks as they may obscure clinically significant details. Understanding and systematically evaluating both categories motivates the need for controlled hallucination generation.

5 Conclusion

我们提出 HalluGen，一个用于可控幻觉合成的系统化框架，并构建了首个带图块级标注的图像复原幻觉数据集。我们展示了 HalluGen 在两项关键应用中的价值：(1) 建立一个幻觉指标基准，揭示现有图像质量指标的失效，并提出 SHAFE，通过对特征误差做软注意力池化提升幻觉敏感度；(2) 训练可泛化到真实复原输出的无参考幻觉检测器。这些结果表明，HalluGen 能迁移到真实的模型失败案例，使在真实场景中系统研究幻觉成为可能。HalluGen 也有若干局限，例如由于扩散先验过强，在均匀区域中难以诱发幻觉（图 8），以及缺乏显式的语义控制。当前数据集仅限于 MR 图像，把 HalluGen 扩展到其它安全攸关领域仍是重要方向。尽管如此，HalluGen 为图像复原中幻觉的生成、检测与评估建立了首个系统化基础。我们公开代码、数据集与指标，以支持后续研究与安全攸关领域中的更安全部署。

6.4 失败案例分析

English original
6.4 Failure Case Analysis

在用 HalluGen 构建数据集的过程中，我们观察到三类反复出现的问题。

English original
We observed three recurring challenges during dataset construction with HalluGen.

(1) 对区域纹理与均匀性的敏感性。幻觉的严重度取决于所选区域的局部结构。当掩膜覆盖平滑或均匀区域时，扩散先验可能占据主导，压制诱发幻觉所需的梯度上升信号。对外在情形，这类区域还会缩小前向算子 𝒜(·) 的有效零空间，使语义偏离更难产生。这些问题在很大程度上被 HVM 与基于熵的图块选择缓解，因为二者都偏好信息更丰富的区域。

English original
(1) Sensitivity to regional texture and homogeneity. Hallucination severity depends on the local structure of the selected region. When masks cover smooth or homogeneous areas, the diffusion prior can dominate and suppress the gradient-ascent signal needed to induce hallucination. For extrinsic cases, such regions also shrink the effective null space of the forward operator 𝒜(·), making semantic deviations harder to produce. These issues are largely mitigated by HVM and entropy-based patch selection, which favor more informative regions.

(2) 对精确形态变化的控制有限。由于 HalluGen 依赖随机采样，它无法确定性地指定幻觉结构的精确形态或拓扑。虽然 HalluGen 提供了空间控制与严重度调节，但不能保证不同运行之间得到完全相同的幻觉图案，这指向了未来在结构感知幻觉先验上的工作。

English original
(2) Limited control over precise morphological changes. Because HalluGen relies on stochastic sampling, it cannot deterministically specify the exact morphology or topology of hallucinated structures. While HalluGen provides spatial control and severity modulation, it does not guarantee identical hallucination patterns across runs, suggesting future work on structure-aware hallucination priors.

(3) 扩散先验强烈影响幻觉的真实感。幻觉的真实感取决于扩散先验的数据分布。为研究这一点，我们在 MVTec AD 上训练了两个扩散模型：(i) 单类别、(ii) 多类别。在相同超参数下，单类别先验产生的幻觉更连贯，而多类别先验常常生成不合理的伪影。当先验覆盖多个类别时，强梯度可能把样本推过物体流形。降低梯度强度有助于稳定结果，但自适应或类别感知的梯度调度仍是开放方向。

English original
(3) Diffusion prior strongly influences hallucination realism. Hallucination realism depends on the data distribution of the diffusion prior. To study this, we trained two MVTec AD diffusion models: (i) class-specific and (ii) multi-class. With identical hyperparameters, the class-specific prior produced more coherent hallucinations, whereas the multi-class prior often generated implausible artifacts. Strong gradients can push samples across object manifolds when the prior spans diverse categories. Lower gradient strengths help stabilize results, but adaptive or class-aware gradient schedules remain an open direction.

Effect of Low-Pass Filtering and Weighted-softmax Aggregation. Table 6 shows that both LP filtering and weighted softmax aggregation improve hallucination detection (AUC). LP filtering suppresses high-frequency noise, helping SHAFE emphasize coherent structural differences. Weighted softmax aggregation further highlights patches with stronger feature deviations, and already outperforms uniform pooling, reflecting the spatially localized nature of hallucinations. Combining both modules increases AUC from 0.52 to 0.78, demonstrating the complementary benefits of frequency smoothing and adaptive spatial weighting.

Effect of Temperature τ. Figure 9 illustrates the impact of varying the temperature τ in the softmax aggregation. 8.3 Localization

8.4 SHAFE 的局限

English original
8.4 Limitations of SHAFE

图 11 展示了 SHAFE 的一些典型局限。第一，由于低通滤波会压制高频内容（我们分类学中的幻觉主要出现在低到中频段），SHAFE 对高频伪影（例如周期性网格图案）不够敏感。因此，带有这类伪影的图像可能得到与干净图像相近的 SHAFE 分数。第二，SHAFE 可能对语义上无信息区域中的大幅强度差异给出高响应，例如脑部边界——那里强度失配很大，但结构偏离很小。

English original
Fig. 11 illustrates representative limitations of SHAFE. First, SHAFE is less sensitive to high-frequency artifacts (e.g., periodic grid patterns) due to the low-pass filtering step, which suppresses high-frequency content because hallucinations in our taxonomy mainly occur in the low- to mid-frequency range. Consequently, images with such artifacts may obtain SHAFE scores similar to clean ones. Second, SHAFE can assign high responses to large intensity differences in semantically uninformative regions, such as brain boundaries, where intensity mismatches are high but structural deviation is minimal.

English original
9 Reference-free Hallucination Detector

我们描述无参考幻觉检测器的结构。如图 13 所示，检测器接收测量 y 与预测重建 x̂ 作为输入，二者都先做低通滤波以抑制高频噪声。滤波后的图像通过一个浅层 ResNet-50 特征提取器，其特征被拼接后送入二分类头。我们也尝试过在特征提取之前直接拼接原始输入，但这会让模型关注低层差异（例如小幅位移或纹理变化）而非真正的语义差异。检测器用交叉熵损失训练 100 个 epoch，学习率 0.001，训练数据为 HalluGen 生成的样本。由于它只依赖「测量–预测」对，该检测器支持完全无参考的幻觉检测。

English original
We describe the architecture of our reference-free hallucination detector. As shown in Fig. 13, the detector takes the measurement y and predicted reconstruction x̂ as inputs, both of which are first low-pass filtered to suppress high-frequency noise. The filtered images are passed through a shallow ResNet-50 feature extractor, and their features are concatenated before a binary classification head. We also experimented with concatenating the raw inputs prior to feature extraction, but this caused the model to focus on low-level differences (e.g., small shifts or texture changes) rather than true semantic discrepancies. The detector is trained with cross-entropy loss for 100 epochs using a learning rate of 0.001 on HalluGen-generated samples. Because it relies only on the measurement–prediction pair, the detector supports fully reference-free hallucination detection.
