KLIP：用扩散先验下的 KL 散度检测逆问题中的局部分布偏移

KLIP: Localized Distribution Shift Detection via KL-Divergence with Diffusion Priors in Inverse Problems

Alireza Kheirandish* · Jihoon Hong* · Sara Fridovich-Keil（佐治亚理工学院 电气与计算机工程学院）

论文要解决的问题是：只用一张健康图像训练出来的扩散先验，在求解稀疏视角 CT、高斯去模糊这类逆问题时，能否判断「这次的测量来自分布外（OOD）图像」，并且指出 OOD 特征落在图像的哪个位置。作者的做法是把先验分布与后验分布之间的 KL 散度当作指标，用后验采样过程中本来就计算出的似然分数更新量来估计它，再对空间上的块与时间上的窗口做限制。整个过程不需要任何 OOD 校准数据。

摘要

English original
Abstract

扩散模型作为计算成像的数据驱动先验已经展现出很好的性能，同时也具备一定的分布外（out-of-distribution, OOD）图像检测能力。然而，现有的 OOD 检测方法通常需要关于偏移分布的一些知识，无法检测细微的或局部的分布偏移，而且是在完整图像上操作，而不是在逆问题中实际可得的间接测量上操作。我们提出一种基于扩散先验与后验分布之间 Kullback-Leibler 散度的 OOD 检测指标，它 (i) 不需要任何校准数据，也不需要关于偏移分布的知识；(ii) 既能判定整幅图像为 OOD，也能定位图像内部的 OOD 图块。实验上我们表明，该指标可以检测细微但在语义上有意义的分布偏移，例如从健康肝脏 CT 扫描到带肿瘤的肝脏 CT 扫描的偏移，并且可以泛化到不同类型的扩散模型、数据集与逆问题上。我们的代码见 https://github.com/voilalab/KLIP。

English original
Diffusion models have shown promising performance as data-driven priors for computational imaging, as well as some capacity to detect out-of-distribution (OOD) images. However, existing approaches to OOD detection often require some knowledge of the shifted distribution, fail to detect subtle or localized distribution shifts, and operate on full images, rather than the indirect measurements available in inverse problems. We propose an OOD detection metric based on the Kullback-Leibler divergence between the diffusion prior and the posterior distribution, that (i) does not require any calibration data or knowledge of the shifted distribution, and (ii) can detect whole images as OOD as well as localize OOD patches within an image. Experimentally, we show that this metric can detect subtle yet semantically meaningful distribution shifts, such as the shift from healthy liver CT scans to those with tumors, and generalizes across different types of diffusion models, datasets, and inverse problems. Our code can be found at https://github.com/voilalab/KLIP.

1 引言

English original
1 Introduction

分布偏移检测是机器学习与计算成像中的基础问题，尤其当模型要被部署到开放世界中时。在很多应用里，最需要捕捉的分布偏移恰恰也是最难检测的，它们往往表现为图像中细微或局部的变化。例如在医学影像中，分布外（out-of-distribution, OOD）特征可能只是小块病灶、肿瘤或撕裂，而这些恰恰是图像诊断价值的来源。我们提出用只在分布内（in-distribution, ID）图像上训练过的扩散模型，同时完成欠定图像重建与空间上局部 OOD 特征的检测。

English original
Distribution shift detection is a foundational problem in machine learning and computational imaging, especially when models are to be deployed in an open world. In many applications, the distribution shifts that are most important to catch are also some of the most difficult to detect, often involving subtle or localized changes in an image. In medical imaging, for example, out-of-distribution (OOD) features may consist of small lesions, tumors, or tears that are essential to the diagnostic value of the image. We propose to use diffusion models trained on in-distribution (ID) images to simultaneously perform underdetermined image reconstruction and detection of spatially localized OOD features.

已有许多用于扩散模型 OOD 检测的方法被提出。统计上最严谨的 OOD 检测指标往往基于 conformal prediction [4]、[3]、[20]，但它们需要一份 OOD 图像的校准数据集，而对开放世界中长尾的罕见异常来说，这样的数据集无法获得。其它 OOD 检测方法更为启发式，但依然需要 OOD 校准数据集 [25]，或者需要训练多个扩散模型 [32]，或者是在完整图像上而不是在计算成像可得的间接测量上操作 [10]，又或者只关注具有全局而非局部 OOD 特征的 OOD 图像 [33]。我们则提出一种 OOD 检测指标，它 (i) 不需要任何 OOD 图像的知识或样本，(ii) 可以作用于逆问题推理时可得的图像间接测量，(iii) 可以检测虽小且局部、但在语义上有意义的分布偏移。

English original
Many approaches have been proposed for OOD detection using diffusion models. The most statistically rigorous OOD detection metrics are often based on conformal prediction [4, 3, 20], but these require access to a calibration dataset of OOD images, which is impossible to acquire for the long tail of rare abnormalities that appear in open-world images. Other approaches to OOD detection are more heuristic, but still require an OOD calibration dataset [25], require training multiple diffusion models [32], operate on full images rather than indirect measurements available in computational imaging [10], or focus on distinguishing OOD images with global rather than local OOD features [33]. We instead propose an OOD detection metric that (i) does not require any knowledge or samples of OOD images, (ii) can be applied to indirect measurements of an image that are available at inference time in an inverse problem, and (iii) can detect small and localized, yet semantically meaningful, distribution shifts.

我们的核心想法是：图像先验分布与给定测量之后的后验分布之间的 Kullback-Leibler（KL）散度，对 OOD 图像应当更大，对 OOD 图像内部的 OOD 区域也应当更大。这个 KL 散度可以只用「在给定一组测量的条件下、扩散采样过程中产生的更新步」来估计，不需要任何 OOD 校准数据。借助「扩散模型在采样过程的不同阶段生成图像的不同成分」这一知识 [8]、[30]，我们进一步把该 KL 散度指标细化：在采样轨迹的特定时间窗口上、以及在图像中空间局部的各个块上估计它的限制。

English original
Our core idea is that the Kullback-Leibler (KL) divergence between the prior distribution of the image, and the posterior distribution of the image given the measurements, should be larger for OOD images and larger for OOD regions within an OOD image. This KL divergence can be estimated using only the update steps generated throughout diffusion sampling conditioned on a set of measurements, requiring no OOD calibration data. Using the knowledge that diffusion models generate different components of an image at different stages of the sampling process [8, 30], we further refine this KL divergence metric by estimating its restriction over specific time windows of the sampling trajectory, and over spatially localized blocks in the image.

我们在一系列带有局部 OOD 特征的图像重建任务上评估所提出的、基于 KL 散度的 OOD 检测指标 KLIP。主要结果集中在稀疏视角 X 射线计算机断层成像（CT）上，其中的扩散先验在一个健康扫描数据集上训练。我们演示了数据集级与图像级的 OOD 检测，检测对象包括合成星形与真实感的合成肝脏肿瘤 [15]，并覆盖两种不同的扩散模型骨干：一种在整幅图像上训练 [37]，另一种在图像块上训练 [14]。我们还用名人面孔上训练得到的扩散先验 [41]，在高斯去模糊任务上对角色面部异常特征的图像级检测，来展示 KLIP 的泛化潜力。如 图 1 所示，KLIP 能定位 (a) 一个合成星形伪影的位置、(b) 一张肝脏 CT 扫描中肿瘤的位置，以及 (c) 《星际迷航：航海家号》角色 Seven of Nine 左眼周围植入物的位置。

English original
We evaluate our proposed KL divergence based OOD detection metric, KLIP, on a range of image reconstruction tasks with localized OOD features. Our primary results focus on sparse view X-ray computed tomography (CT), where the diffusion prior is trained over a dataset of healthy scans. We demonstrate dataset-level and image-level OOD detection of both synthetic stars and realistic synthetic liver tumors [15], across two different diffusion model backbones, one trained on whole images [37] and another trained on image patches [14]. We also demonstrate KLIP's generalization potential via image-level detection of anomalous character facial features in a Gaussian de-blurring task, using a diffusion prior trained on celebrity faces [41]. As shown in Figure 1, KLIP can locate the positions of (a) a synthetic star artifact and (b) tumor within a liver CT scan, and (c) the implant around the left eye of Seven of Nine, a character from Star Trek: Voyager.

English original
To summarize, we:
  • 提出一种近似 OOD 检测指标 KLIP，它基于逆问题中先验分布与后验分布在时间步与块上的限制 KL 散度。我们发现这些时间步与块上的限制为观察分布偏移下的采样过程提供了一个信息丰富的窗口。KLIP 免校准，并且可以用单个在 ID 图像上训练过的扩散模型计算。

English original
  • Introduce a near-OOD detection metric, KLIP, based on the timestep and block restricted KL divergence between prior and posterior distributions in an inverse problem. We find that these timestep and block restrictions offer a rich window into the sampling process under distribution shift. 基于校准的方法

English original
Calibration-based methods.

许多 OOD 检测方法，无论是否使用扩散模型，都依赖一份 OOD 图像校准数据集，用来估计 ID–OOD 的判定边界 [25]，或者训练一个单独的 OOD 扩散模型 [32]。这些方法包括 conformal prediction [4]、[13]、[39]，它能给出围绕 OOD 图像重建结果的、统计上严谨的置信区间。它们还包括在图像内部像素级或区域级量化不确定性的方法 [3]、[20]，以及为降低不确定性而自适应采集更多测量的方案 [44]、[16]。当 ID 与 OOD 数据都可获得时这些方法可以工作得很好，但对于「分布偏移刚刚出现时」的检测、以及检测开放世界中长尾的罕见 OOD 图像并不适用。

English original
Many approaches to OOD detection, with and without diffusion models, rely on a calibration dataset of OOD images with which to estimate an ID–OOD decision boundary [25] or train a separate OOD diffusion model [32]. These methods include conformal prediction [4, 13, 39], which can provide statistically rigorous confidence intervals around OOD image reconstructions. They also include methods that quantify uncertainty at the pixel or region level within an image [3, 20], and adaptive schemes to collect additional measurements to reduce uncertainty [44, 16]. While these methods can work well when both ID and OOD data is available, they are not applicable to distribution shift detection when it first arises, or to detecting the long tail of rare OOD images that exist in an open world.

不确定性量化方法

English original
Uncertainty quantification methods.

已有许多不确定性量化方法被提出，但它们并不专门针对 OOD 检测，也不专门针对分布偏移带来的不确定性。例如，已有蒙特卡洛方法用来估计因测量有限而产生的不确定性 [29]、[1]、[2]，也有集成方法用来量化所学前验权重上的不确定性 [21]、[17]。还有一些不确定性量化方法通过用物理模型模拟校准数据，从而避免使用校准数据集 [38]，这在具有强物理约束（例如偏微分方程）的逆问题中尤其常见 [45]、[50]、[29]。我们的关注点不同：在带扩散先验的一般线性逆问题中，于图像重建过程中免校准地检测局部 OOD 特征。已有的免校准局部 OOD 特征检测方法，关注的是在其它方面高度一致的图像中发现异常，例如制造业的质量控制 [11]，而不是在更多样的 ID 图像中做这件事。

English original
Many approaches have been proposed for uncertainty quantification, but are not specifically focused on OOD detection or uncertainty arising from distribution shifts. For example, Monte Carlo methods have been proposed to estimate uncertainty due to limited measurements [29, 1, 2], and ensemble methods have been proposed to quantify uncertainty over the weights of the learned prior [21, 17]. Some approaches to uncertainty quantification avoid the need for a calibration dataset by simulating calibration data with a physical model [38], especially in inverse problems with strong physical constraints such as partial differential equations [45, 50, 29]. Our focus is instead on calibration-free detection of local OOD features during image reconstruction, in generic linear inverse problems with diffusion priors. Existing approaches to calibration-free detection of localized OOD features have focused on detecting anomalies in otherwise highly consistent images, such as those for quality control in manufacturing [11], rather than in more diverse ID images.

用生成先验做 OOD 检测

English original
OOD detection with generative priors.

在我们关注的「用生成式（具体说是基于扩散的）先验做免校准 OOD 检测」这一领域内也有方法被提出，不过它们通常无法检测那些本身仍属分布内的图像中的局部 OOD 特征 [10]、[33]、[40]。OOD 检测方法的一个常见假设是 OOD 样本对应先验分布下的低密度区域 [6]；[26] 表明对生成模型来说并非如此，[27] 则提出基于分布「典型集（typical set）」概念的另一种 OOD 检测检验。虽然这种做法可以改善数据集级 OOD 检测，但它尚未被评估过是否具备检测我们关注的那种高度局部化 OOD 特征的能力。

English original
Methods have also been proposed within our focus area of calibration-free OOD detection with generative (specifically diffusion-based) priors, though these generally do not allow for detection of localized OOD features in images that are otherwise in-distribution [10, 33, 40]. A common assumption of OOD detection methods is that OOD samples correspond to regions of low density under the prior distribution [6]; [26] showed that this is not the case for generative models, and [27] proposed an alternative OOD detection test based on the notion of a distribution's typical set. While this approach can improve dataset-level OOD detection, it has not been evaluated for capacity to detect the sort of highly localized OOD features that are our focus.

Models & Datasets.

我们在不同设置下评估所提出的 OOD 指标 KLIP（式 (12)）的有效性。首先以计算机断层成像（CT）作为逆问题，为此考虑两种不同的扩散模型：基于 predictor-corrector 的模型 [35]、[37]，以及基于块的模型（PaDIS）[14]。选择这两个模型是为了评估 KLIP 的泛化性，因为它们不仅在采样算法上不同，在似然分数的近似方式上也不同。关于各方法在后验采样中所做近似的更多细节见 7 节。

English original
We evaluate the effectiveness of our proposed OOD metric KLIP (Equation (12)), across different settings. We first consider computed tomography (CT) as our inverse problem, for which we consider two distinct diffusion models: a predictor-corrector based model [35, 37] and a patch-based model (PaDIS) [14]. We selected these models to assess the generalizability of KLIP, as these models differ not only in the sampling algorithm, but also in how the likelihood score is approximated. Further details on the approximations involved in each method for posterior sampling can be found in Section 7.

我们把内点分布定义为健康腹部 CT 扫描，把肝脏肿瘤作为内点分布中不存在的局部 OOD 特征。两个扩散模型都在 Combined Healthy Abdominal Organ Segmentation（CHAOS）数据集 [18] 的训练集上训练，该数据集包含 40 位不同患者的 512 × 512 分辨率腹部 CT 扫描。由于数据集中的患者都是肝脏健康的潜在肝捐献者，肝脏肿瘤必然是既局部又分布外的。我们从测试集中选取 100 幅图像作为 ID 集，并向 ID 集的每一幅图像加入真实感合成肿瘤，从而生成一个 OOD 集。合成肝脏肿瘤按 [15] 的方法生成，其结果与真实患者身上的肿瘤非常接近。

We define the inlier distribution as healthy abdominal CT scans, and liver tumors as the local OOD features absent in the inlier distribution. We train both diffusion models on the training set of the Combined Healthy Abdominal Organ Segmentation (CHAOS) dataset [18], which contains 512 ×\times× 512 resolution abdominal CT scans of 40 different patients. Since the patients whose CT scans are included in the dataset are potential liver donors with healthy livers, liver tumors are guaranteed to be both localized and out of distribution. We select a subset of 100 images from the test set as the ID set, and generate an OOD set by adding realistic synthetic tumors to each image of the ID set. We generate synthetic liver tumors following [15], whose results closely resemble the tumors found in real patients.

We generate two locally OOD datasets: one by adding realistic synthetic scars of various shapes to the faces of 20 different CelebA test images, and another by collecting 15 images of human-like movie and television characters, including those from Star Trek and Harry Potter, whose faces contain localized unusual features not present in the ID CelebA dataset [23].

Experiments.

6 讨论

English original
6 Discussion

我们关注一个研究不足且困难的任务：在只能访问图像间接测量（逆问题语境）的条件下，检测图像中空间上局部的 OOD 特征。我们提出一种 OOD 检测指标 KLIP，它基于先验分布与后验分布之间在空间与时间上受限的 KL 散度，可以在用扩散模型做后验采样时高效估计。该指标在多种成像逆问题（稀疏视角 CT 与高斯去模糊）与内点分布（腹部 CT 扫描与人脸）上、在数据集级与图像级检测空间局部 OOD 特征两类任务中，都优于现有的基于扩散模型的 OOD 检测方法。它还能迁移到不同的扩散模型上，包括整图结构与基于块的结构。

English original
We focus on the understudied and challenging task of detecting spatially localized OOD features in images, given access only to indirect measurements of those images in the context of an inverse problem. We propose an OOD detection metric, KLIP, based on the spatially and temporally restricted KL divergence between the prior and posterior distributions, which we can estimate efficiently during posterior sampling with a diffusion model. This metric outperforms existing diffusion-based OOD detection methods across diverse imaging inverse problems (sparse-view CT and Gaussian deblurring) and inlier distributions (abdominal CT scans and human faces), for both dataset-level and image-level OOD detection of spatially localized OOD features. It also transfers across diffusion models, including both whole-image and patch-based architectures.

尽管结果令人鼓舞，我们的工作仍受以下局限约束。第一，KLIP 对块大小与时间窗口这两个超参数敏感，采用更精细的调参策略很可能进一步提升性能。我们认为这种敏感性源于真实局部 OOD 伪影在大小与形状上的差异，而正是这些差异影响了检测时的最优块大小与时间窗口。第二，我们的评估框架在技术上是逆问题自证（inverse crime），因为我们用同一个前向模型来模拟测量（CT 投影与模糊图像）并做后验采样重建。这虽然为比较不同 OOD 检测方法提供了一个公平的试验台，而且我们在 10 节展示了对模型误设的一定鲁棒性，但我们仍希望未来的工作能在真实世界数据上、针对多样的局部 OOD 检测任务验证并改进 KLIP。

English original
While our results are promising, they are also subject to the following limitations. We posit that this sensitivity is due to variation in the size and shape of real localized OOD artifacts, which influences the optimal block sizes and time windows for detection. Second, our evaluation framework is technically an inverse crime, because we use the same forward models to simulate the measurements (CT projections and blurred images) and perform posterior sampling reconstruction. While this provides a fair testbed to compare different OOD detection methods, and we show some robustness to model misspecification in Section 10, we hope that future work will validate and refine KLIP on real-world data for diverse local OOD detection tasks.

10 Forward Model Mismatch
