以下是译者注，不是原文。它只做一件事：让你在读到公式 (1)–(5) 时知道每个量在做什么，以及这篇论文的三组对比各自在比什么。

  1. 前向算子：多线圈 MRI 的测量写成 ki=F(Six∗)+zik_i = \mathcal{F}(S_i x^\ast) + z_iki​=F(Si​x∗)+zi​，其中 SiS_iSi​ 是第 iii 个线圈的敏感度图，F\mathcal{F}F 是傅里叶变换。欠采样只是用一个二值掩膜 MMM 把一部分 k 空间置零，得到 k~\tilde{k}k~。
  2. 展开（unrolled）重建：把 TTT 次「数据一致性 + 去噪」迭代展开成一个网络，每次迭代叫一个级联（cascade）。数据一致性（DC）就是把采样位置上的 k 空间值拉回测量值；去噪器负责补上缺失的结构。
  3. 谁被冻结：CLIP / DINOv2 / BiomedCLIP 的 ViT-B 编码器全程冻结，只训练「多层特征融合 + UNETR 式解码器」。所以本文的对比不是「大模型微调 vs 小模型」，而是「冻结的通用特征当先验 vs 端到端任务专用网络」。
  4. 两个对照轴：一是分布内（CMRxRecon 训练+测试），二是跨域（CMRxRecon 训练 → fastMRI 膝/脑测试，且测试掩膜的中心比例换成了 0.04 / 0.08，训练时是 24 条 ACS 线）。后者同时变了「解剖」和「采样模式」两件事。
  5. 指标：SSIM 越大越好（论文把它当作首要指标），PSNR 越大越好，NMSE 越小越好。

English original
Abstract

大规模预训练基础模型的出现改变了计算机视觉，使其在多样的下游任务上取得强劲性能。然而，它们在基于物理的逆问题（例如加速心脏 MRI 重建）上的潜力仍很大程度上未被探索。在本工作中，我们研究自然域基础模型能否作为加速心脏 MRI 重建的有效图像先验，并把所得性能与 BiomedCLIP 等领域专用对应模型进行比较。我们提出一个展开式重建框架，在每个级联中引入预训练且冻结的视觉编码器（如 CLIP、DINOv2 与 BiomedCLIP），以引导重建过程。通过大量实验，我们表明：尽管 E2E-VarNet 这类任务专用的最先进重建模型在标准的分布内设定下取得更优性能，基于基础模型的方法仍具竞争力。更重要的是，在具有挑战性的跨域场景中——模型在心脏 MRI 上训练、在解剖结构不同的膝关节与脑数据集上评估——基础模型表现出更好的鲁棒性，尤其是在高加速因子与低频采样受限的情况下。我们进一步观察到，以自然图像预训练的模型（如 CLIP）学到了高度可迁移的结构表征，而领域专用预训练（BiomedCLIP）在更病态的区间带来适度额外增益。总体而言，我们的结果表明，预训练基础模型提供了一个有前景的可迁移先验来源，能在加速 MRI 重建中改善鲁棒性与泛化能力。代码：github.com/Hashmi360/CMR-Recon。

English original
The emergence of large-scale pretrained foundation models has transformed computer vision, enabling strong performance across diverse downstream tasks. However, their potential for physics-based inverse problems, such as accelerated cardiac MRI reconstruction, remains largely underexplored. In this work, we investigate whether natural-domain foundation models can serve as effective image priors for accelerated cardiac MRI reconstruction, and compare the performance obtained against domain-specific counterparts such as BiomedCLIP. We propose an unrolled reconstruction framework that incorporates pretrained, frozen visual encoders, such as CLIP, DINOv2, and BiomedCLIP, within each cascade to guide the reconstruction process. Through extensive experiments, we show that while task-specific state-of-the-art reconstruction models such as E2E-VarNet achieve superior performance in standard in-distribution settings, foundation-model-based approaches remain competitive. More importantly, in challenging cross-domain scenarios, where models are trained on cardiac MRI and evaluated on anatomically distinct knee and brain datasets—foundation models exhibit improved robustness, particularly under high acceleration factors and limited low-frequency sampling. We further observe that natural-image-pretrained models, such as CLIP, learn highly transferable structural representations, while domain-specific pretraining (BiomedCLIP) provides modest additional gains in more ill-posed regimes. Overall, our results suggest that pretrained foundation models offer a promising source of transferable priors, enabling improved robustness and generalization in accelerated MRI reconstruction. English original
1. Introduction

心血管疾病仍是全球首要死因，这凸显了准确、无创的成像对早期诊断与监测的重要性 [38]。心脏磁共振（CMR）成像因能在不使用电离辐射的情况下对心脏解剖、功能与组织特性做出高分辨率评估，已成为强有力的工具 [11]、[21]。

English original
Cardiovascular disease continues to be the leading cause of mortality worldwide, underscoring the importance of accurate and non-invasive imaging for early diagnosis and monitoring [38]. Cardiac magnetic resonance (CMR) imaging has emerged as a powerful tool due to its ability to provide high-resolution assessment of cardiac anatomy, function, and tissue properties without the use of ionizing radiation [11], [21].

尽管临床收益明确，MRI 采集本身很慢，因为 k 空间测量必须在扫描过程中顺序采集。长时间采集会导致患者不适与运动伪影 [39]。加速 MRI 通过欠采样 k 空间来缩短扫描时间；然而，从这种不完整的测量重建图像是一个病态逆问题（即测量数少于未知数）[12]，需要强先验信息。

English original
Despite its clinical benefits, MRI acquisition is inherently slow because k-space measurements must be collected sequentially during the scan. Long acquisition times can lead to patient discomfort and motion artifacts [39]. Accelerated MRI addresses this challenge by undersampling k-space to shorten scan duration; however, reconstructing images from such incomplete measurements is an ill-posed inverse problem (i.e. there are fewer measurements than unknowns) [12] that requires strong prior information.

近年来，基于深度学习的重建方法已成为应对这一挑战的有力方案 [12]、[26]。特别是展开式网络，通过把基于物理的数据一致性与学习到的图像先验结合起来，取得了最先进性能 [38]，有效把 MRI 采集模型与数据驱动的表征整合在一起。然而，这些传统深度学习模型通常为特定任务设计，部署到新设定时往往需要大量重训练或微调 [30]。此外，其临床适用性常受域偏移的挑战——这些偏移来自采集协议、成像设置、扫描仪硬件以及患者人群解剖差异的变化 [8]。这些局限凸显了一个关键挑战：如何开发能在多样设定下可靠泛化的重建方法。

English original
In recent years, deep learning–based reconstruction approaches have emerged as a powerful solution to this challenge [12], [26]. In particular, unrolled networks have achieved state-of-the-art performance [38] by combining physics-based data consistency with learned image priors, effectively integrating the MRI acquisition model with data-driven representations. However, these conventional deep learning models are typically designed for specific tasks and often require substantial retraining or fine-tuning when deployed in new settings [30]. Moreover, their clinical applicability is frequently challenged by domain shifts arising from variations in acquisition protocols, imaging settings, scanner hardware, and anatomical differences across patient populations [8]. These limitations highlight a key challenge: how to develop reconstruction methods that generalize reliably across diverse settings.

更近期，更广泛的深度学习社区日益转向大规模基础模型，这些模型在大量数据集上预训练，并在任务与领域之间展现出强泛化能力 [3]、[15]。它们的成功在自然语言处理中尤其具有变革性 [4]、[18]、[28]，并最近扩展到计算机视觉与医学成像 [5]、[17]、[22]、[27]、[29]、[36]、[41]。通过从大规模数据中学习丰富、可迁移的表征，这些模型即使在低数据或零样本区间也能取得强性能，减少了对任务专用监督的需求。这一范式转变为高度专门化的架构提供了一个有前景的替代方案：把预训练模型当作通用先验来用。

English original
More recently, the broader deep learning community has increasingly shifted toward large-scale foundation models, which are pretrained on extensive datasets and exhibit strong generalization across tasks and domains [3], [15]. Their success has been particularly transformative in natural language processing [4], [18], [28], and has recently been extended to computer vision and medical imaging [5], [17], [22], [27], [29], [36], [41]. By learning rich, transferable representations from large-scale data, these models enable strong performance even in low-data or zero-shot regimes, reducing the need for task-specific supervision. This paradigm shift suggests a promising alternative to highly specialized architectures: leveraging pretrained models as general-purpose priors.

然而，把基础模型应用于医学成像，特别是应用于 MRI 重建这类基于物理的逆问题，仍基本未被探索。一个关键挑战在于大规模预训练所用的自然图像与医学成像数据之间的域差距。医学成像常因隐私与采集成本而受限于标注数据不足 [15]、[23]、[24]，这使它很适合迁移学习；但从自然图像中学到的表征能否有效迁移到这个本质不同的领域，仍不清楚。

English original
However, applying foundation models to medical imaging, and in particular to physics-based inverse problems such as MRI reconstruction–remains largely unexplored. A key challenge lies in the domain gap between natural images used for large-scale pretraining and medical imaging data. While medical imaging is often constrained by limited annotated data due to privacy and acquisition costs [15], [23], [24], making it well-suited for transfer learning, it is unclear whether representations learned from natural images can effectively transfer to this fundamentally different domain.

这些挑战促使我们转换视角：与其设计越来越任务专用的重建架构，我们研究基础模型学到的可迁移表征能否作为加速 MRI 重建的有效先验。

English original
These challenges motivate a shift in perspective: rather than designing increasingly task-specific reconstruction architectures, we investigate whether the transferable representations learned by foundation models can serve as effective priors for accelerated MRI reconstruction.

沿着这一视角，我们研究冻结的视觉基础模型能否作为基于物理的 MRI 重建的有效先验，并把自然图像预训练模型与 BiomedCLIP [41] 等领域专用对应模型进行比较。我们提出一个展开式重建框架，在重建流水线的每个级联中整合预训练视觉编码器，如 CLIP [29]、DINOv2 [27] 与 BiomedCLIP [41]。基础模型在训练期间保持冻结，提供引导迭代精化的特征表征，而数据一致性操作则保持对采集到的 k 空间测量的忠实。

English original
Building on this perspective, we investigate whether frozen vision foundation models can serve as effective priors for physics-based MRI reconstruction, and compare natural-image-pretrained models with domain-specific counterparts such as BiomedCLIP [41]. We propose an unrolled reconstruction framework that integrates pretrained visual encoders, such as CLIP [29], DINOv2 [27], and BiomedCLIP [41], within each cascade of the reconstruction pipeline. The foundation models remain frozen during training and provide feature representations that guide iterative refinement, while data-consistency operations enforce fidelity to the acquired k-space measurements.

通过在 CMRxRecon 2023 数据集 [34] 上的大量实验，我们观察到，任务专用重建模型（如 E2E-VarNet [32]）在标准分布内设定下取得更优性能——这符合预期，因为它们是在领域专用 MRI 数据上端到端优化的。然而，在更具挑战性的条件下，趋势显著不同。在跨域评估和更高加速因子下（对应越来越病态的重建问题），两种方法的性能差距大幅缩小。在这些区间，基于基础模型的重建变得有竞争力，并在部分情况下超过任务专用基线。我们进一步观察到，以自然图像预训练的模型（如 CLIP）学到高度可迁移的结构表征，而领域专用预训练（BiomedCLIP）在更病态的区间带来适度额外增益。

English original
Through extensive experiments on the CMRxRecon 2023 dataset [34], we observe that task-specific reconstruction models, such as E2E-VarNet [32], achieve superior performance in standard in-distribution settings, as expected due to their end-to-end optimization on domain-specific MRI data. However, a markedly different trend emerges under more challenging conditions. In cross-domain evaluations and at higher acceleration factors, corresponding to increasingly ill-posed reconstruction problems, the performance gap between the two approaches narrows substantially. In these regimes, foundation-model-based reconstructions become competitive and, in some cases, surpass task-specific baseline. We further observe that natural-image-pretrained models, such as CLIP, learn highly transferable structural representations, while domain-specific pretraining (BiomedCLIP) provides modest additional gains in more ill-posed regimes.

这些发现揭示出一种依赖区间的权衡：当有充足的领域专用数据时，全监督重建网络表现出色；而在分布偏移下，预训练基础模型提供更鲁棒、更可迁移的先验。总体而言，这表明大规模视觉预训练捕获的结构表征能够泛化到自然图像之外，并可有效用于医学图像重建，尤其是在具有挑战性的跨域设定中。

English original
These findings reveal a regime-dependent trade-off: while fully supervised reconstruction network excel when sufficient domain-specific data is available, pretrained foundation models provide more robust and transferable priors under distribution shifts. Overall, this suggests that large-scale visual pretraining captures structural representations that generalize beyond natural images and can be effectively leveraged for medical image reconstruction, particularly in challenging, cross-domain settings.

总之，我们的主要贡献是：

  1. 据我们所知，我们给出了最早研究冻结视觉基础模型作为基于物理的 MRI 重建先验的工作之一。我们提出一个展开式重建框架，在每个级联中整合预训练编码器（CLIP、DINOv2、BiomedCLIP），并比较领域专用（BiomedCLIP）与自然图像预训练模型。
  2. 我们在分布内与具有挑战性的跨域设定上评估性能，揭示出一种依赖区间的行为：任务专用模型在标准条件下占优，而随着重建难度与域偏移增加，基于基础模型的方法日益具有竞争力。
  3. 我们表明，大规模预训练视觉表征尽管是从自然图像中学到的，却捕获了可迁移的结构先验，能泛化到解剖结构不同的域上的 MRI 重建。
English original
In summary, our main contributions are: (1) To the best of our knowledge, we present one of the first studies investigating frozen vision foundation models as priors for physics-based MRI reconstruction. We introduce an unrolled reconstruction framework that integrates pretrained encoders (CLIP, DINOv2, BiomedCLIP) within each cascade, and compare domain-specific (BiomedCLIP) and natural-image-pretrained models. (2) We evaluate performance across both in-distribution and challenging cross-domain settings, revealing a regime-dependent behavior in which task-specific model dominate under standard conditions, while foundation-model-based approaches become increasingly competitive as reconstruction difficulty and domain shift increase. (3) We show that large-scale pretrained visual representations, despite being learned from natural images, capture transferable structural priors that can generalize to MRI reconstruction across anatomically distinct domains.

Foundation Models.

Foundation models have significantly advanced transfer learning, enabling pretrained models to generalize across diverse tasks [15]. 3. Methodology

The primary objective of this work is to evaluate the effectiveness of vision foundation models in the context of MRI reconstruction. In particular, we aim to assess whether features learned from large-scale pretraining, primarily on natural images–can serve as transferable priors for reconstructing undersampled MRI data. To this end, we propose an unrolled reconstruction framework that integrates pretrained vision foundation models within a physics-based MRI reconstruction pipeline. Given undersampled multi-coil k-space measurements, the objective is to recover an image that is both consistent with the acquired data and guided by informative structural priors. The reconstruction is modeled as a sequence of cascades, each comprising a data-consistency step and a learned refinement module.

Unlike conventional approaches that learn priors solely from task-specific MRI data, our method incorporates frozen visual encoders–such as CLIP [29], DINOv2 [27], and BiomedCLIP [41], within each cascade. These encoders extract feature representations from intermediate reconstructions, which are used to guide iterative refinement. This design enables the model to leverage large-scale pretrained knowledge while maintaining fidelity to the underlying acquisition physics.

3.1 网络架构

English original
3.1. Network Architecture

我们的架构遵循展开式重建范式，由一系列级联迭代精化图像。每个级联包含一个 k 空间中的数据一致性步骤和一个图像域精化模块。数据一致性操作强制与采集测量一致，精化模块则引入学习到的先验以改善重建质量。与依赖任务专用 CNN 先验的传统方法不同，我们引入一个基础模型引导的去噪器，它利用预训练视觉表征。该模块把一个冻结的视觉 Transformer 编码器与一个轻量解码器整合，使每个级联内都能使用可迁移特征。我们先描述所提出的基于基础模型的去噪器架构，然后说明它在展开式重建框架中的整合方式。

English original
Our architecture follows an unrolled reconstruction paradigm, where a sequence of cascades iteratively refines the image. Each cascade consists of a data-consistency step in k-space and an image-domain refinement module. The data-consistency operation enforces agreement with acquired measurements, while the refinement module incorporates learned priors to improve reconstruction quality. In contrast to conventional approaches that rely on task-specific CNN priors, we introduce a foundation-model-guided denoiser, which leverages pretrained visual representations. This module integrates a frozen vision transformer encoder with a lightweight decoder, enabling the use of transferable features within each cascade. We first describe the architecture of the proposed foundation-model-based denoiser, followed by its integration within the unrolled reconstruction framework.

基础模型去噪器

English original
Foundation Model Denoiser.

给定一个中间重建结果，先把复数 MRI 图像转成幅度图，并用基于百分位的缩放 [7] 归一化以减少离群值。随后图像被复制为三个通道、缩放到 224×224224 \times 224224×224，并用 ImageNet [9] 统计量标准化，以匹配预训练编码器的输入分布。归一化后的图像送入冻结的视觉 Transformer 编码器（CLIP、DINOv2 或 BiomedCLIP），其参数在训练全程固定，以保证重建由预训练表征而非领域专用适配来引导。我们分别把所得模型称为 CMR-CLIP、CMR-DINOv2 与 CMR-BiomedCLIP。

English original
Given an intermediate reconstruction, the complex-valued MRI image is converted to a magnitude image and normalized using percentile-based scaling [7] to reduce outliers. The image is then replicated across three channels, resized to 224×224224 \times 224224×224, and standardized using ImageNet [9] statistics to match the input distribution of pretrained encoders. The normalized image is passed through a frozen Vision Transformer encoder (CLIP, DINOv2, or BiomedCLIP), whose parameters remain fixed throughout training to ensure that reconstruction is guided by pretrained representations rather than domain-specific adaptation. We refer to the resulting models as CMR-CLIP, CMR-DINOv2, and CMR-BiomedCLIP, respectively.

我们不只依赖 Transformer 的最后一层，而是从最前面六层提取中间特征，这些特征捕获有利于重建的低层与结构信息 [15]。这些特征通过可学习机制融合：每层先用 LayerNorm [2] 对齐，再用约束为总和等于 1 的 softmax 归一化权重组合 [16]，从而实现对多层表征的自适应整合。融合后的 patch token 被重塑为空间特征图，并由 UNETR 风格 [14] 的层次化解码器处理，该解码器带有来自编码器中间层（第 5、4、3 层）的多尺度跳跃连接。每个跳跃特征在融合前会被重塑、通过双线性插值缩放，并用 1×11 \times 11×1 卷积投影。

English original
Instead of relying solely on the final transformer layer, we extract intermediate features from the first six layers, which capture low-level and structural information beneficial for reconstruction [15]. These features are fused via a learnable mechanism: each layer is first aligned using LayerNorm [2], then combined using softmax-normalized weights constrained to sum to one [16], enabling adaptive integration of multi-level representations. The fused patch tokens are reshaped into spatial feature maps and processed by a UNETR-style [14] hierarchical decoder with multi-scale skip connections from intermediate encoder layers (layers 5, 4, and 3). Each skip feature is reshaped, resized via bilinear interpolation, and projected using 1×11 \times 11×1 convolutions before fusion.

每个解码阶段由双线性上采样和随后的卷积精化组成。与采用完整卷积的标准 UNETR [14] 设计不同，我们使用深度可分离卷积（一个 3×33 \times 33×3 深度卷积后接一个 1×11 \times 11×1 点卷积）以提升参数效率 [13]，并搭配实例归一化 [33] 与 ReLU 激活。每个阶段中，上采样特征与编码器相应的多尺度跳跃特征拼接，如图 1(a) 所示，然后用 3×33 \times 33×3 卷积、实例归一化和非线性进一步精化。该过程在多个阶段重复，逐步恢复空间分辨率并整合高层与低层信息。为保留细粒度细节，我们额外引入一个输入级跳跃连接：把原始复数重建结果通过一个浅卷积层投影，并在最终解码阶段注入。最终输出由一个轻量卷积头产生，得到对应实部与虚部的双通道重建。总体而言，所提出的去噪器结合冻结的基础模型特征、多层 Transformer 融合与轻量层次化解码器，为 MRI 重建提供既可迁移又适配任务的先验。

English original
Each decoding stage consists of bilinear upsampling followed by convolutional refinement. In contrast to standard UNETR [14] designs that employ full convolutions, we use depthwise separable convolutions (a 3×33 \times 33×3 depthwise convolution followed by a 1×11 \times 11×1 pointwise convolution) to improve parameter efficiency [13], along with instance normalization [33] and ReLU activation. At each stage, the upsampled features are concatenated with the corresponding multi-scale skip features from the encoder, as illustrated in Fig. 1(a), and further refined using a 3×33 \times 33×3 convolution, instance normalization, and non-linearity. This process is repeated across multiple stages, progressively recovering spatial resolution and integrating both high-level and low-level information. To preserve fine-grained details, we additionally incorporate an input-level skip connection by projecting the original complex-valued reconstruction through a shallow convolutional layer and injecting it at the final decoding stage. The final output is produced via a lightweight convolutional head, yielding a two-channel reconstruction corresponding to the real and imaginary components. Overall, the proposed denoiser combines frozen foundation model features, multi-layer transformer fusion, and a lightweight hierarchical decoder to provide transferable yet task-adapted priors for MRI reconstruction.

To evaluate cross-domain generalization, we test models trained on CMRxRecon [34] on the fastMRI dataset [40], [32], which consists of fully sampled knee and brain MRI scans acquired on 1.5T and 3T scanners. The knee subset includes coronal proton density-weighted images with and without fat suppression, while the brain subset contains axial T1-weighted, T2-weighted, and FLAIR sequences. Undersampling is simulated using equispaced sampling masks with center fractions of 0.04 and 0.08 [32], corresponding to different amounts of low-frequency information. 4.3 实现细节

English original
4.3. Implementation Details

本框架按标准展开式重建设定实现。所有输入图像缩放到 224×224224 \times 224224×224，以匹配预训练视觉编码器的输入要求。我们与最先进的 E2E-VarNet [32] 比较，并用相同架构与超参数重新实现它以保证公平对比。为保持一致，所有模型都采用与 E2E-VarNet 相同的敏感度图估计网络。所有重建模型都用 12 个级联（展开迭代）训练。基线 E2E-VarNet 约有 29.9M 可训练参数，而基于基础模型的方法由于额外的解码器与融合组件约有 56M 可训练参数，同时基础模型编码器保持冻结。我们为 ×4\times 4×4、×8\times 8×8 与 ×10\times 10×10 加速因子分别训练独立的模型。模型用 Adam 优化器优化，初始学习率为 1×10−31\times10^{-3}1×10−3，在 40 个 epoch 后衰减为 0.1 倍。不使用权重衰减。训练 50 个 epoch，并基于验证性能早停（耐心值 5 个 epoch）。我们最小化重建图像与目标图像之间的 SSIM 损失 [35]、[32]。敏感度估计网络使用 4 个池化层与 8 个基础通道，与先前工作 [32] 一致。所有实验都在 NVIDIA RTX 4090 与 RTX 3090 GPU 上进行。

English original
Our framework is implemented following the standard unrolled reconstruction setting. All input images are resized to 224×224224 \times 224224×224 to match the input requirements of the pretrained vision encoders. We compare our method against the state-of-the-art E2E-VarNet [32], which we reimplement using the same architecture and hyperparameters for a fair comparison. To ensure consistency, we adopt the same sensitivity map estimation network as E2E-VarNet across all models. All reconstruction models are trained with 12 cascades (unrolled iterations). The baseline E2E-VarNet contains approximately 29.9M trainable parameters, while our foundation-model-based approach has approximately 56M trainable parameters due to the additional decoder and fusion components, with the foundation model encoder kept frozen. We train separate models for acceleration factors of ×4\times 4×4, ×8\times 8×8, and ×10\times 10×10. The models are optimized using the Adam optimizer with an initial learning rate of 1×10−31\times10^{-3}1×10−3, which is decayed by a factor of 0.1 after 40 epochs. We use no weight decay. Training is performed for 50 epochs with early stopping based on validation performance (patience of 5 epochs). We minimize the SSIM loss [35], [32] between the reconstructed and target images. For the sensitivity estimation network, we use 4 pooling layers and 8 base channels, consistent with prior work [32]. All experiments were conducted on NVIDIA RTX 4090 and RTX 3090 GPUs.

5.2 跨域泛化结果

5.2. Cross-Domain Generalization Results

我们通过在 CMRxRecon 数据集 [34] 上训练、在 fastMRI 膝与脑数据集 [40] 上测试，评估所提方法在具有挑战性的跨域设定下的泛化能力。这些数据集在解剖结构、图像对比度与采集特性上差异显著，使其成为对鲁棒性的严格检验。除了解剖上的域偏移，我们还引入采样模式的进一步失配。训练时，模型接触的是带固定自动校准信号（ACS）线（24 条中心 k 空间线）的等间距欠采样掩膜。而在测试时，我们使用由中心比例 0.04 与 0.08 定义的等间距掩膜 [32]，其中中心比例指定低频 k 空间被全采样的比例。这导致采样模式的分布不同，并减少了可用的低频信息量，使重建显著更具挑战性。

English original
We evaluate the generalization capability of the proposed approach under challenging cross-domain settings by training on the CMRxRecon dataset [34] and testing on the fastMRI knee and brain datasets [40]. In addition to the domain shift in anatomy, we introduce a further mismatch in the sampling patterns. 分析与讨论

English original
Analysis and Discussion.

在 fastMRI 膝与脑数据集上的跨域结果，揭示了关于基础模型先验在分布偏移下行为的若干重要趋势。

English original
The cross-domain results on fastMRI knee and brain datasets reveal several important trends regarding the behavior of foundation-model-based priors under distribution shift.

在较低加速（×4\times 4×4）下，E2E-VarNet 在膝与脑数据集上都持续取得最佳性能。这是符合预期的，因为全监督重建模型针对训练分布优化，在测量充足时能有效利用数据集特有的先验。然而，当重建变得更具挑战——通过更高加速（×8\times 8×8、×10\times 10×10）与更小的中心比例（0.04）——行为出现了明显转变。E2E-VarNet 的性能下降更快，而基于基础模型的方法表现出更稳定的性能，并持续追平或超过 E2E-VarNet。

English original
At lower acceleration (×4\times 4×4), E2E-VarNet consistently achieves the best performance across both knee and brain datasets. This is expected, as fully supervised reconstruction models are optimized for the training distribution and can effectively exploit dataset-specific priors when sufficient measurements are available. However, as reconstruction becomes more challenging, through higher acceleration (×8\times 8×8, ×10\times 10×10) and reduced center fraction (0.04), a clear shift in behavior emerges. The performance of E2E-VarNet degrades more rapidly, while foundation-model-based approaches exhibit more stable performance and consistently match or outperform E2E-VarNet.

这一趋势在两个解剖域上一致。在膝数据集上，CMR-BiomedCLIP 在 ×8\times 8×8 取得最佳性能，而 CMR-CLIP 与 CMR-BiomedCLIP 在 ×10\times 10×10（尤其是中心比例降低时）超过 E2E-VarNet。类似地，在脑数据集上，基础模型在 ×8\times 8×8 与 ×10\times 10×10 超过 E2E-VarNet，其中 CMR-BiomedCLIP 在最高加速下取得最佳总体性能。这些增益在中心比例 0.04 时最为明显——此时低频信息有限，重建歧义增大，更依赖学习到的先验。

English original
This trend is consistent across both anatomical domains. On the knee dataset, CMR-BiomedCLIP achieves the best performance at ×8\times 8×8, while CMR-CLIP and CMR-BiomedCLIP outperform E2E-VarNet at ×10\times 10×10, particularly under reduced center fraction. Similarly, on the brain dataset, foundation models surpass E2E-VarNet at ×8\times 8×8 and ×10\times 10×10, with CMR-BiomedCLIP achieving the strongest overall performance at the highest acceleration. These gains are most pronounced under center fraction 0.04, where limited low-frequency information increases reconstruction ambiguity and places greater reliance on learned priors.

在基础模型之间比较，CMR-CLIP 在中等加速下表现强劲，表明自然图像预训练捕获了高度可迁移的结构表征。CMR-BiomedCLIP 在更病态的区间提供额外增益，表明当重建难度上升时领域专用预训练变得有益。相比之下，CMR-DINOv2 在各设定下都持续落后，尤其是在严重域偏移下；这可能源于其预训练目标缺少跨模态对齐 [19]，从而可能限制其编码稳健 MRI 重建所需的更高层结构先验的能力。

English original
Comparing foundation models, CMR-CLIP performs strongly at moderate acceleration, indicating that natural-image pretraining captures highly transferable structural representations. CMR-BiomedCLIP provides additional gains in more ill-posed regimes, suggesting that domain-specific pretraining becomes beneficial as reconstruction difficulty increases. In contrast, CMR-DINOv2 consistently underperforms across settings, particularly under severe domain shift, likely due to the absence of cross-modal alignment in its pretraining objective [19], which may limit its ability to encode higher-level structural priors necessary for robust MRI reconstruction.

总体而言，这些结果提示一种依赖区间的行为：任务专用模型在分布内与良态设定中表现出色，而预训练基础模型在严重欠采样与域偏移下提供更鲁棒、更可迁移的先验。这凸显了把大规模预训练表征作为传统任务专用重建流水线的互补替代方案的潜力，尤其是在训练数据不匹配的场景中。

English original
Overall, these results suggest a regime-dependent behavior: task-specific model excel in in-distribution and well-conditioned settings, while pretrained foundation models provide more robust and transferable priors under severe undersampling and domain shift. This highlights the potential of leveraging large-scale pretrained representations as a complementary alternative to conventional task-specific reconstruction pipelines, particularly in scenarios where training data is mismatched.

6 结论

English original
6. Conclusion

我们给出了首个研究冻结视觉基础模型作为基于物理的 MRI 重建先验的工作，提出一个在每个级联中整合预训练视觉编码器的展开式框架。虽然 E2E-VarNet 等任务专用模型在标准分布内设定中取得更优性能，但基于基础模型的方法在跨域偏移与严重欠采样下表现出更强鲁棒性——此时重建问题越来越病态。值得注意的是，随着重建难度增加，这些模型提供更稳定、更可迁移的先验，缩小了与受监督基线的差距，某些情况下甚至反超。此外，我们发现像 CLIP 这样以自然图像预训练的模型已经捕获了高度可迁移的结构表征，而领域专用预训练 BiomedCLIP 在挑战性区间提供适度额外增益。总体而言，我们的发现凸显了大规模预训练视觉表征作为补充先验来源的潜力，可改善 MRI 重建乃至更广泛的基于物理的逆问题中的鲁棒性与泛化。

English original
We present the first study investigating frozen vision foundation models as priors for physics-based MRI reconstruction, introducing an unrolled framework that integrates pretrained visual encoders within each cascade. While task-specific models such as E2E-VarNet achieve superior performance in standard in-distribution settings, foundation-model-based approaches demonstrate greater robustness under cross-domain shifts and severe undersampling, where the reconstruction problem becomes increasingly ill-posed. Notably, as reconstruction difficulty increases, these models provide more stable and transferable priors, narrowing and in some cases surpassing the performance gap with the supervised baseline. Furthermore, we find that natural-image-pretrained models such as CLIP already capture highly transferable structural representations, with domain-specific pretraining BiomedCLIP offering modest additional gains in challenging regimes. Overall, our findings highlight the potential of large-scale pretrained visual representations as a complementary source of priors for improving robustness and generalization in MRI reconstruction and, more broadly, in physics-based inverse problems.
