3D MedDiffusion：面向可控、高质量医学影像生成的 3D 潜在扩散模型

3D MedDiffusion: A 3D Medical Latent Diffusion Model for Controllable and High-quality Medical Image Generation

Haoshen Wang · Zhentao Liu · Kaicong Sun · Xiaodong Wang · Dinggang Shen · Zhiming Cui

医学影像要的是三维、高分辨率、可控。这篇工作把压缩与生成拆开：Patch-Volume 自编码器先用 patch 编码省显存，再用 joint decoder 一次性解码整个体，避免拼接缝；扩散侧用一个「patch 内 + patch 间」双流网络 BiFlowNet 同时管局部细节与全局结构；最后把 ControlNet 挂到冻结的预训练模型上，用很少的训练把它推到稀疏视角 CT、快速 MRI、分割与分类数据增强四类下游任务上。

读之前：三个部件各解决什么问题

这一段是译者注，不是原文。它只做一件事：让你在读公式 (1)–(11) 时知道每个量在流程里的位置。

  1. 压缩（Patch-Volume 自编码器）：3D 体直接进扩散网络太贵，于是先编码到隐空间。第一阶段只在小 patch 上训练编码器与 patch 解码器，显存只需容纳 patch；第二阶段冻结编码器与码本，只微调解码器，让它一次解码整个体（joint decoder），这样拼不出接缝。
  2. 量化（VQ）：编码特征逐位置查码本、取最近邻码字，得到离散的 z̃。所以隐空间是「离散码序列组成的伪体」，扩散就作用在这个隐空间上（latent diffusion）。
  3. 生成（BiFlowNet）：扩散网络要同时照顾两件事：patch 内的细节（由 DiT 背骨的 intra-patch flow 负责）和整个体的全局结构（由 3D U-Net 背骨的 inter-patch flow 负责）。两个流在每一时间步逐元素相加，合成同一个噪声预测。
  4. 条件与下游（ControlNet）：下游任务不是重新训练生成模型，而是克隆一份可训练编码器接任务条件（例如 FBP 重建结果、欠采样 MRI 重建结果、分割掩膜），用零卷积接回冻结主干，只训练这一小份参数。
  5. 指标：FID、MMD 衡量生成分布与真实分布的接近程度（越低越好）；MS-SSIM 在这里被当作多样性指标（越低越多样）；PSNR、SSIM、95HD、Dice 用于重建与分割任务。

摘要

English original
Abstract

医学图像的生成面临显著挑战，原因在于它们高分辨率且是三维的。现有方法在生成高质量的 3D 医学图像时往往表现欠佳，而且目前还没有一个通用的医学影像生成框架。本文提出一种 3D 医学潜在扩散（3D MedDiffusion）模型，用于可控、高质量的 3D 医学图像生成。3D MedDiffusion 包含一个新的、非常高效的 Patch-Volume 自编码器：它通过逐 patch（patch-wise）编码把医学图像压缩进隐空间，并通过逐体（volume-wise）解码把图像恢复回图像空间。此外，我们设计了一个新的噪声估计器，用于在扩散去噪过程中同时捕捉局部细节与全局结构信息。3D MedDiffusion 能够生成细节精细的高分辨率图像（最高 512×512×512），并且由于它在大规模数据集上训练——覆盖 CT 与 MRI 两种模态以及从头部到腿部的不同解剖区域——它可以有效地适配多种下游任务。实验结果表明，3D MedDiffusion 在生成质量上超过了当前最优方法，并在稀疏视角 CT 重建、快速 MRI 重建、分割数据增强与分类数据增强等任务上展现出很强的泛化能力。源代码与模型 checkpoint 见 https://github.com/ShanghaiTech-IMPACT/3D-MedDiffusion。

English original
The generation of medical images presents significant challenges due to their high-resolution and three-dimensional nature. Existing methods often yield suboptimal performance in generating high-quality 3D medical images, and there is currently no universal generative framework for medical imaging. In this paper, we introduce a 3D Medical Latent Diffusion (3D MedDiffusion) model for controllable, high-quality 3D medical image generation. 3D MedDiffusion incorporates a novel, highly efficient Patch-Volume Autoencoder that compresses medical images into latent space through patch-wise encoding and recovers back into image space through volume-wise decoding. Additionally, we design a new noise estimator to capture both local details and global structural information during diffusion denoising process. 3D MedDiffusion can generate fine-detailed, high-resolution images (up to 512x512x512) and effectively adapt to various downstream tasks as it is trained on large-scale datasets covering CT and MRI modalities and different anatomical regions (from head to leg). Experimental results demonstrate that 3D MedDiffusion surpasses state-of-the-art methods in generative quality and exhibits strong generalizability across tasks such as sparse-view CT reconstruction, fast MRI reconstruction, and data augmentation for segmentation and classification. Source code and checkpoints are available at https://github.com/ShanghaiTech-IMPACT/3D-MedDiffusion.

English original
1 Introduction

生成式人工智能（AI）近年带来了引人注目的突破，推动了多个领域与应用的实质性进展。具体而言，它对医学影像与医疗健康的进步贡献显著，未来创新的潜力巨大。生成式 AI 已在多种医学应用中展现出强大能力，包括图像翻译 [1]、分割 [2]，以及计算机断层成像（CT）[3] 与磁共振（MR）图像 [4] 的重建。

English original
Generative artificial intelligence (AI) has led to remarkable breakthroughs in recent years, driving substantial advancements across various domains and applications. Specifically, it has significantly contributed to progress in medical imaging and healthcare, with immense potential for future innovations. Generative AI have shown strong capabilities in various medical applications including image translation [1], segmentation [2], and the reconstruction of Computed Tomography (CT) [3] and Magnetic Resonance (MR) images [4].

推动这种快速进展的生成模型有多种，包括变分自编码器（VAE）[5]、归一化流 [6]、生成对抗网络（GAN）[7] 与去噪扩散概率模型（DDPM）[8]。VAE 与归一化流属于基于似然的模型，它们显式地对数据分布建模，理论上基础扎实，但生成的图像质量往往较为一般。另一方面，GAN 通过对抗训练来学习数据的潜在概率分布，这种方式可能导致训练不稳定，表现为模式崩溃（mode collapse）[9] 等问题。DDPM 通过把扩散过程建模为马尔可夫链 [10]、[8] 来解决这些局限：噪声被逐步加入数据，模型被训练来逆转这一过程以生成样本。这种方法保证了训练稳定，能产生高质量且多样的输出，同时提供了可解释的生成过程。大规模模型如 stable diffusion [11] 在自然图像领域被广泛使用，并很容易被适配到图像修复 [12] 与超分辨率 [13] 等任务上。

English original
Different kinds of generative models have shaped such rapid advancement including Variational Autoencoders (VAE) [5], normalizing flows [6], Generative Adversarial Networks (GAN) [7], and Denoising Diffusion Probabilistic Model (DDPM) [8]. VAEs and normalizing flows are likelihood-based models, which explicitly model the data distribution and are theoretically well-founded. However, they often produce relatively modest generative results. On the other hand, GANs are generative models that employ an adversarial training approach to learn the underlying probability distribution of the data. This method can result in training instability, characterized by issues such as mode collapse [9]. DDPM addresses these limitations by utilizing a diffusion process, modeled as a Markov chain [10], [8]. Noise is incrementally added to the data, and a model is trained to reverse this process to generate samples. This method ensures stable training, producing high-quality, diverse outputs while offering a interpretable generative process. Large-scale models like stable diffusion [11] are widely applied in natural image domains and easily adapted for tasks such as image inpainting [12], and super-resolution [13].

尽管近期有所进展，仍有若干挑战妨碍生成模型在医学影像中被更广泛地应用。第一，把计算机视觉算法迁移到医学影像上很困难，因为医学影像本身是三维的，会带来可观的计算开销。医学领域已有的生成模型在分辨率与质量上往往都受限 [14]、[15]。一些工作依赖逐切片的 2D 生成 [16]、[17]、[18]，而临床实践需要的是三维体成像。第二，缺少高效且可控的生成机制，迫使研究者为每个任务单独开发模型，从而抬高了开发成本。

English original
Despite recent advancements, several challenges continue to impede the broader application of generative models in medical imaging. First, adapting computer vision algorithms to medical imaging is challenging due to its three-dimensional nature, which introduces significant computational overhead. Existing generative models in the medical domain are often limited in both resolution and quality [14], [15]. Some works rely on slice-wise 2D generation [16], [17], [18], while clinical practice needs 3D volumetric imaging. Second, the lack of efficient and controllable generation mechanisms forces the development of task-specific models, thereby increasing development costs.

我们提出 3D 医学潜在扩散（3D MedDiffusion），一个面向医学图像高保真生成的大规模 3D 生成模型。首先，3D MedDiffusion 引入一个新的 Patch-Volume 自编码器：它通过逐 patch 编码器把高分辨率图像压缩为低分辨率隐表示，并通过逐体解码器恢复整张图像。其次，我们设计了一个新的噪声估计器（命名为 BiFlowNet）来取代扩散模型中的标准 U-Net [19]。该模块把 patch 内流（intra-patch flow）与 patch 间流（inter-patch flow）结合起来，同时处理局部（逐 patch）与全局（逐体）信息。这种双流结构对于「从噪声中重建局部细节、同时保留全局结构信息」至关重要。此外，3D MedDiffusion 在一个覆盖 CT 与 MRI 两种模态、包含从头部到腿部多个解剖区域的三维医学图像数据集集合上训练。值得注意的是，ControlNet [20]——一种被广泛用于向扩散模型注入条件的方法——可以无缝地接入预训练的 3D MedDiffusion，从而为各种下游任务实现高效微调。

English original
We propose 3D Medical Latent Diffusion (3D MedDiffusion), a large-scale 3D generative model designed for high-fidelity generation of medical images. First, 3D MedDiffusion introduces a novel Patch-Volume Autoencoder that compresses high-resolution images into low-resolution latent representations via a patch-wise encoder and recovers the entire image with a volume-wise decoder. Second, we design a novel noise estimator (named BiFlowNet) to replace the standard U-Net [19] in diffusion model. This module combines intra-patch and inter-patch flows to process both local (patch-wise) and global (volume-wise) information. The dual flow structure is crucial for reconstructing local details from noise while preserving global structure information. Furthermore, 3D MedDiffusion is trained on a comprehensive and diverse collection of 3D medical image datasets, covering both CT and MRI modalities and multiple anatomical regions from head to leg. Notably, ControlNet [20], a widely used method for injecting conditions into diffusion models, can be seamlessly integrated with the pre-trained 3D MedDiffusion, facilitating efficient fine-tuning for various downstream tasks.

逐 patch 编码利用 3D 医学图像的局部性质降低了训练阶段的计算开销，而逐体解码则保证重建结果没有伪影。

English original
Patch-wise encoding reduces the computational overhead in the training stage by exploiting the local properties in 3D medical images, while volume-wise decoding ensures artifact-free reconstruction results.

2.1 医学图像生成

English original
2.1 Medical Image Generation

医学图像生成有望解决若干临床任务，例如模态翻译、CT 增强与 MR 加速。已有研究探索过用基于 GAN 的方法生成 3D 医学图像。Jin 等人 [21] 提出一种自编码 GAN 来生成 3D 脑部 MRI 图像。Cirillo 等人 [22] 提出一个以多通道 3D 脑部 MR 图像为条件的 3D 模型，用来生成用于分割的肿瘤掩膜。Sun 等人 [23] 设计了一个分层结构的 GAN，以应对生成高分辨率 3D 图像的内存需求。

English original
Medical image generation is promising to address several clinical tasks, such as modality translation, CT enhancement, and MR acceleration. Existing research has explored the application of GAN-based methods for generating 3D medical images. Jin et al. [21] introduced an autoencoding GAN to generate 3D brain MRI images. Cirillo et al. [22] proposed a 3D model conditioned on multi-channel 3D brain MR images to generate tumor masks for segmentation. Sun et al. [23] developed a GAN with a hierarchical design to address memory demands of generating high-resolution 3D images.

然而，GAN 框架固有的训练不稳定过程常常导致模式崩溃与低质量生成。近来，由于高保真生成与稳定训练，许多研究者把注意力转向扩散模型（DM）[8]。Müller-Franzes 等人 [24] 提出一种条件潜在 DM，展示了其在生成 2D 医学图像上的前景。Moghadam 等人 [25] 表明 DM 能够生成种类广泛的病理组织学图像。然而，三维成像在医学场景中更为普遍。由于 DM 处理三维数据的计算复杂度很高，这一任务成为挑战。因此，许多研究者探索用 DM 高效生成 3D 医学图像的方法。Zhu 等人 [16] 用伪 3D 架构构建 3D 图像生成器，并通过精修器或调优来处理体一致性（volumetric inconsistency）问题。Friedrich 等人 [14] 把小波分解后的图像送入扩散模型以提升效率。Khader 等人 [15] 采用一个结合自编码器与视频 DM 的潜在 DM 来生成高质量的 3D CT 与 MR 图像。Guo 等人 [26] 提出 MAISI，一个同期出现的、基于潜在 DM 的框架，通过张量切分并行（tensor-splitting parallelism）提升自编码器效率。该方法把特征图切成更小的段，从而可以在多块 GPU 上并行处理，或在单块 GPU 上串行处理。它类似于在特征空间上做滑窗推理，所以产生的伪影比直接在图像空间上切分要少。与之相对，我们的 Patch-Volume 自编码器采用逐 patch 与逐体的两阶段训练策略，保证单 GPU 推理时没有边界伪影。此外，我们也可以结合张量并行 [27] 做多 GPU 推理以加速这一过程。

English original
Nevertheless, the unstable training process inherent to GAN framework often results in mode collapse and low quality generation. Recently, many researchers have shifted their focus to diffusion models (DMs) due to their high fidelity generation and stable training [8]. Müller-Franzes et al. [24] proposed a conditional latent DM, demonstrating its promise for generating 2D medical images. Moghadam et al. [25] showed that DMs were capable of generating a wide range of histopathological images. However, 3D imaging is more prevalent in medical contexts. This task poses challenges due to high computational complexity of DMs for three-dimensional data. Consequently, many researchers explored efficient methods for generating 3D medical images using DMs. Zhu et al. [16] developed 3D image generators using pseudo-3D architectures, addressing volumetric inconsistencies through refiners or tuning. Friedrich et al. [14] applied a diffusion model to wavelet-decomposed images for efficiency. Khader et al. [15] employed a latent DM combining an autoencoder and video DM to generate high-quality 3D CT and MR images. Guo et al. [26] introduced MAISI, a concurrent latent DM based framework that improved autoencoder efficiency via tensor-splitting parallelism. This method divides feature maps into smaller segments, enabling parallel processing across multiple GPUs or sequential processing on a single GPU. It resembles sliding window inference on feature space, so it produces less artifacts than directly applying it on image space. In contrast, our Patch-Volume Autoencoder follows a two-stage patch-wise and volume-wise training strategy, ensuring no boundary artifacts during single-GPU inference. Besides, we can also integrate tensor parallelism [27] for multi-GPU inference to accelerate the process.

2.2 Medical Generative Model for Downstream Tasks

医学生成模型在多种下游任务上大有可为 [28]、[29]、[30]。近期研究探索了用 DM 做数据增强，例如训练条件 DM 从分割掩膜生成合成数据 [26]。另一些工作把 DM 用于求解逆问题，包括 CT 重建 [31] 与图像到图像的翻译 [32]。然而，这些方法大多需要为特定任务做专门的训练。与之相对，我们的方法借助 ControlNet [20] 免去了任务专用训练，能够为多种下游任务高效微调。

Other works have applied DMs to solve inverse problems, including CT reconstruction [31] and image-to-image translation [32]. However, most of these methods require dedicated training for specific tasks. In contrast, our method leverages ControlNet [20] to eliminate task-specific training, enabling efficient fine-tuning for diverse downstream tasks.

English original
3.1.2 Volume-wise Training

我们把 patch 解码器 DP 微调为联合解码器 DJ，从而不必逐个处理 patch 再拼接。它使高分辨率重建成为可能，且不产生边界伪影。

我们提出一个新的噪声估计器，称为 BiFlowNet，它把 patch 内流与 patch 间流整合起来生成隐表示。patch 内流捕捉细粒度细节，patch 间流维持全局结构一致性。双流的隐特征被整合起来，以同时达成这两个目标。

5.3 分割数据增强

5.3 Data Augmentation for Segmentation

基于深度学习的医学图像分割常常受限于训练数据不足。在本实验中，我们借助 3D MedDiffusion 的强生成能力来扩充数据、提升分割性能。我们在 KiTs19 数据集 [58] 上用标准 nnU-Net [62] 做分割，任务包含肾脏与肿瘤区域。测试集由 40 例 CT 图像及其分割掩膜组成，其余 170 例用于训练。我们定义以下五种训练设置：（1）100% 合成：以训练集的分割掩膜为条件，用 3D MedDiffusion 生成新的 CT 图像。（2）50% 真实 + 50% 合成：从训练集中随机选取 50% 的真实数据，然后以其分割掩膜为条件，用 3D MedDiffusion 对剩下 50% 生成新的 CT 图像。（3）100% 真实：使用全部 170 例。（4）100% 真实 + 50% 合成：使用全部 170 例，并对其中 50% 的病例生成新的 CT 图像。（5）100% 真实 + 100% 合成：使用全部 170 例，并对其中 100% 的病例生成新的 CT 图像。

(3) 100% Real: Use all 170 cases. (4) 100% Real + 50% Synthesis: Use all 170 cases and generate new CT images for 50% of the selected cases. (5) 100% Real + 100% Synthesis: Use all 170 cases and generate new CT images for 100% of the selected cases.

6 结论

English original
6 Conclusion

本文提出 3D 医学潜在扩散（3D MedDiffusion）模型，用于生成高质量的 3D 医学图像。3D MedDiffusion 通过两个关键设计部件取得其性能：1）Patch-Volume 自编码器，2）BiFlowNet 噪声估计器。此外，它可以与 ControlNet 结合，从而高效地适配医学场景中大量下游任务。我们的实验也展示了其优越的生成性能以及在多种任务上的强泛化能力。

English original
In this paper, we propose a 3D Medical Latent Diffusion (3D MedDiffusion) model to generate high-quality 3D medical images. 3D MedDiffusion achieves its performance through two key design components: 1) the Patch-Volume Autoencoder and 2) the BiFlowNet noise estimator. Additionally, it can be integrated with ControlNet, enabling efficient adaptation to a large range of downstream tasks in medical scenarios. Our experiments also demonstrate superior generative performance and strong generalizability across diverse tasks.

尽管取得了令人鼓舞的结果，我们的方法仍有局限。第一，它缺乏生成任意分辨率图像的能力。任意尺寸生成或许可以借助隐式神经编码器 [64] 实现，这将是我们未来的工作。第二，我们的方法没有把年龄与性别作为合成医学图像的条件因素。未来工作可以探索引入更多条件以提升临床适用性。第三，扩散模型生成高分辨率 3D 医学图像需要大量时间与资源。我们希望在将来进一步提升其时间与内存效率。

English original
Although achieving promising results, our method still has limitations. First, it lacks the ability to produce images at arbitrary resolutions. Arbitrary-size generation could potentially be achieved with implicit neural encoder [64], which will be our future work. Second, our method does not incorporate age and gender as conditioning factors for synthesizing medical images. Future work could explore the inclusion of additional conditions to enhance clinical applicability. Third, diffusion models require significant time and resources for generating high-resolution 3D medical images. We aim to further improve its time and memory efficiency in the future.
