English original
Abstract

深度 MRI 重建通常由条件模型完成：它们对欠采样采集做解混叠，以恢复与全采样数据一致的图像。由于条件模型在训练时就掌握了成像算子，它们在面对不同算子时可能泛化不佳。无条件模型转而学习与算子解耦的生成式图像先验，从而提升在与成像算子相关的域偏移下的可靠性。近期的扩散模型因其高样本保真度而尤具前景。然而，使用静态图像先验进行推理可能表现欠佳。本文提出首个用于 MRI 重建的自适应扩散先验 AdaDiff，以提升在域偏移下的性能与可靠性。AdaDiff 利用一个高效的扩散先验，该先验通过在大幅逆向扩散步上进行对抗映射来训练。训练之后执行两阶段重建：快速扩散阶段用训练好的先验产生初始重建，适配阶段则通过更新先验以最小化数据一致性损失来进一步精修结果。在多对比度脑部 MRI 上的实验清楚表明，AdaDiff 在域偏移下优于竞争的条件与无条件方法，并在域内取得更优或相当的性能。

English original
Deep MRI reconstruction is commonly performed with conditional models that de-alias undersampled acquisitions to recover images consistent with fully-sampled data. Since conditional models are trained with knowledge of the imaging operator, they can show poor generalization across variable operators. Unconditional models instead learn generative image priors decoupled from the operator to improve reliability against domain shifts related to the imaging operator. Recent diffusion models are particularly promising given their high sample fidelity. Nevertheless, inference with a static image prior can perform suboptimally. Here we propose the first adaptive diffusion prior for MRI reconstruction, AdaDiff, to improve performance and reliability against domain shifts. AdaDiff leverages an efficient diffusion prior trained via adversarial mapping over large reverse diffusion steps. A two-phase reconstruction is executed following training: a rapid-diffusion phase that produces an initial reconstruction with the trained prior, and an adaptation phase that further refines the result by updating the prior to minimize data-consistency loss. Demonstrations on multi-contrast brain MRI clearly indicate that AdaDiff outperforms competing conditional and unconditional methods under domain shifts, and achieves superior or on par within-domain performance.

1 Introduction

磁共振成像（MRI）因其出色的软组织对比度而成为诊断应用中的首选模态，但检查时间历来较长，限制了其临床使用。一个根本性的解决办法是通过欠采样 k 空间采集来缩短扫描时间，并把重建当作一个不适定逆问题来求解 (Lustig 等, 2007)、(Gu 等, 2021)。近年来，深度学习方法已成为 MRI 重建的黄金标准，因为它们能够基于数据驱动的先验求解复杂的逆问题 (Liang 等, 2020)、(Yang 等, 2016)、(Wang 等, 2016)、(Hammernik 等, 2017)、(Schlemper 等, 2017)、(Dar 等, 2020b)、(Kwon 等, 2017)、(Yaman 等, 2021)。许多已提出的方法基于条件模型：它们把欠采样采集作为输入处理，以恢复与全采样采集一致的输出图像 (Zhu 等, 2018)、(Aggarwal 等, 2019)、(Hyun 等, 2018)、(Lee 等, 2018)、(Knoll 等, 2019)、(Yoon 等, 2018)、(Ye 等, 2018)、(Quan 等, 2018)、(Yu 等, 2018)、(Adler 与 Oktem, 2018)、(Guo 等, 2021)。这种条件映射既可以显式地从成对的欠采样/全采样采集训练集中学习 (Dar 等, 2020a)、(Mardani 等, 2019)、(Biswas 等, 2019)、(Wang 等, 2019)、(Yang 等, 2020)、(Eo 等, 2018)、(Cheng 等, 2019)、(Qin 等, 2019)、(Hosseini 等, 2020)；为降低对训练数据的要求，它也可以通过自监督 (Tamir 等, 2019)、(Wang 等, 2020a)、(Cole 等, 2020)、(Yaman 等, 2020)、(Huang 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2020)、(Aggarwal 等, 2021) 或循环一致性方法 (Quan 等, 2018)、(Oh 等, 2020)、(Lei 等, 2021)、(Chung 等, 2021)、(Lu 等, 2021) 在欠采样采集上隐式地学习。无论采用哪种学习策略，条件模型捕获的都是抑制欠采样伪影的解混叠先验，因此它们显式地掌握了成像算子——该算子反映了加速所选的采样模式与线圈敏感度 (Polak 等, 2020)、(Feng 等, 2021)、(Küstner 等, 2020)、(Sriram 等, 2020)。深度重建模型通常基于一个相对标准化的成像算子来训练，以帮助最大化性能 (Knoll 等, 2020)。然而在测试阶段，终端用户可能不得不对成像算子做临时改动（例如改变加速率、采样密度或线圈数），以满足图像质量或扫描时间上的实际考量。由于成像算子本质上决定了欠采样采集中的混叠伪影特征，这类算子上的域偏移会损害重建性能，并迫使条件模型重新训练 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2021)。为避免泛化性能的潜在损失，亟需对训练集与测试集之间成像算子的变化具有韧性的深度重建模型。

English original
Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) is a preferred modality in diagnostic applications due to its exceptional soft-tissue contrast, yet canonically long exams hinder its clinical use. A fundamental solution is to shorten scan times by undersampling k-space acquisitions and solve an ill-posed inverse problem to reconstruct images (Lustig et al., 2007); (Gu et al., 2021). In recent years, deep learning methods have become a gold standard in MRI reconstruction, given their ability to solve complex inverse problems based on data-driven priors (Liang et al., 2020); (Yang et al., 2016); (Wang et al., 2016); (Hammernik et al., 2017); (Schlemper et al., 2017); (Dar et al., 2020b); (Kwon et al., 2017); (Yaman et al., 2021). Many proposed methods are based on conditional models that process undersampled acquisitions provided as input to recover output images that are consistent with fully-sampled acquisitions (Zhu et al., 2018); (Aggarwal et al., 2019); (Hyun et al., 2018); (Lee et al., 2018); (Knoll et al., 2019); (Yoon et al., 2018); (Ye et al., 2018); (Quan et al., 2018); (Yu et al., 2018); (Adler 与 Oktem, 2018); (Guo et al., 2021). This conditional mapping can be learned explicitly from a training dataset of paired undersampled and fully-sampled acquisitions (Dar et al., 2020a); (Mardani et al., 2019); (Biswas et al., 2019); (Wang et al., 2019); (Yang et al., 2020); (Eo et al., 2018); (Cheng et al., 2019); (Qin et al., 2019); (Hosseini et al., 2020). To alleviate requirements on training data, the mapping can also be learned implicitly on undersampled acquisitions via self-supervision (Tamir et al., 2019); (Wang et al., 2020a); (Cole et al., 2020); (Yaman et al., 2020); (Huang et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2020); (Aggarwal et al., 2021) or cycle-consistency approaches (Quan et al., 2018); (Oh et al., 2020); (Lei et al., 2021); (Chung et al., 2021); (Lu et al., 2021). Regardless of the learning strategy, conditional models capture a de-aliasing prior to suppress undersampling artifacts, so they have explicit knowledge of the imaging operator that reflects the choice of sampling patterns and coil sensitivities for acceleration (Polak et al., 2020); (Feng et al., 2021); (Küstner et al., 2020); (Sriram et al., 2020). Deep reconstruction models are commonly trained based on a relatively standardized imaging operator to help maximize performance (Knoll et al., 2020). However, in the testing stage, an end user might have to prescribe spontaneous changes to the imaging operator (e.g. changes in acceleration rate, sampling density or number of coils) in order to meet practical considerations on image quality or scan time. Since the imaging operator inherently determines the characteristics of aliasing artifacts in undersampled acquisitions, such domain shifts in the operator can compromise reconstruction performance and necessitate re-training of conditional models (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2021). To avoid potential losses in generalization performance, deep reconstruction models that are resilient against variations in the imaging operator between the training and test sets are direly needed.

另一类框架采用无条件模型：它们不被训练去执行重建任务（即把欠采样映射到全采样），而是通过辅助任务捕获生成式图像先验，例如加性噪声去除 (Ahmad 等, 2020)、图像自编码 (Tezcan 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2020)、(Tezcan 等, 2022) 或图像生成 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021)、(Luo 等, 2020)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)、(Elmas 等, 2022)。图像先验只在推理时与成像算子结合，因此由于对欠采样不可知，它们提升了对可变算子的泛化能力 (Tezcan 等, 2019)、(Narnhofer 等, 2019)。对抗先验尤其突出，因为它们对细致的组织结构表现出更高的敏感性 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)，但生成图像样本的多样性可能较差 (Dhariwal 与 Nichol, 2021)。作为一个有前景的替身，扩散模型在保持相当样本质量的同时提升了样本多样性 (Ho 等, 2020)。近期研究报道，通过在扩散先验与成像算子之间交替投影、以强制与采集数据一致，可获得出色的重建 (Jalal 等, 2021)、(Chung 与 Ye, 2022)、(Song 等, 2022)、(Chung 等, 2022)、(Luo 等, 2022)、(Xie 与 Li, 2022)、(Peng 等, 2022)。尽管如此，静态扩散先验会在 MR 图像分布的域偏移下限制模型性能，而这种偏移可能来自脉冲序列或扫描仪设置的变化。

English original
An alternative framework employs unconditional models that are not trained to perform the reconstruction task (i.e., mapping undersampled to fully-sampled data), but instead to capture generative image priors through auxiliary tasks such as additive noise removal (Ahmad et al., 2020), image autoencoding (Tezcan et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2020); (Tezcan et al., 2022) or image generation (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021); (Luo et al., 2020); (Korkmaz et al., 2022); (Elmas et al., 2022). Image priors are only combined with the imaging operator during inference, so they improve generalization against variable operators as they are agnostic to undersampling (Tezcan et al., 2019); (Narnhofer et al., 2019). Adversarial priors are particularly prominent as they offer elevated sensitivity to detailed tissue structure (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Korkmaz et al., 2022), but they might manifest poor diversity in generated image samples (Dhariwal 与 Nichol, 2021). As a promising surrogate, diffusion models enhance sample diversity while maintaining comparable sample quality (Ho et al., 2020). Recent studies have reported remarkable reconstructions with alternated projections through diffusion priors and through the imaging operator to enforce consistency to acquired data (Jalal et al., 2021); (Chung 与 Ye, 2022); (Song et al., 2022); (Chung et al., 2022); (Luo et al., 2022); (Xie 与 Li, 2022); (Peng et al., 2022). Still, static diffusion priors can limit model performance under domain shifts in the MR image distribution, which can result from changes in the pulse sequence or scanner settings.

本文提出一种新的基于扩散的方法 AdaDiff，以提升加速 MRI 重建在域偏移下的性能与可靠性。AdaDiff 学习一个用于高保真图像生成的无条件扩散先验（图 1），并在推理期间适配该扩散先验以增强性能（图 2）。原始扩散模型通过一长串推理步生成图像，导致图像采样耗时很长 (Ho 等, 2020)。我们改为提出一种基于对抗映射器的扩散模型，以少数几个大幅逆向扩散步生成图像，从而显著加速图像采样。推理时随后采用两阶段重建：快速扩散阶段基于训练好的先验、以快速图像采样产生初始重建；适配阶段则通过更新先验以最小化数据一致性损失来产生精修后的重建。对抗式扩散先验使 AdaDiff 能够在比静态、未训练或非对抗扩散先验更少的推理迭代中重建高质量图像。

English original
Here we introduce a novel diffusion-based method, AdaDiff, to improve performance and reliability against domain shifts in accelerated MRI reconstruction. AdaDiff learns an unconditional diffusion prior for high-fidelity image generation (Fig. 1), and adapts the diffusion prior during inference for enhanced performance (Fig. 2). Vanilla diffusion models generate images through a long sequence of inference steps, resulting in prolonged image sampling (Ho et al., 2020). We instead propose a diffusion model based on an adversarial mapper to generate images in few, large reverse diffusion steps for a notable speed up in image sampling. A two-phase reconstruction is then employed during inference: a rapid-diffusion phase that produces an initial reconstruction by fast image sampling based on the trained prior, and an adaptation phase that produces a refined reconstruction by updating the prior to minimize data-consistency loss. The adversarial diffusion prior enables AdaDiff to reconstruct high-quality images in fewer inference iterations compared to static, untrained or non-adversarial diffusion priors.

本文在混合对比度上训练的单一模型基础上，展示多对比度脑部 MRI 的重建。实验报告了域内情形——训练集与测试集之间成像算子与图像分布相匹配——以及跨域情形——算子或 MR 图像分布存在域偏移。对比对象包括最先进的传统方法、条件模型与无条件深度模型。总体上，AdaDiff 在域内情形取得更优或相当的性能，并在跨域情形优于竞争方法。实现 AdaDiff 的代码见 https://github.com/icon-lab/AdaDiff。

English original
The proposed method is demonstrated for reconstruction of multiple contrasts in brain MRI based on a unified model trained on mixed contrasts. Experiments are reported for within-domain cases with matched imaging operator and image distribution between training-test sets, and cross-domain cases with a domain shift in the operator or the MR image distribution. Comparisons are provided against state-of-the-art traditional, conditional and unconditional deep models. In general, AdaDiff achieves superior or on par performance in within-domain cases, and outperforms competing methods in cross-domain cases. Code to implement AdaDiff is available at https://github.com/icon-lab/AdaDiff.

2 Related Work

把图像先验与成像算子解耦的无条件模型，有望提升 MRI 重建的泛化能力 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Tezcan 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2020)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)、(Ahmad 等, 2020)、(Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021)、(Luo 等, 2020)。在这种生成建模（GM）框架中，图像先验通常被学习来捕获关于高质量 MRI 数据分布的信息 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Tezcan 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2020)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)、(Elmas 等, 2022)、(Luo 等, 2020)。一种常见做法依托生成对抗网络（GAN），由它间接刻画数据分布 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Liu 等, 2020)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)、(Elmas 等, 2022)。尽管生成的图像样本很真实，基于 GAN 的方法仍可能受表征多样性偏低的困扰，从而妨碍重建性能。

English original
Unconditional models that decouple the image prior from the imaging operator promise enhanced generalization in MRI reconstruction (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Tezcan et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2020); (Korkmaz et al., 2022); (Ahmad et al., 2020); (Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021); (Luo et al., 2020). In this generative modeling (GM) framework, image priors are typically learned to capture information regarding the distribution of high-quality MRI data (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Tezcan et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2020); (Korkmaz et al., 2022); (Elmas et al., 2022); (Luo et al., 2020). A common approach rests on generative adversarial networks (GANs) that indirectly characterize the data distribution (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Liu et al., 2020); (Korkmaz et al., 2022); (Elmas et al., 2022). Despite the realism of generated image samples, GAN-based methods can be susceptible to low representational diversity that can hamper reconstruction performance.

近期一类基于扩散的生成模型有望比 GAN 获得更强的表征多样性。扩散模型使用多步过程，逐步把高斯噪声变换为图像样本。与 GAN 不同，它们直接刻画数据分布的相关量（例如对数似然的导数或下界）。学到的先验在推理时与成像算子耦合 (Jalal 等, 2021)、(Chung 与 Ye, 2022)、(Song 等, 2022)、(Chung 等, 2022)、(Luo 等, 2022)、(Peng 等, 2022)。随后可通过在扩散先验与算子之间反复投影来完成重建。通过扩散先验的投影涉及图像样本的生成。例如，(Jalal 等, 2021)、(Luo 等, 2022) 提出用基于分数的函数与朗之万动力学采样；(Chung 与 Ye, 2022)、(Song 等, 2022) 使用预测器求解随机微分方程，随后进行朗之万采样。基于扩散的 MRI 重建通常报告了很高的图像质量 (Peng 等, 2022)。

English original
A recent class of GMs based on diffusion promise enhanced representational diversity over GANs. Diffusion models use a multi-step process to gradually transform Gaussian noise into image samples. Unlike GANs, they directly characterize correlates of the data distribution (e.g., derivative or lower bound of log-likelihood). Unlike methods based on static diffusion priors, AdaDiff performs subject-specific adaptation of its prior during inference to increase conformity of its prior to the distribution of the test data. AdaDiff 通过由生成器与判别器组成的对抗映射器实现逆向扩散步（图 3）。生成器采用残差编码器-解码器结构，以帮助把图像样本在相邻时步之间投影，输入为时间索引 ttt（即扩散过程中的当前时步值）与一组随机正态变量 zzz (Xiao 等, 2022)。共使用 6 个编码器阶段，每个阶段含 2 个平坦残差块，随后接一个（2 倍）下采样残差块。最后两个阶段使用注意力层，且最后阶段省略下采样。共使用 6 个解码器阶段，每个阶段含 3 个平坦残差块，随后接一个（2 倍）上采样残差块。第二个阶段使用一个注意力层，且最后阶段省略上采样。生成器每个块中的特征图根据隐嵌入向量做自适应组归一化 (Zheng 等, 2020)。该向量由包含 8 个全连接（FC）层的多层感知机（MLP）计算，用于嵌入随机正态变量 zzz (Karras 等, 2019)。判别器采用残差编码器结构 (He 等, 2016)，以区分真实与合成图像样本。共使用 6 个编码器阶段，每个阶段含一个（2 倍）下采样残差块。最后用一个含 1 个 FC 层的 MLP 做判别。所有生成器与判别器块都接收一个时间嵌入向量，该向量由时间索引的正弦编码经 2 层 MLP 投影得到 (Song 等, 2020)，并作为通道特定的偏置项加到特征图上。上采样通过插入零值中间像素并与可学习的有限冲激响应（FIR）滤波器卷积实现；下采样通过与该可学习 FIR 卷积并丢弃中间像素实现，如 (Karras 等, 2020) 所述。生成器块与 MLP 层使用 SiLU 激活函数，判别器块使用负斜率为 0.2 的 leaky ReLU。生成器与判别器均用 2 个通道表示图像的实部与虚部。

AdaDiff implemented reverse diffusion steps via an adversarial mapper comprising a generator and a discriminator (Fig. 3). The generator followed a residual encoder-decoder structure to help project the image sample between consecutive time steps, given time index ttt (i.e., the current time step value in the diffusion process) and a set of random normal variables zzz (Xiao et al., 2022). A total of 6 encoder stages were used, each stage containing 2 flat residual blocks followed by a downsampling residual block (by a factor of 2). Attention layers were used in the last two stages, and downsampling was omitted in the final stage. A total of 6 decoder stages were used, each stage containing 3 flat residual blocks followed by an upsampling residual block (by a factor of 2). An attention layer was used in the second stage, and upsampling was omitted in the final stage. Feature maps in each generator block were modulated via adaptive group normalization given a latent embedding vector (Zheng et al., 2020). This vector was computed via a multi-layer perceptron (MLP) containing 8 fully-connected (FC) layers to embed random normal variables zzz (Karras et al., 2019). The discriminator followed a residual encoder structure (He et al., 2016) to distinguish actual versus synthetic image samples. A total of 6 encoder stages were used, each containing a downsampling residual block (by a factor of 2). A final MLP with 1 FC layer was used for discrimination. All generator and discriminator blocks received a time embedding vector computed by projecting a sinusoidal encoding of the time index through a 2-layer MLP (Song et al., 2020), and this vector was added as a channel-specific bias term onto feature maps. Upsampling was performed by inserting intermediate zero-valued pixels and convolving with a learnable finite-impulse-response (FIR) filter, and downsampling was performed by convolving with a learnable FIR and discarding intermediate pixels as described in (Karras et al., 2020). SiLU activation functions were used in generator blocks and MLP layers, and leaky ReLU functions with negative slope 0.2 were used in discriminator blocks. Both the generator and discriminator employed 2 channels to represent the real and imaginary parts of images.

6 讨论

English original
6 Discussion

所提出的 AdaDiff 方法已在 MRI 重建上与传统、条件与无条件基线进行对比。考察了训练-测试集之间加速率与图像分布均匹配的域内任务。还考察了若干跨域任务，包括加速率不匹配、采样轨迹不匹配、线圈数不匹配或图像分布不匹配。我们发现，AdaDiff 相对条件模型提升了在成像算子域偏移下的可靠性，并相对所有竞争模型提升了在 MR 图像分布域偏移下的可靠性。重要的是，AdaDiff 中的适配过程在域内与跨域场景下都明显优于基于静态先验的竞争扩散模型。然而，考察对更广泛解剖变化的可靠性（例如跨不同身体部位的泛化），以及成像算子的其他变化（例如在笛卡尔与非笛卡尔轨迹之间、在不同线圈阵列之间的泛化），仍是重要的未来工作。

English original
The proposed AdaDiff method was demonstrated against conditional and unconditional baselines for MRI reconstruction. Within-domain tasks were considered with matching acceleration rate and image distribution across the training-test sets. Several cross-domain tasks were also examined with mismatched acceleration rates, mismatched sampling trajectories, mismatched number of coils, or mismatched image distribution. We find that AdaDiff offers improved reliability against domain shifts in the imaging operator against conditional models, and against domain shifts in the MR image distribution against all competing models. Importantly, the adaptation procedure in AdaDiff notably improves reconstruction performance over competing diffusion models based on static priors for both within- and cross-domain scenarios. Yet, it remains important future work to examine reliability against broader changes in anatomy such as generalization across different body parts, and other changes in the imaging operator such as generalization across Cartesian versus non-Cartesian trajectories and across different coil arrays.

AdaDiff 学习一个图像先验，通过扩散采样生成初始重建，然后用推理期优化把先验适配到测试受试者。尽管 AdaDiff 使用了快速扩散过程，先验适配仍会自然抬高运行时间，超过那些一次前向传播即可恢复图像的条件模型。AdaDiff 更接近带迭代图像采样的常规扩散模型，但由于先验适配涉及反向传播，其推理时间仍相对更长。正如预期，AdaDiff 的推理时间与采用类似推理优化的基于 GAN 的先验适配方法相近。计算复杂度的另一个实际关切是推理时的内存占用。在竞争方法中，只利用前向传播的条件模型与常规扩散模型内存占用相对有限。相比之下，包括 AdaDiff 在内的先验适配方法涉及网络的前向与反向传播，因此需要额外内存来存储模型梯度。为提升使用推理优化的方法的实用性，可以通过在 MRI 体数据内相邻横断面之间共享优化后的模型参数 (Korkmaz 等, 2022)，或在多 GPU 上并行计算来提高效率。这里我们采用 Adam 算法，因为它在先验适配中表现良好，并且能保持训练-推理流程之间以及各竞争方法之间的一致性。注意 (Narnhofer 等, 2019) 最初基于 iPALM 算法 (Pock 与 Sabach, 2016) 实现 GANprior。系统研究不同算法在先验适配中的相对收益——包括计算效率与重建性能——仍是重要的未来工作。

English original
AdaDiff learns an image prior that generates an initial reconstruction via diffusion sampling, and then adapts the prior to the test subject with an inference optimization. Despite AdaDiff's rapid diffusion process, prior adaptation naturally elevates run times over conditional models that recover images in a single forward pass. While AdaDiff is closer to regular diffusion models with iterative image sampling, it still yields relatively longer inference due to backward passes involved in prior adaptation. As expected, AdaDiff has similar inference time to the GAN-based prior adaptation method that employs a similar inference optimization. Another practical concern regarding computational complexity is memory load during inference. Among competing methods, conditional models and regular diffusion models that only leverage forward passes have relatively limited memory load. In contrast, prior adaptation methods including AdaDiff involve both forward and backward passes through the network, so they introduce additional memory load to store model gradients. To improve practicality of methods that use inference optimization, efficiency can be increased by sharing optimized model parameters across spatially proximate cross-sections within an MRI volume (Korkmaz et al., 2022), or by parallel computations on multiple GPUs. Here, we adopted the Adam algorithm observed to perform well in prior adaptation for consistency between training-inference procedures and among competing methods. Note that (Narnhofer et al., 2019) originally implemented GANprior based on the iPALM algorithm (Pock 与 Sabach, 2016). It remains important future work to systematically investigate the relative benefits of different algorithms in prior adaptation, including computational efficiency and reconstruction performance.

加速技术近来被考虑用来加快常规扩散模型特征性的慢速采样过程。一种优雅的思路是用另一种重建方法得到的图像来启动采样，包括欠采样 k 空间数据的零填充重建 (Chung 等, 2022)。在未报告的分析中，我们观察到用零填充重建启动先验适配的变体，相对 AdaDiff 在性能或推理时间上都没有明显收益。使用基于学习的方法得到的重建可能有助于加速先验适配，但这也需要独立训练第二套重建模型。另一种强有力的做法是用小步长训练扩散模型，在推理时重新缩放到大步长 (Peng 等, 2022)。该方法缩短了采样过程，但逆向扩散步的精度可能欠优。AdaDiff 则通过对抗映射器在大步长上实现逆向扩散，以提高精度。话虽如此，把 AdaDiff 的对抗映射器与上述加速方法结合，可能带来进一步的收益。

Acceleration techniques have recently been considered to speed up the characteristically slow sampling process in regular diffusion models. An elegant approach is to initiate sampling with the image obtained from a separate reconstruction method, including zero-filled reconstructions of undersampled k-space data (Chung et al., 2022). In unreported analyses, we observed that a variant model that initiated prior adaptation with zero-filled reconstructions does not offer notable benefits in performance or inference time against AdaDiff. While employing reconstructions from learning-based methods might help accelerate prior adaptation, it also necessitates independent training of secondary reconstruction models. Another powerful approach is to train diffusion models with small step size and to rescale to large step sizes during inference (Peng et al., 2022). This method shortens the sampling process, but reverse diffusion steps can potentially have suboptimal accuracy. Instead, AdaDiff implements reverse diffusion over large step sizes via an adversarial mapper for improved accuracy. That said, combining the adversarial mapper in AdaDiff with the abovementioned acceleration approaches might offer further benefits.

近期若干研究提出了 MRI 重建的图像先验适配。一类方法用把低维隐变量映射为图像的未训练先验做重建 (Jin 等, 2019)。为此采用随机初始化权重的卷积架构，其中卷积算子起到正则化合成图像的作用 (Arora 等, 2020)、(Ke 等, 2020)、(Zou 等, 2021)、(Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021)。推理时，未训练先验与成像算子结合并被适配，以强制合成数据与采集数据一致。虽然该做法已报告了性能良好的 MRI 重建，但通常需要明显更长的推理优化，才能让反映未训练先验的图像集与反映成像算子的图像集相交 (Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021)。另一类方法改用预训练在 MR 图像上的先验，以提供更好的适配初始化点。该类先前研究主要提出基于 GAN 模型的先验，由 GAN 隐式刻画 MR 图像分布 (Narnhofer 等, 2019)、(Korkmaz 等, 2022)，也有一些依赖基于图像块的自编码器模型 (Tezcan 等, 2019) 或卷积模型 (Aggarwal 与 Jacob, 2021)、(Han 等, 2018)。我们的工作与近期基于未训练先验的方法不同之处在于：AdaDiff 利用预训练在高质量线圈合并 MR 图像上的先验来提升推理优化效率；它与使用预训练先验的方法也不同，因为 AdaDiff 利用一种新颖的扩散先验来提升图像样本的保真度。

Several recent studies have proposed adaptation of image priors for MRI reconstruction. A group of methods reconstruct with untrained priors that map low-dimensional latent variables onto images (Jin et al., 2019). Convolutional architectures with randomly initialized weights are adopted for this purpose, wherein convolution operators serve to regularize synthesized images (Arora et al., 2020); (Ke et al., 2020); (Zou et al., 2021); (Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021). For inference, the untrained priors are combined with the imaging operator and adapted to enforce consistency between synthesized and acquired data. While performant MRI reconstruction has been reported with this approach, markedly longer inference optimization is typically required to intersect the image set reflecting the untrained prior with the image set reflecting the imaging operator (Darestani 与 Heckel, 2021). An alternative group of methods instead employ priors pre-trained on MR images to provide an improved initialization point for adaptation. Previous studies in this group have predominantly proposed priors based on GAN models that implicitly characterize the MR image distribution (Narnhofer et al., 2019); (Korkmaz et al., 2022), while some rely on patch-based auto-encoder models (Tezcan et al., 2019) or convolutional models (Aggarwal 与 Jacob, 2021); (Han et al., 2018). Our work differs from recent methods based on untrained priors in that AdaDiff leverages a prior pre-trained on high-quality coil-combined MR images to improve efficiency during inference optimization. It also differs from methods with pre-trained priors since AdaDiff leverages a novel diffusion prior to improve fidelity of image samples.

理论上，先验适配可以基于常规扩散模型而非本文采用的对抗扩散模型来执行。然而，常规扩散模型在先验适配的语境下可能带来若干局限。注意，用常规扩散模型生成初始重建，涉及在数百个逆向扩散步上做图像采样并与数据一致性投影交错 (Chung 等, 2022)。因此，用常规扩散模型计算初始重建所需的推理时间与先验适配阶段相当（例如见表 11 中的 DDPM 与 AdaDiff），会显著抬高整体计算负担。此外，基于数百次数据一致性投影的初始重建天然会与采集 k 空间数据更匹配。反过来，常规扩散模型已经具有很低的数据一致性损失，从而显著限制了先验适配能带来的额外收益。为改进常规扩散模型的先验适配，可以在初始重建阶段部分省略数据一致性投影，以确保数据一致性损失保持在合理的高水平。考察常规扩散模型下先验适配的最优训练与推理流程仍是未来工作。

In theory, prior adaptation can be performed based on regular diffusion models instead of the adversarial diffusion model considered here. However, regular diffusion models might elicit several limitations in the context of prior adaptation. Note that generation of the initial reconstruction with regular diffusion models involves image sampling across hundreds of reverse diffusion steps interleaved with data-consistency projections (Chung et al., 2022). Thus, computing the initial reconstruction with regular diffusion models requires comparable inference time to the prior adaptation stage (e.g., see DDPM and AdaDiff in Table 11), significantly elevating the overall computational burden. Furthermore, the initial reconstruction based on hundreds of data-consistency projections naturally yields an enhanced match to acquired k-space data. In turn, regular diffusion models already yield low data-consistency loss, significantly limiting the added benefit that can be achieved via prior adaptation. To improve prior adaptation with regular diffusion models, data-consistency projections might be partly omitted during the initial reconstruction stage to ensure a reasonably high level of data-consistency loss. It remains future work to examine the optimal training and inference procedures for prior adaptation with regular diffusion models.

AdaDiff 的重建性能可能通过若干技术路线得到改进。第一，所有报告的模型都是把每个数据集中多个不同对比度的采集汇合后训练的，训练好的模型随后用于独立重建各个 MRI 对比度。在每个对比度上分别训练独立模型可能提升性能，代价是计算负担 (Dar 等, 2020b)。当每个受试者都有多对比度加速 MRI 协议可用时，也可以使用联合重建模型，利用对比度之间的结构相关性来提升性能 (Dar 等, 2020a)、(Polak 等, 2020)、(Gaillochet 等, 2020)、(Xuan 等, 2022)。对 AdaDiff 而言，这需要训练多对比度扩散先验。或者，可以把对比度类型作为辅助信息提供，以在统一模型中保持对单个对比度的特异性 (Liu 等, 2022)、(Dalmaz 等, 2022a)。第二，可以采用循环一致性学习策略，以减轻 AdaDiff 对包含全采样采集的数据集的依赖 (Quan 等, 2018)、(Oh 等, 2020)、(Ozbey 等, 2022)。这里 AdaDiff 为计算效率只在最终时步优化生成器参数来执行先验适配。理论上，在全部扩散步上执行先验适配可以改进重建性能。但在实践中，跨多个扩散步的推理优化需要计算并存储所有步的梯度。反过来，这会显著抬高 AdaDiff 的内存占用与推理时间，限制其实用性。

Reconstruction performance for AdaDiff might be improved through several lines of technical development. First, all reported models were trained by pooling acquisitions across multiple distinct contrasts in each dataset. The trained models were then used for independently reconstructing individual MRI contrasts. Performance might be improved by training separate models on each contrast, at the expense of computational burden (Dar et al., 2020b). When a multi-contrast accelerated MRI protocol is available in each subject, joint reconstruction models can also be used to exploit structural correlations among contrasts to improve performance (Dar et al., 2020a); (Polak et al., 2020); (Gaillochet et al., 2020); (Xuan et al., 2022). For AdaDiff, this would involve training of a multi-contrast diffusion prior. Alternatively, contrast type can be provided as side information to maintain specificity to individual contrasts in a unified model (Liu et al., 2022); (Dalmaz et al., 2022a). Second, cycle-consistent learning strategies can be adopted to alleviate the dependence of AdaDiff on datasets comprising fully-sampled acquisitions (Quan et al., 2018); (Oh et al., 2020); (Ozbey et al., 2022). Here, AdaDiff was implemented to perform prior adaptation by optimizing the generator parameters at the final time step for computational efficiency. In theory, performing prior adaptation on the entire set of diffusion steps could improve reconstruction performance. In practice, however, inference optimization over multiple diffusion steps requires computation and storage of gradients across all steps. In turn, this would substantially elevate the memory load and inference time for AdaDiff, limiting practical utility.

本研究的首要关注点是改进基于扩散的 MRI 重建泛化能力的学习策略。因此，我们采用了在先前生成建模研究中报告高性能的卷积生成器与判别器架构 (Song 等, 2020)、(Xiao 等, 2022)、(Karras 等, 2020)。仍需进一步工作来评估所采用架构中各种设计元素对 AdaDiff 性能的贡献。未来研究还应探索替代架构（如 transformer 骨干 (Dalmaz 等, 2022b)、(G\"ung\"or 等, 2022)、(Guo 等, 2022)）的效用，以及不同归一化层对模型性能的影响。为表示复数 MRI 数据，我们遵循基于学习的 MRI 重建的常见做法，用独立的网络通道表示实部与虚部 (Schlemper 等, 2017)、(Eo 等, 2018)、(Sriram 等, 2020)、(Aggarwal 等, 2019)、(Yang 等, 2020)。近期研究表明，复数网络运算可能在面向相位的重建任务中带来收益 (Dedmari 等, 2018)、(Küstner 等, 2020)、(Cole 等, 2021)、(Wang 等, 2020b)。探索在 AdaDiff 中采用复数运算的潜在收益仍是重要的未来工作。

The primary focus of the current study was on a learning strategy to improve generalization in diffusion-based MRI reconstruction. Thus, we adopted convolutional generator and discriminator architectures reported to offer high performance in previous studies on generative modeling (Song et al., 2020); (Xiao et al., 2022); (Karras et al., 2020). Further work is warranted to assess the contributions of various design elements in the employed architectures to AdaDiff's performance. Future studies should also be conducted to explore the utility of alternative architectures such as transformer backbones (Dalmaz et al., 2022b); (G\"ung\"or et al., 2022); (Guo et al., 2022), and the influence of different normalization layers on model performance. To represent complex MRI data, we used separate network channels for the real and imaginary components following common practice in learning-based MRI reconstruction (Schlemper et al., 2017); (Eo et al., 2018); (Sriram et al., 2020); (Aggarwal et al., 2019); (Yang et al., 2020). Recent studies suggest that complex-valued network operations might offer benefits particularly in phase-oriented reconstruction tasks (Dedmari et al., 2018); (Küstner et al., 2020); (Cole et al., 2021); (Wang et al., 2020b). It remains important future work to explore the potential benefits of adopting complex-valued operations in AdaDiff.

7 结论

English original
7 Conclusion

本研究提出了首个基于扩散建模、用于 MRI 重建的先验适配方法。AdaDiff 利用逆向扩散的对抗映射器，能够在少数几步内高效生成图像。推理时，通过训练好的扩散先验做快速投影得到初始重建；随后通过把先验进一步适配到测试受试者来计算最终重建。与最先进基线相比，AdaDiff 在域内任务中具有竞争力，并在跨域任务中取得更优的重建。因此，AdaDiff 在高性能 MRI 重建方面大有前景。

English original
In this study, we introduced the first prior adaptation method based on diffusion modeling for MRI reconstruction. AdaDiff leverages an adversarial mapper for reverse diffusion that enables efficient image generation in few steps. During inference, an initial reconstruction is obtained via rapid projection through the trained diffusion prior. The final reconstruction is then computed by further adapting the prior to the test subject. Compared against state-of-the-art baselines, AdaDiff performs competitively in within-domain tasks, and achieves superior reconstructions in cross-domain tasks. Therefore, AdaDiff holds great promise for high-performance MRI reconstruction.
