跨分布扩散先验驱动的稀疏视角 CT 迭代重建

Cross-Distribution Diffusion Priors-Driven Iterative Reconstruction for Sparse-View CT

Haodong Li · Shuo Han · Haiyang Mao · Yu Shi · Changsheng Fang · Jianjia Zhang · Weiwen Wu · Hengyong Yu

稀疏视角 CT 的难点不只是投影少，还在于测试数据常常换了扫描仪、协议和解剖部位。这篇论文把 SiT（可扩展插值 Transformer）扩散模型用 CFG 在多个数据集上训练成一个「跨分布先验」，再把它嵌进 ASD-POCS 的投影域数据一致性迭代里，让生成式修正只发生在实测正弦图允许的不确定性范围内。

English original
Abstract

稀疏视角 CT（SVCT）重建能提高时间分辨率并降低辐射剂量，但由于视角减少带来的伪影，以及扫描仪、协议或解剖变异带来的域偏移，其临床应用受到限制；在分布外（OOD）场景下性能会明显下降。我们提出跨分布扩散先验驱动的迭代重建（CDPIR）框架，来处理 SVCT 中的 OOD 问题。CDPIR 把由可扩展插值 Transformer（SiT）得到的跨分布扩散先验与基于模型的迭代重建方法结合起来。具体而言，我们训练一个 SiT 骨干网络——扩散 Transformer（DiT）架构的扩展——来建立统一的随机插值框架，并在多个数据集上使用无分类器引导（CFG）。通过在训练时用空嵌入随机丢弃条件，模型学到的跨分布先验更具可迁移性：它鼓励学到域不变（domain-invariant）的解剖结构，同时允许域特定（domain-specific）的外观调制。在采样阶段，这种对全局敏感、基于 Transformer 的扩散模型在统一的随机插值框架内利用该跨分布先验，从而能够灵活而稳定地控制「多分布到噪声」的插值路径与解耦的采样策略，改善对 OOD 重建的适应能力。通过在数据保真与采样更新之间交替，我们的模型在 SVCT 重建中取得了当前最佳性能，并更好地保留细节。大量实验结果表明，CDPIR 明显优于现有方法，尤其在 OOD 条件下，凸显了它在高难度成像场景中的稳健性与潜在临床价值。代码见 https://github.com/Graeme-Lee/CDPIR 。

English original
Sparse-View CT (SVCT) reconstruction improves temporal resolution and reduces radiation dose, yet its clinical use is hindered by artifacts due to view reduction and domain shifts from scanner, protocol, or anatomical variations, leading to performance degradation in out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios. We propose a Cross-Distribution Diffusion Priors-Driven Iterative Reconstruction (CDPIR) framework to tackle the OOD problem in SVCT. CDPIR integrates cross-distribution diffusion priors, derived from a Scalable Interpolant Transformer (SiT), with model-based iterative reconstruction methods. Specifically, we train a SiT backbone, an extension of the Diffusion Transformer (DiT) architecture, to establish a unified stochastic interpolant framework, leveraging Classifier-Free Guidance (CFG) across multiple datasets. By randomly dropping the conditioning with a null embedding during training, the model learns a more transferable cross-distribution prior that encourages domain-invariant anatomical structures while allowing domain-specific appearance modulation. During sampling, the globally sensitive transformer-based diffusion model exploits the cross-distribution prior within the unified stochastic interpolant framework, enabling flexible and stable control over multi-distribution-to-noise interpolation paths and decoupled sampling strategies, thereby improving adaptation to OOD reconstruction. By alternating between data fidelity and sampling updates, our model achieves state-of-the-art performance with superior detail preservation in SVCT reconstructions. Extensive experimental results demonstrate that CDPIR significantly outperforms existing approaches, particularly under OOD conditions, highlighting its robustness and potential clinical value in challenging imaging scenarios. The code is available at https://github.com/Graeme-Lee/CDPIR.

1 Introduction

稀疏视角计算机断层成像（SVCT）已成为降低辐射剂量、加快数据采集的有效策略。通过把所需的角度投影数降到最少，SVCT 提高了先进 CT 系统的效率，在时间敏感的场景中尤其如此。SVCT 的优势在基于 Medipix 探测器的光子计数 CT（PCCT，又称能谱 CT）用于肢体成像时格外突出 [1], [2], [3], [4]，这类系统可以借助新型对比剂实现功能与分子成像。与传统 CT 相比，PCCT 能获得丰富的能谱信息，但通常扫描时间长、辐射剂量大，在要求高空间分辨率的肢体应用中尤为如此。SVCT 通过缩短采集时间、降低剂量直接缓解这些限制，使能谱成像更适用于纵向研究、动态增强协议以及对运动敏感的临床场景。然而，SVCT 也会引入严重伪影并有丢失精细结构细节的风险。因此，要充分发挥 SVCT 的临床潜力、提升肢体 PCCT 系统的实用性，发展稳健的重建算法至关重要。

English original
Sparse-view computed tomography (SVCT) has emerged as an effective strategy to reduce radiation dose and accelerate image acquisition. By minimizing the number of required angular projections, SVCT improves the efficiency of advanced CT systems, particularly in time-sensitive scenarios. The benefits of SVCT are especially pronounced in Medipix detector-based photon-counting CT (PCCT), also known as spectral CT, for extremity imaging [1], [2], [3], [4], which enables functional and molecular imaging with novel contrast agents. Compared with conventional CT, PCCT acquires rich spectral information but typically suffers from long scan durations and substantial radiation exposure, especially in extremity applications where high spatial resolution is essential. SVCT directly alleviates these limitations by shortening acquisition time and reducing dose, making spectral imaging more practical for longitudinal studies, dynamic contrast-enhanced protocols, and motion-prone clinical settings. Nonetheless, SVCT introduces severe artifacts and the risk of losing fine structural details. Consequently, developing robust reconstruction algorithms is critical to fully realize the clinical potential of SVCT and to improve the practical utility of extremity PCCT systems.

近年来，已有许多方法被提出来解决 SVCT 图像重建问题。传统的基于模型的迭代重建（MBIR）技术 [5], [6] 已被广泛探索。这类方法把先验假设强加到解空间上以抑制伪影，但往往难以保留高频细节，并且需要大量调参。近来，深度学习成为强有力的替代方案。基于 CNN 的方法（例如 FBPConvNet [7] 与 RED-CNN [8]）利用成对训练数据学习图像先验，把低质量重建直接映射为高质量对应物。这些方法相较传统方法展现出显著性能提升，主要归功于其强归纳偏置与学习层次化特征的能力。此外，基于 Transformer 的网络近来通过捕获长程依赖与全局上下文，在 SVCT 重建中显示出潜力 [9], [10], [11]。尽管其高昂计算开销对大幅 CT 图像构成挑战，但高效变体（例如复原 Transformer，Restormer [12]）提供了可扩展的设计，在局部细节保留与全局上下文建模之间取得平衡。

English original
In recent years, many methods have been developed to address SVCT image reconstruction issue. Traditional model-based iterative reconstruction (MBIR) techniques [5], [6] have been widely explored. They impose prior assumptions onto the solution space to reduce artifacts, but often struggle with preserving high-frequency details and require extensive tuning. Recently, deep learning has emerged as a powerful alternative. CNN-based methods (e.g., FBPConvNet [7] and RED-CNN [8]) employ paired training data to learn image priors and directly map low-quality reconstructions to their high-quality counterparts. These methods have demonstrated remarkable performance improvements over traditional approaches, mainly due to their strong inductive biases and ability to learn hierarchical features. In addition, Transformer-based networks have recently shown promise in SVCT reconstruction by capturing long-range dependencies and global contexts [9], [10], [11]. Although their high computational cost poses challenges for large CT images, efficient variants such as the Restoration Transformer (Restormer) [12] offer scalable designs to balance local detail preservation with global context modeling.

在实践中，获取成对的训练目标往往很困难，对 PCCT 尤其如此——匹配的参考数据常常拿不到。这促使人们采用自监督与零样本策略 [13], [14], [15]，以减少对干净目标或重新训练的依赖。尽管取得了成功，多数 CNN 与 Transformer 方法以及许多自监督、零样本策略仍然是判别式点估计器，用逐像素目标（例如 MSE）训练，容易给出条件均值解，产生高频纹理被削弱的过度平滑重建。这些局限促使重建转向生成式先验，特别是去噪扩散概率模型（DDPM）[16] 与基于分数的生成模型（SGM）[17], [18], [19]，它们能对复杂图像分布建模，并为病态逆问题提供类后验采样。基于扩散的逆问题求解器（DIS），例如 DiffusionMBIR [20] 与分解扩散采样器（DDS）[21]，进一步在生成轨迹中强制数据一致性以融入测量保真，从而由物理与学习到的先验共同引导迭代精化。近期的进展还通过可扩展骨干网络与少步采样方案，使基于扩散的重建越来越实用。

English original
In practice, obtaining paired training targets can be difficult, especially for PCCT where matched reference data are often unavailable. This has motivated self-supervised and zero-shot strategies [13], [14], [15] to reduce reliance on clean targets or retraining. Despite their success, most CNN and Transformer methods and many self-supervised and zero-shot strategies remain discriminative point-estimators trained with pixel-wise objectives (e.g., MSE), which tends to yield conditional-mean solutions and produces over-smoothed reconstructions with attenuated high-frequency textures. These limitations have motivated a shift toward generative priors for reconstruction, particularly denoising diffusion probabilistic models (DDPMs) [16] and score-based generative models (SGMs) [17], [18], [19], which can model complex image distributions and provide posterior-like sampling for ill-posed inverse problems. Diffusion-based inverse solvers (DIS), such as DiffusionMBIR [20] and the Decomposed Diffusion Sampler (DDS) [21], further integrate measurement fidelity by enforcing data consistency during the generative trajectory, enabling iterative refinement guided by both physics and learned priors. Recent advances also make diffusion-based reconstruction increasingly practical via scalable backbones and reduced-step sampling schemes.

在真实部署中，SVCT 重建还面临训练与测试数据之间的显著分布偏移。例如，AAPM 低剂量 CT 挑战赛数据集 [22] 是用西门子 SOMATOM 扫描仪按腹部协议采集的，而斯坦福 COCA 数据集 [23] 由冠状动脉钙化与胸部 CT 扫描组成，解剖焦点、采集协议不同，重建参数也未明确。这类跨扫描仪、跨协议的变化会导致分布外（OOD）场景：测试测量偏离训练分布，使基于 CNN、Transformer 与扩散模型的重建都明显退化。这些观察凸显了两个根本挑战（图 1）：① 稀疏视角伪影，即由投影欠采样引起的严重条状伪影与结构保真度损失；② 跨域分布差异，即依赖扫描仪与协议的变化所诱发的 OOD 场景。

English original
In real-world deployment, SVCT reconstruction also faces substantial distribution shifts between training and testing data. For example, the AAPM Low-Dose CT Grand Challenge dataset [22] was acquired on Siemens SOMATOM scanners with abdominal protocols, whereas the Stanford COCA dataset [23] consists of coronary calcium and chest CT scans with different anatomical foci, acquisition protocols, and unspecified reconstruction parameters. Such cross-scanner and cross-protocol variations lead to out-of-distribution (OOD) scenarios in which testing measurements deviate from the training distribution, causing CNN-, Transformer-, and diffusion-based reconstructions to degrade markedly. These observations highlight two fundamental challenges (Fig. 1): ① Sparse-view artifacts, i.e., severe streak artifacts and loss of structural fidelity arising from undersampled projections; and ② Cross-domain distribution differences, i.e., scanner- and protocol-dependent variations that induce OOD scenarios.

重要的是，在病态稀疏视角采集与 OOD 偏移的共同作用下，基于学习的先验可能表现出性质不同的失效模式：幻觉——视觉上合理却违反数据约束的错误结构。这在医学成像中尤其令人担忧，因为幻觉出的纹理或结构可能伪装成病变，或掩盖细微病灶，带来诊断风险。近来提升跨域偏移下稳健性的努力大致可分为三个方向。(1) 测试时适配：在重建过程中把预训练先验适配到目标测量上。例如 SCD 注入轻量 LoRA 模块，在采样时执行由数据一致性驱动的适配以改善 OOD 性能；DDIP3D 则把这一思路扩展为面向体逆问题的高效、连贯的 3D 适配 [24], [25]。(2) 大容量 Transformer 模型：通过扩大表示能力与长程上下文建模来缓解 OOD 退化。例如 X-GRM 采用可扩展的 Transformer 架构，报告在域内与域外稀疏视角输入上都取得很强的重建质量 [26]。(3) 零样本基于分数的求解器：把预训练的扩散/分数先验与显式数据保真耦合，从而无需任务特定重训练即可应对分布偏移。例如变分分数求解器（VSS）优化一个把扩散先验项与数据一致性项结合的变分目标来做 SVCT 重建，实现分布感知的重建，并在某些设定下支持零样本部署 [27]。

English original
Importantly, under the combined effects of ill-posed sparse-view acquisition and OOD shift, learning-based priors may exhibit a qualitatively different failure mode: hallucinations—visually plausible yet incorrect structures that violate data constraints. This is particularly concerning in medical imaging, as hallucinated textures or structures may mimic pathology or obscure subtle lesions, posing diagnostic risks. Recent efforts to improve robustness under cross-domain shifts can be broadly grouped into three directions. (1) Test-time adaptation adapts a pre-trained prior to the target measurements during reconstruction. For example, SCD injects lightweight LoRA modules and performs data-consistency-driven adaptation at sampling time to improve OOD performance, while DDIP3D extends this idea to efficient and coherent 3D adaptation for volumetric inverse problems [24], [25]. (2) Large-capacity transformer models mitigate OOD degradation by scaling representation power and long-range context modeling. For example, X-GRM employs a scalable transformer-based architecture and reports strong reconstruction quality on both in-domain and out-of-domain sparse-view inputs [26]. (3) Zero-shot score-based solvers aim to handle distribution shifts without task-specific retraining by coupling a pretrained diffusion/score prior with explicit data fidelity. For example, Variational Score Solver (VSS) optimizes a variational objective that combines a diffusion prior term with a data-consistency term for SVCT reconstruction, enabling distribution-aware reconstruction and, in some settings, zero-shot deployment [27].

尽管进展令人鼓舞，这些方向在稳健的临床应用上仍有局限：测试时适配会带来逐病例的优化开销；大容量模型提升了平均稳健性，却无法在罕见 OOD 情形下充分控制认知不确定性；而在严重病态的情形中，若语义先验压过测量约束，扩散先验还可能引入随机幻觉。这些观察表明，稳健的 OOD SVCT 重建需要：(i) 一个可扩展的跨分布生成先验，用来表示异构扫描仪与协议下的合理解剖；以及 (ii) 一个显式的、基于物理的迭代数据一致性机制，对从未见过的正弦图强制满足实测投影。

English original
Despite encouraging progress, these directions still have limitations for robust clinical applications: test-time adaptation can incur per-case optimization overhead; large-capacity models improve average robustness but do not fully control epistemic uncertainty under rare OOD cases; and diffusion priors may introduce stochastic hallucinations if semantic priors dominate measurement constraints in severely ill-posed cases. These observations suggest that robust OOD SVCT reconstruction requires (i) a scalable, cross-distribution generative prior to represent plausible anatomy across heterogeneous scanners and protocols, and (ii) an explicit physics-based iterative data-consistency mechanism to enforce measured projections for previously unseen sinograms.

受这一原则启发，我们提出跨分布扩散先验驱动的迭代重建（CDPIR）框架（图 1）。CDPIR 的主要创新集中在先验一侧，包含两个组件。第一，它采用可扩展插值 Transformer（SiT）[28] 作为生成骨干，该骨干在随机插值公式下支持系统的模型容量缩放与全局上下文建模。第二，它利用无分类器引导（CFG）[29]，通过域 token 实现跨分布训练。训练时随机丢弃域 token，CDPIR 便同时学习一个域条件分支与一个空条件分支，促使扩散先验捕获各训练域共享的解剖级结构，同时建模依赖域的外观统计（例如纹理、噪声与边缘锐度）。SiT 与 CFG 结合，得到一个既有表现力又可扩展的跨分布随机插值扩散先验。最后，CDPIR 把该跨分布先验整合进带投影域数据一致性更新的残差引导迭代重建流程。这种整合把生成式精化限制在实测正弦图允许的不确定性范围内，从而在跨域 OOD 偏移下既压制稀疏视角伪影，又缓解由先验驱动的幻觉。

Motivated by this principle, we propose a Cross-distribution Diffusion Priors-driven Iterative Reconstruction (CDPIR) framework (Fig. 1). The main innovations of CDPIR focus on the prior side through two components. First, it employs the Scalable Interpolant Transformer (SiT) [28] as the generative backbone, which supports systematic model-capacity scaling and global context modeling within the stochastic interpolant formulation. Second, it leverages classifier-free guidance (CFG) [29] to enable cross-distribution training via domain tokens. Finally, CDPIR integrates this cross-distribution prior into a residual-guided iterative reconstruction pipeline with projection-domain data-consistency updates. This integration restricts generative refinement to the uncertainty permitted by the measured sinogram, thereby suppressing sparse-view artifacts while mitigating prior-driven hallucinations under cross-domain OOD shifts.

5 讨论

English original
5 Discussion

我们的实验表明，CDPIR 在具有挑战性的分布偏移下仍保持稳健，能有效应对 OOD 重建场景。与传统迭代方法以及当前最佳的扩散基线 DDS [21] 相比，CDPIR 在压制条纹与其他采样引入的伪影的同时，更好地保留细解剖结构，在同分布与 OOD 设定下都给出更忠实的重建。

English original
Our experiments demonstrate that CDPIR remains robust under challenging distribution shifts and can effectively handle OOD reconstruction scenarios. Compared with traditional iterative methods and the state-of-the-art diffusion baseline DDS [21], CDPIR better preserves fine anatomical structures while suppressing streaking and other sampling-induced artifacts, yielding more faithful reconstructions in both in-distribution and OOD settings.

为评估实际收敛性与采样效率，我们在 AAPM--COCA 设定下的一个代表性 OOD 测试病例上，用从 100 到 1,000 的不同采样步数评估重建质量。我们比较 DDS [21]、DDIP3D [25] 与 CDPIR。如图 15 所示，DDS 与 DDIP3D 通常需要接近 1,000 步才能达到可接受质量，而 CDPIR 用少得多的步数就能得到高质量重建。这一行为与我们在 SiT 表述中使用的基于插值的随机过程更好的数值稳定性一致：速度参数化带来更平滑、更稳定的收敛动力学。在实践中我们发现，300 步已能提供令人满意的重建质量，同时显著缩短推理时间。

English original
To evaluate practical convergence and sampling efficiency, we evaluate reconstruction quality with a reduced number of sampling steps from 100 to 1,000 using a representative OOD testing case from the AAPM--COCA setting. We compare DDS [21], DDIP3D [25], and CDPIR. As shown in Fig. 15, DDS and DDIP3D typically require close to 1,000 steps to reach acceptable quality, whereas CDPIR achieves high-quality reconstructions with substantially fewer steps. This behavior is consistent with the improved numerical stability of the interpolant-based stochastic process used in our SiT formulation, where the velocity parameterization yields smoother and more stable convergence dynamics. In practice, we find that 300 steps already provide satisfactory reconstruction quality while significantly reducing inference time.

尽管有这些收益，基于扩散的重建本质上仍是迭代的，因此运行时间往往高于一次性回归器。表 X 汇总了代表性扩散基线与 CDPIR 在 1,000 采样步下每切片的平均推理时间、峰值显存占用与参数量，我们也报告了 300 步的 CDPIR-300。在 300 步下，CDPIR 用 2,194 MB 峰值显存与 142.9 M 参数在 507.2 秒（8.45 分钟）内重建一次完整 CT 扫描，而 DDIP3D 需要 23.31 分钟、8,252 MB 峰值显存与 374.0 M 参数。虽然当前运行时间对时间关键的临床流程而言可能仍偏高，但在不改变重建目标的前提下，有若干实用策略可进一步降低计算：利用图 15 中观察到的快速收敛来采用减步调度与早停；用确定性的概率流 ODE 采样消除随机开销；对 Transformer 块做混合精度推理与算子/内核融合；以及更高效的正投影/反投影实现，包括跨迭代的缓存与复用。这些优化与 CDPIR 正交，可被引入以提升吞吐并促成近实时部署。

English original
Despite these gains, diffusion-based reconstruction remains inherently iterative and therefore often requires higher runtime than one-shot regressors. Table X summarizes the average inference time per slice under 1,000 sampling steps, peak GPU memory footprint, and parameter count for representative diffusion-based baselines and CDPIR. We also report CDPIR-300 with 300 sampling steps. With 300 steps, CDPIR reconstructs one full CT scan in 507.2 s (8.45 min) with 2,194 MB peak GPU memory and 142.9 M parameters, whereas DDIP3D requires 23.31 min with 8,252 MB peak memory and 374.0 M parameters. While the current runtime may still be high for time-critical clinical workflows, several practical strategies can further reduce computation without changing the reconstruction objective: reduced-step schedules and early stopping enabled by the fast convergence observed in Fig. 15; deterministic probability-flow ODE sampling to eliminate stochastic overhead; mixed-precision inference and kernel/operator fusion for Transformer blocks; and more efficient projector/backprojector implementations, including caching and reuse across iterations. These optimizations are orthogonal to CDPIR and can be incorporated to improve throughput and facilitate near-real-time deployment.

CDPIR 的强泛化能力部分归因于其基于 Transformer 的架构。与可能受 CNN 归纳偏置约束的 U-Net 扩散模型相比，Transformer 能捕获长程依赖、随模型容量有效扩展，并把修正信号传播到可靠性异质的区域。该设计不仅提升重建保真度，也为探索替代归纳偏置提供了灵活基础，例如 Mamba [38] 这样的状态空间模型：它引入更强的序列结构，可能进一步提升结构化体数据的处理效率。

English original
The strong generalizability of CDPIR is partly attributable to its Transformer-based architecture. Compared with U-Net diffusion models that may be constrained by CNN inductive biases, Transformers capture long-range dependencies, scale effectively with model capacity, and propagate correction signals across regions with heterogeneous reliability. This design not only improves reconstruction fidelity but also provides a flexible foundation for exploring alternative inductive biases, such as state-space models like Mamba [38], which introduce stronger sequential structure and may further improve efficiency in structured volumetric data.

我们还观察到一种非对称迁移效应：即便在 COCA 上测试，用 AAPM 训练有时也能与用 COCA 训练持平甚至更好，这体现在图 6 的 AAPM→\rightarrow→COCA 方向上。这可由数据集特性解释：COCA 聚焦于裁剪过的心脏/胸部区域，视野更小、解剖变异更有限，且在我们的划分中训练切片更少。相反，AAPM 提供更广的解剖覆盖，包含更完整的胸腹部结构，训练集也大得多（多出约 600+600+600+ 个切片）。因此，用 AAPM 训练的模型可能学到更强、更可迁移的先验，导致「训练 AAPM→\rightarrow→测试 COCA」在绝对 PSNR 上与「训练 COCA→\rightarrow→测试 COCA」持平或更高。我们加入这一说明，以免解读跨域结果时产生困惑。

English original
We also observe an asymmetric transfer effect in which training on AAPM can sometimes match or outperform training on COCA even when testing on COCA, as reflected by the AAPM→\rightarrow→COCA direction in Fig. 6. This can be explained by dataset characteristics: COCA is centered on a cropped cardiac/chest region, which reduces field-of-view, limits anatomical variability, and provides fewer training slices in our split. In contrast, AAPM offers broader anatomical coverage, including more complete thoracic and abdominal structures, and a substantially larger training set with approximately 600+600+600+ additional slices. Consequently, models trained on AAPM may learn a stronger and more transferable prior, leading to cases where Training AAPM→\rightarrow→Testing COCA matches or exceeds Training COCA→\rightarrow→Testing COCA in absolute PSNR. We include this clarification to avoid confusion when interpreting cross-domain results.

另一个活跃的研究方向探索用隐式神经表示（INR）与 3D 高斯表示（3DGS）做断层重建。INR 把体数据建模为基于坐标的连续神经场，例如 SIREN 或 NeRF [39], [40], [41], [42], [43]；3D 高斯泼溅则用可学习的高斯原语表示场景。这些方法可以通过直接从自身测量优化表示来重建受试对象，从而减少对跨域泛化的依赖。但它们通常需要针对受试对象的优化、稠密或多视角采集以及大量优化时间，这可能限制其在稀疏视角或时间关键的临床 CT 中的适用性。相反，CDPIR 学习一个跨扫描仪与协议都能泛化的群体级扩散先验，并把它嵌入物理引导的迭代循环以获得稳健重建。这些范式是互补的，未来的混合方法可能把 INR/3DGS 参数化与基于扩散的先验结合，以统一连续空间建模与跨域泛化。

English original
Another active research direction explores implicit neural representations (INRs) and 3D Gaussian representations (3DGS) for tomographic reconstruction. INRs model volumetric data as continuous coordinate-based neural fields, such as SIREN or NeRF [39], [40], [41], [42], [43], while 3D Gaussian splatting represents scenes with learnable Gaussian primitives. These approaches can reconstruct a subject by directly optimizing the representation from its own measurements, thereby reducing reliance on cross-domain generalization. However, they typically require subject-specific optimization, dense or multi-view acquisitions, and substantial optimization time, which can limit applicability to sparse-view or time-critical clinical CT. In contrast, CDPIR learns a population-level diffusion prior that generalizes across scanners and protocols and embeds it into a physics-guided iterative loop for robust reconstruction. These paradigms are complementary, and future hybrid approaches may combine INR/3DGS parameterizations with diffusion-based priors to unify continuous spatial modeling and cross-domain generalization.

CDPIR 的核心优势之一，是它能够跨异构数据集训练，潜在可覆盖数以百计的成像域。通过学习一个把共享结构信息与域特定调制结合起来的可迁移跨分布先验，CDPIR 能适配心脏或肢体成像等专门任务，同时保持广泛适用性。其可扩展性使统一的检查点能在不同数据集与采集体制间泛化而无需任务特定重训练，从而把 CDPIR 定位为医学图像重建中有前景的基础模型，具有广泛的部署潜力。在医学成像之外，CDPIR 把生成先验与显式数据一致性结合的原则，还可能扩展到其他 AI 领域，包括多种模态下的复杂合成任务与通用文本应用 [45]。

English original
A central advantage of CDPIR is its ability to train across heterogeneous datasets, potentially spanning hundreds of imaging domains. By learning a transferable cross-distribution prior that combines shared structural information with domain-specific modulation, CDPIR adapts to specialized tasks, such as cardiac or extremity imaging, while maintaining broad applicability. Its scalability enables unified checkpoints to generalize across datasets and acquisition regimes without task-specific retraining, positioning CDPIR as a promising foundation model for medical image reconstruction with broad deployment potential. Beyond medical imaging, CDPIR's principles of combining generative priors with explicit data consistency could extend to other AI domains, including complex synthesis tasks in diverse modalities and general-purpose text applications [45].

6 结论

English original
6 Conclusion

总之，CDPIR 把基于 Transformer 的扩散去噪与显式数据一致性结合，实现高质量 SVCT 重建。基于 patch 的位置嵌入使模型能在保真约束下做局部修正，同时保持全局连贯，兼顾细节恢复与结构完整性。带无分类器引导的条件扩散 Transformer 支持多域训练下的跨分布先验学习，而统一的随机插值框架提升了对 OOD 场景的适应能力。在 AAPM、XCAT、COCA 与真实 PCCT 数据集上的大量实验确认，CDPIR 优于 DiffusionMBIR 与 DDS 等 SOTA 基线。通过把局部精度与全局一致性统一起来，CDPIR 推进了医学图像重建领域，并为可扩展、可泛化、可临床部署的生成式重建模型铺平了道路。

English original
In summary, CDPIR integrates transformer-based diffusion denoising with explicit data consistency to deliver high-quality SVCT reconstruction. Patch-based positional embeddings enable localized corrections under fidelity constraints while maintaining global coherence, ensuring both fine detail recovery and structural integrity. The conditional diffusion transformer with classifier-free guidance supports cross-distribution prior learning under multi-domain training, while the unified stochastic interpolant framework enhances adaptability to OOD scenarios. Extensive experiments on AAPM, XCAT, COCA, and real-world PCCT datasets confirm that CDPIR outperforms SOTA baselines such as DiffusionMBIR and DDS. By uniting local precision with global consistency, CDPIR advances the field of medical image reconstruction and paves the way for scalable, generalizable, and clinically deployable generative reconstruction models.
