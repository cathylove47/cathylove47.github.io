用生成式反馈做判别式模型的测试时适配

Diffusion-TTA: Test-time Adaptation of Discriminative Models via Generative Feedback

Mihir Prabhudesai* · Tsung-Wei Ke* · Alexander C. Li · Deepak Pathak · Katerina Fragkiadaki（*同等技术贡献）

已有的测试时适配方法大多要先做联合训练，再在测试时用重建损失微调；它们提升的往往只是自己那个偏弱的初始模型。这篇论文反过来：判别式模型完全不动地拿来，用一个预训练扩散模型当「裁判」，让分类器的输出决定扩散模型的条件，再按扩散损失把梯度回传到分类器权重上，把本来就强的预训练模型继续往上推。

English original
Abstract

生成建模的进展，特别是扩散模型的出现，引出了一个根本问题：怎样才能把这些模型有效地用于判别式任务？在这项工作中，我们发现生成模型可以成为判别式模型极好的测试时适配器。我们的方法 Diffusion-TTA 借助扩散模型给出的生成式反馈，把图像分类器、分割器和深度预测器等预训练判别式模型，适配到测试集中每一个无标签样本上。做法是：用判别式模型的输出去调制扩散模型的条件，然后把梯度回传到判别式模型的参数上，最大化图像似然目标。我们展示 Diffusion-TTA 显著提升了多种大规模预训练判别式模型的准确率，例如 ImageNet 分类器、CLIP 模型、图像像素标注器和图像深度预测器。Diffusion-TTA 优于现有的测试时适配方法，包括 TTT-MAE 和 TENT；在在线适配设定下——判别式模型随着测试集中样本源源不断地到来而持续更新——它的优势尤其明显。代码、结果与可视化见我们的网站：diffusion-tta.github.io/。

English original
The advancements in generative modeling, particularly the advent of diffusion models, have sparked a fundamental question: how can these models be effectively used for discriminative tasks? In this work, we find that generative models can be great test-time adapters for discriminative models. Our method, Diffusion-TTA, adapts pre-trained discriminative models such as image classifiers, segmenters and depth predictors, to each unlabelled example in the test set using generative feedback from a diffusion model. We achieve this by modulating the conditioning of the diffusion model using the output of the discriminative model. We then maximize the image likelihood objective by backpropagating the gradients to discriminative model’s parameters. We show Diffusion-TTA significantly enhances the accuracy of various large-scale pre-trained discriminative models, such as, ImageNet classifiers, CLIP models, image pixel labellers and image depth predictors. Diffusion-TTA outperforms existing test-time adaptation methods, including TTT-MAE and TENT, and particularly shines in online adaptation setups, where the discriminative model is continually adapted to each example in the test set. We provide access to code, results, and visualizations on our website: diffusion-tta.github.io/.

1 Introduction

目前，机器学习中所有想要从输入 xxx 预测输出 yyy 的最先进预测模型，本质上都是判别式的：也就是说，它们是训练来把 xxx 直接映射到 yyy（的某个分布）的函数。

English original
Currently, all state-of-the-art predictive models in machine learning, that care to predict an output yyy from an input xxx, are discriminative in nature, that means, they are functions trained to map directly xxx to (a distribution of) yyy.

例如，现有的成功图像理解模型——无论是分类器、目标检测器、分割器还是图像描述器——都是通过端到端优化最终目标，将图像编码成与下游任务相关的特征，因此会忽略无关的细节。它们在训练分布内表现出色，但一旦离开这个分布，常常会戏剧性地失效 [14, 25]，主要原因是它们学到了捷径通路，以便更好地拟合 p(y∣x)p(y|x)p(y∣x)。

English original
For example, existing successful image understanding models, whether classifiers, object detectors, segmentors, or image captioners, are trained to encode an image into features relevant to the downstream task through end-to-end optimization of the end objective, and thus ignore irrelevant details. Though they shine within the training distribution, they often dramatically fail outside of it [14, 25], mainly because they learn shortcut pathways to better fit p(y∣x)p(y|x)p(y∣x).

生成模型则是在一个难得多的任务上训练的：建模 p(x∣y)p(x|y)p(x∣y)。在测试时，可以用贝叶斯法则把这些模型反演过来，推断出能使当前样本的数据似然 p(x∣y^)p(x|\hat{y})p(x∣y^​) 最大的假设 y^\hat{y}y^​。判别式模型接收输入、以前馈方式预测输出；生成模型则反向工作，在拟合数据 xxx 的假设 yyy 空间中搜索。这种迭代过程也被称为分析-综合（analysis-by-synthesis）[52]，比前馈推断要慢。然而，生成的能力带来了更丰富、更细致的数据理解，从而增强了它的判别潜力 [22]。近期的前人工作，例如 Diffusion Classifier [27]，确实发现在对分布外图像进行分类时，反演生成模型比 ResNet [17] 和 ViT [11] 等流行判别式模型泛化得更好。但同时也发现，在几乎所有流行基准上（测试集位于训练分布之内），纯判别式方法仍然优于生成式反演方法。这并不太令人意外，因为生成模型瞄准的是一个困难得多的问题，而且它们从未直接用最终任务的目​​标训练过。

English original
Generative models on the other hand are trained for a much harder task of modeling p(x∣y)p(x|y)p(x∣y). At test-time, these models can be inverted using the Bayes rule to infer the hypothesis y^\hat{y}y^​ that maximizes the data likelihood of the example at hand, p(x∣y^)p(x|\hat{y})p(x∣y^​). Unlike discriminative models that take an input and predict an output in a feed-forward manner, generative models work backwards, by searching over hypotheses yyy that fit the data xxx. This iterative process, also known as analysis-by-synthesis [52], is slower than feed-forward inference. However, the ability to generate leads to a richer and a more nuanced understanding of the data, and hence enhances its discriminative potential [22]. Recent prior works, such as Diffusion Classifier [27], indeed find that inverting generative models generalizes better at classifying out-of-distribution images than popular discriminative models such as ResNet [17] and ViT [11]. However, it is also found that pure discriminative methods still outperform inversion of generative methods across almost all popular benchmarks, where test sets are within the training distribution. This is not too surprising since generative models aim to solve a much more difficult problem, and they were never directly trained using the end task objective.

在本文中，我们不再把生成模型和判别式模型看作互相竞争的选项，而是主张它们在推断阶段应当被耦合起来，从而同时利用两者的长处：生成式反演的迭代推理能力，以及判别式模型更好的拟合能力。一种朴素的做法是把两者集成（ensemble），即 pθ(y∣x)+pϕ(y∣x)2\frac{p_\theta(y|x)+p_\phi(y|x)}{2}2pθ​(y∣x)+pϕ​(y∣x)​，其中 θ\thetaθ 和 ϕ\phiϕ 分别代表判别式模型与生成模型的参数。但我们在实验中发现，这样做并没有带来多少性能提升，因为两个模型的融合发生得太晚，发生在它们各自预测的最末端。我们改为：借助生成模型，在测试时通过迭代优化去适配判别式模型，让两个模型都朝着最大化样本似然的方向被优化。

English original
In this paper, instead of considering generative and discriminative models as competitive alternatives, we argue that they should be coupled during inference in a way that leverages the benefits of both, namely, the iterative reasoning of generative inversion and the better fitting ability of discriminative models. A naive way of doing so would be to ensemble them, that is pθ(y∣x)+pϕ(y∣x)2\frac{p_\theta(y|x) + p_\phi(y|x)}{2}2pθ​(y∣x)+pϕ​(y∣x)​, where θ\thetaθ and ϕ\phiϕ stand for discriminative and generative model parameters, respectively. However, empirically we find that this does not result in much performance boost, as the fusion between the two models happens late, at the very end of their individual predictions. Instead, we propose to leverage generative models to adapt discriminative models at test time through iterative optimization, where both models are optimized for maximizing a sample’s likelihood.

我们提出基于扩散的测试时适配（Diffusion-based Test Time Adaptation, Diffusion-TTA）。它把图像分类器、分割器、深度预测器等判别式模型适配到单张无标签图像上：用它们的输出去调制图像扩散模型的条件，并最大化图像扩散似然。这一步相当于对生成模型做反演，从而推断出能带来「条件图像似然最高」的判别假设所对应的判别式权重。我们的模型类似编码器-解码器结构：预训练的判别式模型把图像编码成一个假设，例如一个物体类别标签、一张分割图或一张深度图，这个假设再作为条件喂给预训练的生成模型，把图像重新生成出来。我们展示 Diffusion-TTA 能在 ImageNet 及其变体等成熟基准上有效地适配图像分类器，无论是分布内还是分布外样本；也能在 ADE20K 和 NYU Depth 数据集上适配图像分割器与深度预测器。

English original
We present Diffusion-based Test Time Adaptation (TTA) (Diffusion-TTA), a method that adapts discriminative models, such as image classifiers, segmenters and depth predictors, to individual unlabelled images by using their outputs to modulate the conditioning of an image diffusion model and maximize the image diffusion likelihood. This operates as an inversion of the generative model, to infer the discriminative weights that result in the discriminative hypothesis with the highest conditional image likelihood. Our model is reminiscent of an encoder-decoder architecture, where a pre-trained discriminative model encodes the image into a hypothesis, such as an object category label, segmentation map, or depth map, which is used as conditioning to a pre-trained generative model to generate back the image. We show that Diffusion-TTA effectively adapts image classifiers for both in- and out-of-distribution examples across established benchmarks, including ImageNet and its variants, as well as image segmenters and depth predictors in ADE20K and NYU Depth dataset.

此前已有工作把生成模型用于图像分类器和分割器的测试时适配：在判别任务损失与自监督图像重建损失的联合目标下协同训练模型，例如 TTT-MAE [12] 或 Slot-TTA [37]。在测试时，用图像重建损失微调判别式模型。虽然这些模型证明了适配确实能提升性能，但这种提升往往来自它们初始前馈判别模型本身较弱。这一点在我们的 TTT-MAE 基线 [12] 中看得非常清楚：适配前的结果明显低于一个预训练的前馈分类器。相比之下，我们的方法不做任何联合训练，而是直接用预训练的生成式扩散模型，在测试时适配「已经预训练好的」判别式模型。

English original
Generative models have previously been used for test time adaptation of image classifiers and segmentors, by co-training the model under a joint discriminative task loss and a self-supervised image reconstruction loss, e.g., TTT-MAE [12] or Slot-TTA [37]. At test time, the discriminative model is finetuned using the image reconstruction loss. While these models show that adaptation boosts performance, this boost often stems from the subpar performance of their initial feed-forward discriminative model. This can be clearly seen in our TTT-MAE baseline [12], where the before-adaptation results are significantly lower than a pre-trained feed-forward classifier. In contrast, our approach refrains from any joint training, and instead directly adapts pre-trained discriminative models at test time using pre-trained generative diffusion models.

我们在多个任务、数据集和模型架构上测试了本方法。在分类方面，我们测试预训练的 ImageNet 分类器在 ImageNet [9] 及其分布外变体（C、R、A、v2、S）上的表现；此外，我们测试大规模开放词表 CLIP 分类器在 CIFAR100、Food101、FGVC、Oxford Pets 和 Flowers102 数据集上的表现。适配 ImageNet 分类器时，我们使用 DiT [36] 作为生成模型，它是一个在 ImageNet 上从头训练的扩散模型；适配开放词表 CLIP 分类器时，我们使用 Stable Diffusion [40] 作为生成模型。如图 1 所示，我们相对于最初的分类器取得了一致的提升。我们还测试了语义分割和深度估计任务的适配：SegFormer [49] 分割器和 DenseDepth [1] 深度预测器在 ADE20K 与 NYU Depth v2 数据集上的性能都大幅提升。对于分割和深度预测，我们使用在各自数据集上从头训练的条件潜扩散模型 [40]。我们对 Diffusion-TTA 方法的各个组成部分做了大量消融，并分析了扩散生成式反馈如何增强判别式模型。我们希望这项工作能激发更多关于「判别式编码器 + 生成式解码器」组合的研究，以更好地处理训练分布之外的图像。代码与训练好的模型已在项目网站公开：diffusion-tta.github.io/。

English original
We test our approach on multiple tasks, datasets and model architectures. For classification, we test pre-trained ImageNet clasisifers on ImageNet [9], and its out-of-distribution variants (C, R, A, v2, S). Further we test, large-scale open-vocabulary CLIP-based classifiers on CIFAR100, Food101, FGVC, Oxford Pets, and Flowers102 datasets. For adapting ImageNet classifiers we use DiT [36] as our generative model, which is a diffusion model trained on ImageNet from scratch. For adapting open-vocabulary CLIP-based classifiers, we use Stable Diffusion [40] as our generative model. We show consistent improvements over the initially employed classifier as shown in Figure 1. We also test on the adaptation of semantic segmentation and depth estimation tasks, where segmenters of SegFormer [49] and depth predictors of DenseDepth [1] performance are greatly improved on ADE20K and NYU Depth v2 dataset. For segmentation and depth prediction, we use conditional latent diffusion models [40] that are trained from scratch on their respective datasets. We show extensive ablations of different components of our Diffusion-TTA method, and present analyses on how diffusion generative feedback enhances discriminative models. We hope our work to stimulate research on combining discriminative encoders and generative decoder models, to better handle images outside of the training distribution. Our code and trained models are publicly available in our project’s website: diffusion-tta.github.io/.

2 Related Work

用于判别式任务的生成模型。近来有大量工作探索把生成模型用于判别式任务，我们将其分为以下三类：(i) 基于反演的方法：给定测试输入 xxx 和条件生成模型 pϕ(x∣c)p_\phi(x|\mathbf{c})pϕ​(x∣c)，这类方法寻找使测试输入估计似然 pϕ(x∣c)p_\phi(x|\mathbf{c})pϕ​(x∣c) 最大的条件表示 c\mathbf{c}c 来做出预测。根据条件生成模型的架构不同，这种做法可以推断输入图像的文本描述 [8, 27]、类别标签 [27] 或语义图 [7]。例如，Li 等人 [27] 表明：在 Stable Diffusion [40] 这类文生图模型下，寻找使图像似然最大的文本提示（prompt）可以得到很强的零样本分类器；而在类别条件扩散模型下，寻找使图像似然最大的类别索引可以得到很强的标准分类器。(ii) 用于数据增广的生成模型：这类方法用生成模型去增强训练数据分布 [2, 45, 51]。它们用生成模型合成数据，再在增广后的训练集上训练判别式分类器。例如，Azizi 等人 [2] 表明，用他们的大规模扩散模型 Imagen [41] 生成的合成数据增广 ImageNet 的真实训练数据，可以提升分类准确率。(iii) 生成模型作为特征提取器：这类技术在预训练阶段用生成模型学习特征，再在下游判别式任务上微调 [3, 10, 18, 35, 42, 50]。生成式预训练通常能为下游任务提供很强的初始化。然而这些方法需要带监督的数据做模型微调，无法直接以零样本方式应用。

English original
Generative models for discriminative tasks Recently there have been many works exploring the application of generative models for discriminative tasks. We group them into the following three categories: (i) Inversion-Based Methods: Given a test input xxx and a conditional generative model pϕ(x∣c)p_\phi(x|\mathbf{c})pϕ​(x∣c), these methods make a prediction by finding the conditional representation c\mathbf{c}c that maximizes the estimated likelihood pϕ(x∣c)p_\phi(x|\mathbf{c})pϕ​(x∣c) of the test input. Depending on the architecture of the conditional generative model, this approach can infer the text caption [8, 27], class label [27], or semantic map [7] of the input image. For instance, Li et al. [27] showed that finding the text prompt that maximizes the likelihood of an image under a text-to-image model like Stable Diffusion [40] is a strong zero-shot classifier, and finding the class index that maximizes the likelihood of an image under a class-conditional diffusion model is a strong standard classifier. (ii) Generative Models for Data Augmentation: This category of methods involves using a generative model to enhance the training data distribution [2, 45, 51]. These methods create synthetic data using the generative model and train a discriminative classifier on the augmented training dataset. For instance, Azizi et al. [2] showed that augmenting the real training data from ImageNet with synthetic data from Imagen [41], their large-scale diffusion model, improves classification accuracy. (iii) Generative Models as feature extractors: These techniques learn features with a generative model during pretraining and then fine-tune them on downstream discriminative tasks [3, 10, 18, 35, 42, 50]. Generative pre-training is typically a strong initialization for downstream tasks. However, these methods require supervised data for model fine-tuning and cannot be directly applied in a zero-shot manner.

我们的工作建立在「基于反演的方法 (i)」这一概念之上。我们提出：与其去反演条件表示，不如直接用似然损失去适配一个预训练的判别式模型。这一策略使我们能够有效地结合生成模型与判别式模型各自最好的部分。我们的方法与利用生成模型的其它途径（如生成式预训练，或生成额外的合成带标签图像）是互补的。

4.5 讨论——局限

English original
4.5 Discussion - Limitations

Diffusion-TTA 用生成式扩散损失优化预训练的判别式模型与生成式模型。这一优化可能存在一个平凡解：判别式模型 fθf_\thetafθ​ 过拟合生成式损失，从而精确地预测 pϕ(c∣x)p_\phi(\mathbf{c}|x)pϕ​(c∣x)。实验上我们发现这并未发生：如表 7 所示，Diffusion-TTA 始终优于 Diffusion Classifier [27]。我们猜想，判别式模型之所以没有过拟合生成式损失，原因在于（预训练的）判别式模型的权重初始化，它使得模型无法收敛到这个平凡解。例如，如果生成式模型与判别式模型的预测彼此相差太远，即 distance(pϕ(c∣x),pθ(c∣x))\textrm{distance}(p_\phi(\mathbf{c}|x), p_\theta(\mathbf{c}|x))distance(pϕ​(c∣x),pθ​(c∣x)) 太大，那么就很难通过优化 θ\thetaθ 到达一个能预测出 pϕ(c∣x)p_\phi(\mathbf{c}|x)pϕ​(c∣x) 的位置。为验证这一猜想，我们在表 6 中做了几组消融：(i) 随机初始化 θ\thetaθ；(ii) 不优化 θ\thetaθ，而是直接优化条件变量 c\mathbf{c}c。两种情况下结果都明显差于优化预训练的判别式模型。

English original
Diffusion-TTA optimizes pre-trained discriminative and generative models using a generative diffusion loss. A trivial solution to this optimization could be the discriminative model fθf_\thetafθ​ overfitting to the generative loss and thus predicting precisely pϕ(c∣x)p_\phi (\mathbf{c}|x)pϕ​(c∣x). Empirically we find that this does not happen: Diffusion-TTA consistently outperforms Diffusion Classifier [27], as shown in Table 7. We conjecture that the reason why the discriminative model does not overfit to the generative loss is because of the weight initialization of the (pre-trained) discriminative model, which prevents it from converging to this trivial solution. For instance, if the predictions of the generative and discriminative model are too far from one another that is: distance(pϕ(c∣x),pθ(c∣x))\textrm{distance}(p_\phi (\mathbf{c}|x), p_\theta(\mathbf{c}|x))distance(pϕ​(c∣x),pθ​(c∣x)) is too high, then it becomes very difficult to optimize θ\thetaθ to reach a location such that it predicts pϕ(c∣x)p_\phi (\mathbf{c}|x)pϕ​(c∣x). To verify our conjecture, we run a few ablations in Table 6, i) We randomly initialize θ\thetaθ, ii) Instead of optimizing θ\thetaθ we directly optimize the conditioning variable c\mathbf{c}c. In both cases, we find that results are significantly worse than when optimizing the pre-trained discriminative model.

Diffusion-TTA 用判别式模型的输出去调制图像扩散模型的任务条件。这让我们能够直接使用现成的预训练模型，但也使我们无法在生成部分与判别部分之间建立更紧密的整合。探索这种整合是未来工作的一个直接方向。目前生成模型学的是 p(x∣y)p(x|y)p(x∣y)，其中 xxx 是输入图像。为不同层训练不同的条件生成模型，例如 p(l1∣y)p(l1|y)p(l1∣y) 或 p(l1∣l2)p(l1|l2)p(l1∣l2)（l1l1l1、l2l2l2 是不同层的特征），可能提供更丰富的学习信号，值得研究。最后，Diffusion-TTA 相当慢，因为必须采样多个时间步才能得到扩散损失的良好估计。用一致性模型（Consistency Models）[43] 提升测试时适配速度，是一个直接的未来方向。

English original
Diffusion-TTA modulates the task conditioning of an image diffusion model with the output of the discriminative model. While this allows us to use readily available pre-trained models it also prevents us from having a tighter integration between the generative and the discriminative parts. Exploring this integration is a direct avenue of future work. Currently the generative model learns p(x∣y)p(x|y)p(x∣y), where xxx is the input image. Training different conditional generative model for different layers such as p(l1∣y)p(l1|y)p(l1∣y) or p(l1∣l2)p(l1|l2)p(l1∣l2), where l1l1l1 and l2l2l2 are features at different layers, could provide diverse learning signals and it is worth studying. Finally, Diffusion-TTA is significantly slow as we have to sample multiple timesteps to get a good estimate of the diffusion loss. Using Consistency Models [43] to improve the test-time-adaptation speed is a direct avenue for future work.

5 结论

English original
5 Conclusion

我们提出了 Diffusion-TTA，一种利用预训练扩散模型给出的生成式反馈来改进预训练判别式模型（分类器、分割器和深度预测器）的测试时适配方法。适配针对测试集中的每一个样本进行，做法是把扩散似然的梯度反向传播到判别式模型的权重上。我们展示本模型优于此前最先进的 TTA 方法，并且在多种判别式与生成式扩散模型变体上都有效。「视觉感知即生成模型反演」这一假说在该领域早期就已被 pursued [33, 53]。当今生成模型惊人的性能，以及视觉皮层中前馈通路与自上而下通路的存在 [15]，促使我们重新审视这一范式。我们希望这项工作能激发该方向上的进一步研究。

English original
We introduced Diffusion-TTA, a test time adaptation method that uses generative feedback from a pre-trained diffusion model to improve pre-trained discriminative models, such as classifiers, segmenters and depth predictors. Adaptation is carried out for each example in the test-set by backpropagating diffusion likelihood gradients to the discriminative model weights. We show that our model outperforms previous state-of-the-art TTA methods, and that it is effective across multiple discriminative and generative diffusion model variants. The hypothesis of visual perception as inversion of a generative model has been pursued from early years in the field [33, 53]. The formidable performance of today’s generative models and the presence of feed-forward and top-down pathways in the visual cortex [15] urges us to revisit this paradigm. We hope that our work stimulates further research efforts in this direction.

我们在图像分类任务上比较 Diffusion-TTA 与 Diffusion Classifier [27] 的计算延迟。Diffusion Classifier [27] 是一种离散标签搜索方法，需要对数据集中的每一个类别都做一次扩散模型前向，计算时间随类别数线性增长。相比之下，Diffusion-TTA 是一种基于梯度的优化方法，通过更新模型权重来最大化图像似然；其计算速度取决于适配步数和模型规模，而与类别数无关。如图 8 所示，当类别数较少时我们的方法不如 Diffusion Classifier 高效，但类别数增多后就更高效。值得注意的是，像 Diffusion Classifier 这样的离散标签搜索方法不适用于在线适配，对于稠密标注任务也不可行（在像素标注上做搜索，复杂度是图像像素数的指数级）。

In contrast, Diffusion-TTA is a gradient-based optimization method that updates model weights to maximize the image likelihood. The computational speed depends on the number of adaptation steps and the model size, not the number of categories. 对单张图像做测试时适配时，我们在每个适配步随机采样 180 组不同的噪声 ϵ\epsilonϵ 与时间步 ttt 对，组成批大小为 180 的 mini-batch。时间步 ttt 在 1 到 1000 的范围内按均匀分布采样，ϵ\epsilonϵ 从单位高斯分布采样。我们对每张输入图像做 5 步测试时适配。优化器采用随机梯度下降（SGD）（或 Adam 优化器 [24]），学习率、权重衰减与动量分别设为 0.005（或 0.00001）、0 和 0.9。为了把「因扩散模型而带来的提升」单独分离出来，我们在测试时适配过程中不使用任何形式的数据增广。

English original
For test-time-adaptation of individual images, we randomly sample 180 different pairs of noise ϵ\epsilonϵ and timestep ttt for each adaptation step, composing a mini-batch of size 180. Timestep ttt is sampled over an uniform distribution from the range 1 to 1000 and epsilon ϵ\epsilonϵ is sampled from an unit gaussian. We apply 5 test-time adaptation steps for each input image. We adopt Stochastic Gradient Descent (SGD) (or Adam optimizer [24]), and set learning rate, weight decay and momentum to 0.005 (or 0.00001), 0, and 0.9, respectively. To isolate the contribution of the improvement due to the diffusion model we do not use any form of data augmentation during test-time adaptation.

我们使用 Stable Diffusion v2.0 [40] 来适配 CLIP 模型。对 CLIP 分类器，我们遵循其标准预处理：把图像缩放并中心裁剪到 224×224224 \times 224224×224 像素。对 Stable Diffusion，我们把图像缩放到 512×512512 \times 512512×512 像素，这是 Stable Diffusion 模型的标准图像尺寸。对 Stable Diffusion 中的 CLIP 文本编码器与分类器，我们使用文本提示 "a photo of a <class_name>"，其中 <class_name> 是数据集中给出的类别标签名称。

We use Stable Diffusion v2.0 [40] to adapt CLIP models. For the CLIP classifier, we follow their standard preprocessing step where we resize and center-crop to a resolution of 224×224224 \times 224224×224 pixels. For Stable Diffusion, we process the images by resizing them to a resolution of 512×512512 \times 512512×512 pixels which is the standard image size in Stable Diffusion models. For the CLIP text encoder in Stable Diffusion and the classifier we use the text prompt of "a photo of a <class_name>", where <class_name> is the name of the class label as mentioned in the dataset.
