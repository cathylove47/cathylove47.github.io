面向分布偏移的不确定性估计：用生成先验求解逆问题†

Towards Distribution-Shift Uncertainty Estimation for Inverse Problems with Generative Priors

Namhoon Kim · Sara Fridovich-Keil（佐治亚理工学院 电气与计算机工程学院）

生成先验能从极少的测量里恢复图像，但当病人不在先验的训练分布里时，它也会自信地幻觉。这篇短文提出一个不用校准集、可对任何计算成像逆问题计算的实例级指标：同一物体在不同随机测量子集下重建得越不稳定，就越可能是分布外。

读之前，先分清三件事

这一段是译者注，不是原文。论文很短，但三个概念撑起了全部论证，先摆清楚再读会省力。

  1. 稀疏视角 CT 的逆问题：测量写成 y=Ax⋆+ϵy = A x^{\star} + \epsilony=Ax⋆+ϵ，其中 AAA 是 Radon 变换（把图像变成投影正弦图），x⋆x^{\star}x⋆ 是待重建的衰减图。稀疏视角把投影角度的数量压到 11/22/33 个，于是测量总数少于图像像素数，问题严重欠定，必须靠先验补。
  2. 先验怎么进来：近端梯度法每一步先做一次数据保真下降（沿 A⊤(Ax−y)A^{\top}(Ax-y)A⊤(Ax−y) 走一小步），再把结果送进近端算子 fθf_{\theta}fθ​。本文的 fθf_{\theta}fθ​ 是一个「学习近端网络」（LPN）：它被参数化成某个输入凸网络 ψθ\psi_\thetaψθ​ 的梯度，因此天然就是某个隐式正则项的近端算子——先验不是外加的正则，而是网络本身。
  3. 指标怎么算：对同一幅图、同一批可用测量，随机抽 10 个子集，各重建一次，再看这 10 幅重建在每个像素上的标准差。分布内的图，先验能把它「钉住」，10 次结果彼此接近；分布外的图，先验会往训练分布拉，而拉的方向随测量子集变化，于是抖动变大。整个过程不需要任何外部校准集，也不需要重训练。

摘要

English original
Abstract

生成模型作为数据驱动先验，在求解逆问题（例如从欠采样测量重建医学图像）时已显示出很强的潜力。这类数据驱动先验能在减少所需测量数的同时提升重建质量，但当待重建图像落在用于训练该先验的图像分布之外时，它们也带来了幻觉的风险。这一设定下已有的不确定性量化方法：(i) 需要一个分布内的校准数据集，而它未必随手可得；(ii) 给出的是启发式而非统计意义上的不确定性估计；或者 (iii) 量化的是模型过参数化或测量有限所带来的不确定性，而不是分布偏移所带来的不确定性。我们提出一个实例级、无需校准集的不确定性指标：它对分布偏移敏感，不需要关于训练分布的任何先验知识，也不产生重训练开销。具体而言，我们假设分布内图像的重建，相对随机测量的变化会比分布外图像的重建更稳定，并且可以把这种稳定性当作检测分布偏移的代理。该不确定性指标对计算成像中的任意逆问题都能高效计算；我们用在 MNIST 数字断层重建上的初步实验来演示它，其中的生成先验是一个学习近端网络，只用数字“0”训练，却在全部十个数字上评估。这些实验表明，我们的不确定性指标——不同测量子集所得重建之间的高度差异——确实对分布外（OOD）数字给出比分布内数字更高的不确定性。此外，这种更高的不确定性准确地预示了我们在这些 OOD 数字上观察到的更高重建误差。我们的结果提示了一种部署策略：把生成先验与轻量级护栏配对，从而对分布内图像实现激进的测量削减，同时在生成先验运行于分布之外时自动发出警告。代码见 https://github.com/voilalab/uncertainty_quantification_LPN。

English original
Generative models have shown strong potential for use as data-driven priors in solving inverse problems, such as reconstructing medical images from undersampled measurements. Although these data-driven priors can improve reconstruction quality while reducing the number of required measurements, they also introduce the risk of hallucination when the image to be reconstructed falls outside the distribution of images used to train the data-driven prior. Existing approaches to uncertainty quantification in this setting (i) require an in-distribution calibration dataset, which may not be readily available, (ii) provide heuristic rather than statistical uncertainty estimates, or (iii) quantify uncertainty arising from model overparameterization or limited measurements rather than uncertainty arising from distribution shift. We propose an instance-level, calibration-set-free uncertainty indicator that is sensitive to distribution shift, requires no prior knowledge of the training distribution, and incurs no retraining cost. Specifically, we posit that reconstructions of in-distribution images will be more stable with respect to variation in random measurements compared to reconstructions of out-of-distribution images, and that we can use this stability as a proxy for detecting distribution shift. This uncertainty indicator is efficiently computable for any inverse problem in computational imaging; we demonstrate it with preliminary experiments on tomographic reconstruction of MNIST digits, where the generative prior is a learned proximal network trained only on digit “0” and evaluated on all ten digits. These experiments show that our uncertainty indicator, high variation among reconstructions from different measurement subsets, indeed shows higher uncertainty for out-of-distribution (OOD) digits compared to in-distribution digits. Moreover, this higher uncertainty accurately predicts the higher reconstruction error we observe for these OOD digits. Our results motivate a deployment strategy that pairs generative priors with lightweight guardrails, to enable aggressive measurement reduction in computational imaging for in distribution images while automatically warning when the generative prior is operating out of distribution. Code is available at https://github.com/voilalab/uncertainty_quantification_LPN.

1 引言

English original
1 Introduction

从严重欠采样的测量中重建图像，是许多科学与临床成像模态的核心问题。例如在 X 射线计算机断层成像（CT）中，测量数量受限，因为每增加一个投影都会增加致癌的辐射剂量 [1]；在磁共振成像（MRI）中，每次测量采集都很慢，这会加剧运动伪影并限制可成像的患者数量 [2]。学习到的生成先验如今提供了一个有吸引力的补救办法：通过嵌入关于「哪些图像是合理的」这一强数据驱动假设，它们能从通常测量量的一小部分——有时少一个数量级——中恢复高质量重建，从而有望实现更快、更安全、更可及的成像 [3]。

English original
Reconstructing images from severely undersampled measurements lies at the heart of many scientific and clinical imaging modalities. For example, in X-ray computed tomography (CT) measurements are limited because each additional projection increases carcinogenic radiation dose [1]; in magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) each measurement is slow to collect, increasing motion blur and limiting the number of patients who can be imaged [2]. Learned generative priors now offer a compelling remedy: by embedding strong, data-driven assumptions about plausible images, they can recover high-quality reconstructions from a fraction of the usual measurements—sometimes an order of magnitude fewer—promising faster, safer, and more accessible imaging [3].

然而，这些收益附带一个假设：未见过的患者必须与先验训练所用图像相似。当该假设不成立时——例如医院换了新扫描仪、服务的人群不同，或遇到罕见病理——先验就可能幻觉出结构上看似合理但临床上错误的细节 [4]。检测这类分布偏移至关重要。遗憾的是，如今的不确定性量化（UQ）工具包给出的解决方案并不完美。保形预测是统计上严格保证的金标准，但它需要从新分布中抽取一个小校准集 [5]–[10]，而这在部署时往往无法获得。基于自助法启发式 [11]–[14] 或集成 [15]–[20] 的替代方案，要么牺牲统计有效性，要么度量了错误的不确定性来源（例如模型容量，而不是分布不匹配）。

English original
However, these benefits come with an assumption: the unseen patient must resemble the images on which the prior was trained. When that assumption fails—because a hospital acquired a new scanner, serves a different population, or encounters a rare pathology—the prior can hallucinate structurally plausible but clinically erroneous details [4]. Detecting such distribution shifts is critical. Unfortunately, today’s uncertainty quantification (UQ) toolkits offer an imperfect solution. Conformal prediction, the gold standard for statistically rigorous guarantees, requires a small calibration set drawn from the new distribution [5]–[10], which is often unavailable at deployment time. Alternatives based on bootstrap heuristics [11]–[14] or ensembling [15]–[20] either sacrifice statistical validity or measure the wrong source of uncertainty (e.g. model capacity rather than distribution mismatch).

我们认为，医学与计算成像邀请一种更简单、无需校准集的视角。每个重建任务天然提供同一物体的多次测量，例如 CT 中测得的不同投影角度。把这些测量当作内部校准集，即使不存在外部校准数据，我们也能构造实例级的 UQ 信号。对一个固定的测量集合，我们随机抽取其子集，并用每个子集分别做一次图像重建。如果未知的成像目标对生成先验而言是分布内的，我们预期来自这些不同测量子集的重建彼此一致，且与真值一致。反之，如果未知成像目标是分布外的，先验可能把重建拉离真值，导致不同测量子集所得重建之间出现更大的差异。因此我们假设：不同测量子集重建图像之间的差异，可以作为检测分布偏移的代理。

English original
We argue that medical and computational imaging invites a simpler, calibration-set-free perspective. Each reconstruction task naturally supplies multiple measurements of the same object, for example the different projection angles measured in CT. By treating these measurements as an internal calibration set, we can build instance-level UQ signals even when no external calibration data exist. For a fixed set of measurements, we randomly subsample subsets of these measurements and perform image reconstruction separately with each subset. If the unknown imaging target is in distribution for the generative prior, we expect the reconstructions from these different subsets of measurements to be consistent with each other and with the ground truth. However, if the unknown imaging target is out of distribution, the prior may pull the reconstruction away from the ground truth, leading to greater variations between reconstructions from different measurement subsets. We therefore hypothesize that variation between images reconstructed from different measurement subsets can serve as a proxy to detect distribution shift.

我们在一个玩具设定中检验该假设：从模拟的稀疏视角 CT 投影重建 MNIST [21] 数字。我们的生成先验是一个在数字“0”上训练的学习近端网络（LPN）[22]，却在全部十个数字上评估，因此数字“1”到“9”都是分布外的；见图 1。我们通过实验验证：(i) 与分布外数字的重建相比，分布内数字由不同投影角度子集得到的重建方差更低；(ii) 当测量最受限时该效应最明显，因为在这种设定下重建最依赖生成先验。

English original
We test this hypothesis in a toy setting of reconstructing MNIST [21] digits from simulated sparse-view CT projections. Our generative prior is a learned proximal network (LPN) [22] trained on digit “0” and evaluated on all ten digits, so that digits “1” through “9” are out of distribution; see Figure 1. We validate empirically that (i) reconstructions of in-distribution digits from different subsets of projection angles have lower variance compared to reconstructions of out of distribution digits, and (ii) this effect is most pronounced when measurements are most limited, as in this setting the reconstruction relies most on the generative prior.

这些观察提示了一套在计算成像中部署生成先验的方案：把激进的测量削减与轻量级护栏结合起来，以防止分布外幻觉。预训练的生成先验提供样本效率；护栏——对每次扫描在不同测量子集之间做简单的交叉验证——在先前偏离其训练分布时自动发出警告，提示临床医生或用户补采测量，或改用更保守的重建方法。

English original
These observations suggest a deployment recipe for generative priors in computational imaging that couples aggressive measurement reduction with lightweight guardrails to protect against out of distribution hallucination. The pretrained generative prior provides sample efficiency; the guardrail—simple cross-validation between measurement subsets for each scan—issues an automatic warning whenever the prior strays beyond its training distribution, prompting clinicians or users to acquire additional measurements or switch to a more conservative reconstruction method.

2.1 稀疏视角计算机断层成像（CT）

English original
2.1 Sparse-View Computed Tomography (CT)

CT 是一种非侵入式成像技术，它利用从多个角度采集的 X 射线投影来生成物体的横截面图像 [23], [24]。

English original
CT is a non-invasive imaging technique that creates cross-sectional images of an object by using X-ray projections collected from multiple angles [23], [24].

稀疏视角 CT 有意限制投影角度的数量，以降低患者接受的辐射剂量。然而，减少投影角度会使逆问题严重欠定，因为测量总数（角度数 ×\times× 探测器分辨率）变得小于待重建的图像像素或体素数量。在这种情况下，滤波反投影（FBP）等传统重建方法会产生被条纹伪影破坏的低质量图像 [1]。

English original
Sparse-view CT deliberately limits the number of projection angles to reduce the patient's radiation dose. However, reducing the number of projection angles leads to a severely underdetermined inverse problem, as the total number of measurements (angles ×\times× detector resolution) becomes smaller than the number of image pixels or voxels to be reconstructed. Under these conditions, traditional reconstruction methods like Filtered Back-Projection (FBP) produce low-quality images corrupted by streak artifacts [1].

这些伪影可以通过对重建图像引入结构先验来去除：一种是显式几何约束，例如低全变差 [25]；另一种是更近期的方法——利用在其训练图像中学到复杂结构的强数据驱动生成模型 [26]。

English original
These artifacts can be removed by introducing a structural prior on the reconstructed image, either with an explicit geometric constraint such as low total variation [25], or more recently by leveraging strong data-driven generative models that learn complex structure in their training images [26].

然而，这些数据驱动先验对分布偏移敏感，在分布外图像上可能产生看起来逼真的伪影（幻觉）[4]。

English original
However, these data-driven priors are sensitive to distribution shift, and can produce realistic-looking artifacts (hallucinations) on out of distribution images [4].

2.2 学习先验中的不确定性与分布偏移

English original
2.2 Uncertainty and Distribution Shift in Learned Priors

不确定性量化方法的目标是检测分布偏移，并就生成先验可能产生的幻觉向用户发出警告。

English original
Uncertainty quantification methods aim to detect distribution shifts and warn users of potential hallucination from generative priors.

基于校准的保形方法。保形预测方法利用一个小的、分布匹配的校准数据集，给出统计上严格的有限样本置信区间。在成像领域，这类方法涵盖：通过采集更多测量来收紧置信区间的任务驱动流程 [5]；产生逐像素或掩码区域置信区间的方法 [6], [9]；面向扩散模型的特定风险控制 [8], [7]；以及分布偏移下的图像分诊 [10]。然而，这些保形预测方法都预设可以获得来自新的（已偏移）分布的校准数据，这限制了它们在首次遭遇 OOD 检测时的实用性。

English original
Calibration-based conformal methods. Conformal prediction methods leverage a small, distribution-matched calibration dataset to produce statistically rigorous finite-sample confidence intervals. In imaging, these methods span task-driven pipelines that tighten confidence intervals by acquiring more measurements [5], methods that produce pixelwise or masked-region confidence intervals [6], [9], diffusion-specific risk control [8], [7], and distribution-shift image triage [10]. However, these conformal prediction methods presuppose access to calibration data from the new (shifted) distribution, which limits their utility for first-encounter OOD detection.

合成测量自助法与物理感知方法。若干 UQ 方法提出绕过真实校准数据集，改从假定或近似的物理模型中采样一个合成校准集。例如，[11] 从初始重建中重采样合成 CT 投影，并通过假设在 CT 零空间上具有等变性，来纠正经典参数自助法的过度自信。对于由偏微分方程（PDE）支配的物理系统，若干方法提出确定性物理代理模型，并配合潜空间中的不确定性演化 [12], [13], [14]。这些技术虽然不需要分布匹配的校准数据集，但会继承物理代理模型或合成模型中的偏差与误差，而且对所得到的置信区间缺乏严格的有效性保证。

English original
Synthetic–measurement bootstraps and physics-aware methods. Several UQ methods propose to bypass a true calibration dataset by sampling a synthetic calibration dataset from an assumed or approximate physical model. For example, [11] resample synthetic CT projections from an initial reconstruction, and correct the overconfidence of classical parametric bootstrap by assuming equivariance across the CT nullspace. For physical systems governed by partial differential equations (PDEs), several methods propose deterministic physics surrogates together with latent-space uncertainty evolution, [12], [13], [14] While these techniques do not require a distributionally matched calibration dataset, they inherit any bias or errors in the physics surrogate or the synthetic model, and lack rigorous guarantees of validity for the resulting confidence intervals.

贝叶斯与集成方法。从显式或隐式后验分布中采样——在学习到的先验之上使用马尔可夫链蒙特卡洛（MCMC）采样 [15], [16] 或其随机梯度变体（SG-MCMC）[17], [18]——可以刻画因测量有限而产生的认知不确定性，但忽略了分布不匹配带来的不确定性。基于集成的近似 [19], [20] 能够刻画已训练模型权重上的不确定性，但集成中的所有成员共享同一份训练数据，因此在重建 OOD 图像时可能一起失效。

English original
Bayesian and ensemble methods. Sampling from an explicit or implicit posterior distribution—using Markov Chain Monte Carlo (MCMC) sampling [15], [16] or its stochastic gradient variant (SG-MCMC) [17], [18] over a learned prior—captures epistemic uncertainty due to limited measurements but ignores uncertainty due to distribution mismatch. Ensemble-based approximations [19], [20] can capture uncertainty over trained model weights, but all members of the ensemble share the same training data and can therefore fail in unison when reconstructing OOD images.

4 Experiments

我们首先比较 LPN 在稀疏视角 CT 中对分布内图像与 OOD 图像所达到的重建质量。在图 2 中，我们画出每个数字 100 次重建的 PSNR 与 SSIM 的均值与范围（最小值–最大值），其中对每个数字所选 10 张 MNIST 图像各有 10 个随机测量种子。我们把使用学习先验（在数字“0”上训练）的近端方法重建，与标准的无正则 FBP 基线比较。结果表明，学习先验即使对分布外数字也有益，但对分布内数字有效得多，而且随着测量角度数减少，这种优势尤为明显。

We begin by comparing the reconstruction quality an LPN achieves for sparse-view CT of in-distribution versus OOD images. In Figure 2 we plot the mean and range (min–max) PSNR and SSIM of the 100 reconstructions for each digit, including 10 random measurement seeds for each of the 10 MNIST images selected for each digit. We compare reconstructions from the proximal method with our learned prior (trained on digit “0”) against a standard unregularized FBP baseline. The results indicate that the learned prior is beneficial even for out of distribution digits, but much more effective for in-distribution digits, and especially so as the number of measurement angles is reduced.

我们定性地（图 3）和定量地（图 4）评估所提出的分布偏移指标，即不同测量所得重建之间的差异。如果目标图像处于先验网络学到的分布内，我们预期即使随机测量角度集合变化，它也能给出一致的预测。相反，如果目标图像对先验而言是分布外的，我们预期随机测量角度变化时重建的方差更高。我们发现的确如此：重建在随机测量上的方差可以检测分布偏移。该效应在测量角度数很少时最明显，这与学习先验对重建影响最大、因而分布偏移风险最大的情形一致。

English original
We evaluate our proposed distribution shift metric, variation across reconstructions from different measurements, qualitatively in Figure 3 and quantitatively in Figure 4. If the target image is in the distribution learned by the prior network, we expect it to produce consistent predictions even as the set of random measurement angles changes. In contrast, if the target image is out of distribution for the prior, we expect higher variance of the reconstructions when the random measurement angles change. This is exactly what we find: reconstruction variance over random measurements detects distribution shift. The effect is most prominent when the number of measurement angles is small, which aligns with the setting when the learned prior has the most influence on the reconstruction and thus distribution shift poses the greatest risk.

5 讨论

English original
5 Discussion

生成模型作为数据驱动先验，在求解 CT 重建等逆问题中展现出很大前景，可提升图像质量并减少测量。然而，当目标图像与训练先验所用的分布不同时，数据驱动先验在分布偏移下存在幻觉风险。本文验证了一个简单的假设：这种分布偏移脆弱性可以在不带来大量计算或数据采集负担的情况下被检测出来——方法是评估重建在可用测量的随机子集之间有多一致。我们的工作提示了一种简单策略：持续采集额外测量，直到重建稳定性跨过期望阈值，从而检测并缓解分布偏移。

English original
Generative models have shown great promise as data-driven priors in solving inverse problems like CT reconstruction, enhancing image quality and reducing measurements. However, data-driven priors pose risks of hallucination under distribution shift, when the target image differs from the distribution used to train the prior. Here we validate the simple hypothesis that this distribution shift fragility can be detected without extensive computational or data-collection burden, by evaluating how consistent the reconstruction is across random subsets of the available measurements. Our work suggests a simple strategy to detect and mitigate distribution shift by collecting additional measurements until reconstruction stability crosses a desired threshold.

局限与未来工作。尽管我们提出的分布偏移不确定性估计器广泛适用于各类逆问题，我们的初步实验验证仍是初步的，且局限在以学习近端网络作为数据驱动先验、对 MNIST 数字做断层重建的玩具设定。未来工作的一个高优先级事项，是在不同成像逆问题中具有实际意义的多样的数据集上、以及在包括扩散模型在内的多样数据驱动先验下，评估我们提出的不确定性指标的潜力。我们也承认，可能存在我们所提出的不确定性指标与分布偏移相关性不准的情形。例如，一张本身属于分布内、但比训练数据更含噪的图像，可能被我们的指标错误地标记为 OOD。反过来，如果图像中属于 OOD 的部分对测量没有贡献（即分布偏移与前向模型相关），我们的指标就无从把它检测为 OOD。处理这类情形同样是未来工作的高优先级事项。最后，我们鼓励未来工作对我们提出的 OOD 不确定性指标进行统计分析，以构建有效且稳健的置信区间，并就应使用多少（组）测量给出指导，从而获得最准确的 OOD 检测。

English original
Limitations and future work. Though our proposed distribution shift uncertainty estimator is broadly applicable across inverse problems, our initial experimental validation is preliminary and limited to the toy setting of tomographic reconstruction of MNIST digits using a learned proximal network as data-driven prior. It is a high priority for future work to evaluate the potential of our proposed uncertainty metric on diverse datasets of practical significance in different imaging inverse problems, and with diverse data-driven priors including diffusion models. We also acknowledge that there may be settings in which our proposed uncertainty indicator may not correlate accurately with distribution shift. For example, an otherwise in-distribution image that is noisier than the training dataset may be erroneously flagged as OOD by our metric. Conversely, if the portion of an image that is OOD does not contribute to the measurements (i.e. if the distribution shift is correlated with the forward model), our metric would have no way to detect it as OOD. Addressing cases like these is also a high priority for future work. Finally, we encourage future work to consider statistical analysis of our proposed OOD uncertainty metric, to build valid and robust confidence intervals and offer guidance on how many (sets of) measurements to employ for the most accurate OOD detection.
