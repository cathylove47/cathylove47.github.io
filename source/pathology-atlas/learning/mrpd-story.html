硬 DC / 软 CG

RPM

Abstract

预训练于自然图像的大规模隐扩散模型（large latent diffusion model, LLDM）所含的隐式视觉知识十分丰富，并且被假设对自然图像与医学图像普遍适用。为从实践角度检验这一假设，我们提出了一个用于欠采样 MRI 重建的新框架——通过提示大规模隐扩散模型实现欠采样 MRI 重建（MRPD）。现有方法多在 MRI 数据集上训练，对不同数据采集场景的泛化能力通常有限；MRPD 则支持无监督且普遍自适应的 MRI 重建。在无监督重建中，MRSampler 以随机相位调制的「由硬到软」控制引导 LLDM。给定任意单来源或多来源 MRI 数据集，MRPD 的性能都会因一个仅微调 LLDM 自编码器的轻量 MRAdapter 而得到普遍提升。在 FastMRI 与 IXI 上的实验表明，MRPD 是唯一同时支持「无 MRI 数据库」与「有 MRI 数据库」两种场景的模型，并在域外（out-of-domain, OOD）采样、对比度与器官上，于所比较的无监督、监督与 MRI 扩散方法中取得最佳泛化性。据我们所知，MRPD 是第一个在实证上展示「在海量自然图像上预训练的 LLDM 对 MRI 具有普遍效力」的方法。我们的官方实现见 https://github.com/Z7Gao/MRPD。

English original
Implicit visual knowledge in a large latent diffusion model (LLDM) pre-trained on natural images is rich and hypothetically universal to natural and medical images. To test this hypothesis from a practical perspective, we propose a novel framework for undersampled MRI Reconstruction by Prompting a large latent Diffusion model (MRPD). While the existing methods trained on MRI datasets are typically of limited generalizability toward diverse data acquisition scenarios, MRPD supports unsupervised and universally adaptive MRI reconstruction. For unsupervised reconstruction, MRSampler guides LLDM with a random-phase-modulated hard-to-soft control. With any single- or multiple-source MRI dataset, MRPD's performance is boosted universally by a lightweight MRAdapter that only finetunes the LLDM's autoencoder. Experiments on FastMRI and IXI show that MRPD is the only model that supports both MRI database-free and database-available scenarios and attains the best generalizability towards out-of-domain (OOD) samplings, contrasts, and organs among compared unsupervised, supervised, and MRI diffusion methods. To our knowledge, MRPD is the first method that empirically shows the universal prowess of an LLDM pre-trained on vast natural images for MRI. Our official implementation is at https://github.com/Z7Gao/MRPD.

Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) is a cornerstone in clinical diagnostics renowned for its safety. 2.1 Diffusion Models

扩散模型（DM）（Ho 等，2020；Dhariwal 与 Nichol，2021；Song 等，2021）是一类无条件生成方法，它把高斯分布变换为经验数据分布。前向过程是一个马尔可夫过程：按照固定的噪声计划逐步向干净图像 x0x_0x0​ 加入高斯噪声，在第 ttt 步得到中间含噪的 xtx_txt​。逆向过程则使用一个预训练的神经网络，即去噪函数，逐步对 xtx_txt​ 去噪。为降低计算负担，人们提出了隐扩散模型（LDM）（Rombach 等，2022）：它先把数据嵌入维度更低的隐空间，再学习去噪函数。LLDM（Rombach 等，2022；Ramesh 等，2021；Saharia 等，2022）是在互联网规模数据集上训练的一类 LDM。图 1 展示了 Stable Diffusion（SD）（Rombach 等，2022）v1.5 的组成：它以 CLIP（Radford 等，2021）作文本编码器，在由 585 万自然图像-文本对组成的 LAION-5B 数据集（Schuhmann 等，2022）上训练自编码器 VQGAN（Esser 等，2021）与隐图像生成器（去噪 UNet）。隐式视觉知识存在于去噪 UNet 中。

English original
Diffusion model (DM) (Ho et al., 2020; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021; Song et al., 2021) is a class of unconditional generative methods that transform a Gaussian distribution into an empirical data distribution. The forward process is a Markovian process that gradually adds Gaussian noise with a fixed schedule to a clean image x0x_0x0​ and gets intermediate noisy xtx_txt​ in the ttt step. The reverse process involves using a pre-trained neural network, i.e. denoising function, to gradually denoise xtx_txt​. To reduce the computational burden, the latent diffusion model (LDM) (Rombach et al., 2022) is proposed. It embeds data in a lower-dimension latent space before learning the denoising function. LLDM (Rombach et al., 2022; Ramesh et al., 2021; Saharia et al., 2022) is a class of LDM trained on Internet-scale dataset. We demonstrate the composition of Stable Diffusion (SD) (Rombach et al., 2022) v1.5 in Fig. 1. It has CLIP (Radford et al., 2021) as its text encoder, trains an autoencoder, VQGAN (Esser et al., 2021), and a latent image generator, denoising UNet, on the LAION-5B dataset (Schuhmann et al., 2022) consisting of 5.85M natural image-text pairs. The implicit visual knowledge lies in the denoising UNet.

3.3 普遍自适应的 MRAdapter

English original
3.3 Universally Adaptive MRAdapter

我们不提出一个静态的无监督模型，而是发现 LLDM 允许构建一个面向欠采样 MRI 重建的自适应模型，使任意给定的 MRI 数据都能普遍提升性能。一种直接的做法是用现有最先进的 LLDM 微调技术（例如 ControlNet（Zhang 等，2023））调整去噪 UNet。然而，适配数据先验既计算低效，又不可避免地损害 LLDM 的泛化性。相反，我们观察到 MRSampler 的像素域软、硬约束都会经过自编码器——一个带来信息损失的有损图像压缩器。因此我们提出 MRAdapter，只适配自编码器的一部分，仅占整个 LLDM 参数的 0.2%（图 1(c)）。具体而言，给定任意 MR 图像，我们用 mathrmL2mathrm{L}_2mathrmL2​ 损失微调自编码器的首层与末层。MRI 图像取幅度、归一化到 [-1,1]，并复制三份以对齐自编码器接口。这些卷积变成两组图像滤波器：起始处的 MR→自然图像滤波用于把输入适配到 LLDM，末端的自然→MR 图像滤波用于精修 MRI 输出。MRAdapter 在提升 MRI 重建的同时保持了生成式基础模型的泛化性。给定任意单来源或多来源 MRI 数据集，MRAdapter 都能普遍提升重建，5.3 节对此进行了讨论与评估。

However, adapting the data prior is both computationally inefficient and inevitably harmful to LLDM's generalizability. LLDM

Consequently, any given undersampled complex-valued k-space slice is first transformed into the image domain with inverse Fourier Transform, normalized, and duplicated the magnitude into a 3-channel input to LLDM. 首先，传统监督模型需要针对每一种具体成像场景分别训练与部署，这限制了它们在真实世界的临床应用（CMRxRecon，2024）。因此，可泛化的通用模型（Ouyang 等，2019；Wang 等，2023；Dar 等，2020；Liu 等，2021）对于在 MRI 设备上高效部署非常可取。在所有对比方法中，MRPD 对不同采样模式、器官与对比度的泛化最好，并能通过微调普遍提升性能。其次，在许多实际成像场景中获取全采样数据集不可行（Yaman 等，2020），包括实时成像与术中 MRI（Mislow 等，2010）——那里采集到的本就是高度欠采样的图像。即便数据可得，出于数据隐私问题，把不同医院异构的真实 MRI 数据聚合起来也不可行（Wang 等，2023）。MRPD 以「无数据库」（ours.A）与「有数据库」（ours.B）两个版本，以及在高 R 下的优越性能，恰好契合这些真实场景。最后，用现成的扩散加速技术加速 MRPD 是可行的。表 IX 展示了我们的方法在单线圈 FastMRI（R=4×\times×）上配合 DDIM 加速（Song 等，2021）的性能与推理时间。当 T=500 时，我们的方法被加速 2×2\times2×，同时保持不错的性能。利用最先进的 MRI 扩散加速技术（Chung 等，2022b；Ozturkler 等，2023b；Cao 等，2024）实现更快的推理同样有前景。

English original
Firstly, conventional supervised models require training and deployment for each specific imaging scenario, limiting their clinical application in the real world (CMRxRecon, 2024). As a result, generalizable universal models (Ouyang et al., 2019; Wang et al., 2023; Dar et al., 2020; Liu et al., 2021) are highly desirable for efficient deployment on MRI machines. Among all compared methods, MRPD generalizes the best towards different sampling patterns, organs, and contrasts and improves the performance universally with finetuning. Secondly, acquiring fully sampled datasets is infeasible in many practical imaging scenarios (Yaman et al., 2020), including real-time imaging and intraoperative MRI (Mislow et al., 2010), where highly undersampled images are acquired. Even available, aggregating heterogeneous realistic MRI data from various hospitals is infeasible for data privacy issues (Wang et al., 2023). MRPD suits well for those real-world scenarios with both database-free (ours.A) and database-available (ours.B) versions and the superior performance at high R's. Lastly, it is viable to accelerate MRPD with off-the-shelf diffusion acceleration techniques. At T=500, our method is accelerated for 2×2\times2× while maintaining a decent performance. 6.2 RPM 的原理

English original
6.2 The Rationale of RPM

与 DiffuseRecon（Peng 等，2022）和 Resample（Song 等，2024）类似，我们发现了在重建时对中间图像引入随机性的好处，并提出 RPM：把原始图像相位与随机相位组合，从而向 MR 图像注入一种可分离的噪声。在表 VI 中我们发现，加入的原始相位越多，重建越差。同时，图 5(a) 可视化了我们方法在无 RPM 时（对应 λ=0\lambda=0λ=0）的表现：PSNR 与 SSIM 逐步下降，说明未调制的图像在采样过程中偏离了正确的数据流形。这表明在强大的 LLDM 先验之下，对 MRI 重建而言，适当注入噪声带来的收益显著超过丢弃原始欠采样图像相位的代价。

English original
Similar to DiffuseRecon (Peng et al., 2022) and Resample (Song et al., 2024), we discover the advantage of introducing stochasticity to the intermediate images for reconstruction and propose RPM to inject a separable noise to MR images by combining the original image phase with a random one. In Table VI, we find that the more original phase is added, the worse the reconstruction is. Meanwhile, Fig. 5(a) visualizes our method w/o RPM, corresponding to λ=0\lambda=0λ=0. PSNR and SSIM drop step by step, meaning that unmodulated images deviate from the correct data manifold during sampling. This indicates that given the strong LLDM prior, the benefits of proper noise injection significantly outweigh the drawback of discarding the original undersampled image phase for MRI reconstruction.

6.3 局限与未来工作

English original
6.3 Limitations and Future Work

当前 MRPD 的推理时间长于监督模型。为加速，我们利用了跳过早期扩散步骤（Meng 等，2022）以及每两步才施加一次硬 DC 与软 CG（Song 等，2024）的做法。上一节我们展示了 DDIM 加速的结果。这些初步结果说明 MRPD 有进一步加速的潜力。此外，针对多线圈采集使用多 GPU 并行计算对 MRPD 也是可行的。

English original
Current MRPD requires a longer inference time than supervised models. To speed up, we have exploited the utilization of skipping the early diffusion steps (Meng et al., 2022) and enforcing hard DC and soft CG every two steps (Song et al., 2024). In the previous section, we show the results of DDIM acceleration. These preliminary results demonstrate the potential of MRPD to be further accelerated. Furthermore, parallel computing using multiple GPUs for multi-coil acquisition is viable for MRPD.

MRPD 是把 LLDM 用于 MRI 重建的一个概念验证，可以与现有的测试时适配方法（Ozturkler 等，2023a）结合以获得进一步提升。

English original
MRPD is a proof-of-concept to utilize LLDMs for MRI reconstruction and can be combined with existing test-time adaptation methods (Ozturkler et al., 2023a) for further improvements.

7 结论

English original
7 Conclusion

我们已成功证明：LLDM 含有对自然图像与医学图像都通用的视觉知识，可用于提升欠采样 MRI 重建的自适应性与泛化性。我们提出 MRPD——一个基于在海量自然图像上预训练的 LLDM 的、新颖的无监督且普遍适配的框架。我们通过涵盖多种采样模式、MRI 对比度与器官的严格测试加以验证，迄今尚未发现任何一种数据是 MRPD 无法泛化的。此外，MRPD 同时适配「可访问 MRI 数据库」与「不可访问 MRI 数据库」两类场景。优越的泛化性与普遍自适应的特性，使 MRPD 有可能推动基于基础模型的加速 MRI 重建研究。

English original
We have successfully demonstrated that an LLDM contains visual knowledge that is universal for natural and medical images and can be leveraged to improve the adaptability and generalizability of undersampled MRI reconstruction. We propose MRPD, a novel unsupervised and universally adapted framework based on an LLDM pre-trained on vast natural images. We validate through rigorous tests, encompassing a variety of sampling patterns, MRI contrasts, and organs, and have yet to find a single data type on which our MRPD does not generalize. Furthermore, MRPD adapts to scenarios both with and without access to MRI databases. The superior generalizability and the universally adaptive characteristics make MRPD potentially catalyze related foundation-model-based research for accelerated MRI reconstruction.
