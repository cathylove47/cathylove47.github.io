伪逆引导扩散模型：用与问题无关的扩散先验求解逆问题

Pseudoinverse-Guided Diffusion Models for Inverse Problems

Jiaming Song · Arash Vahdat · Morteza Mardani · Jan Kautz（NVIDIA）

同一个不在任何逆问题上训练的扩散模型，可以解超分辨率、修补、JPEG 复原，甚至把三者串起来。关键在于把测量模型求伪逆后拉回数据空间，用「去噪结果与测量在伪逆空间中的差」构造引导项——这一项只经过去噪器反传，所以测量可以带噪声、可以非线性、甚至不可微。

摘要

English original
Abstract

扩散模型已成为求解各类逆问题的有力候选。为特定逆问题训练的模型效果很好，但只适用于它自己的场景；而使用与问题无关模型的方法虽然通用，经验性能往往更差。为化解这一两难，我们提出伪逆引导扩散模型（Pseudoinverse-guided Diffusion Models，ΠGDM），用与问题无关的模型来弥补性能差距。ΠGDM 直接从逆问题的测量模型估计条件分数，无需额外训练；与许多只处理无噪声线性测量的现有方法不同，它可以处理带噪、非线性甚至不可微的测量。我们在若干图像复原任务上展示了 ΠGDM 的经验有效性，包括超分辨率、修补与 JPEG 复原。在 ImageNet 上，ΠGDM 与针对特定任务训练的当前最优扩散模型相比具有竞争力，并且是首次用与问题无关的扩散模型达到这一水平。ΠGDM 还能求解测量过程由若干更简单测量复合而成的更广泛一类逆问题。

English original
Diffusion models have become competitive candidates for solving various inverse problems. Models trained for specific inverse problems work well but are limited to their particular use cases, whereas methods that use problem-agnostic models are general but often perform worse empirically. To address this dilemma, we introduce Pseudoinverse-guided Diffusion Models (ΠGDM), an approach that uses problem-agnostic models to close the gap in performance. ΠGDM directly estimates conditional scores from the measurement model of the inverse problem without additional training. It can address inverse problems with noisy, non-linear, or even non-differentiable measurements, in contrast to many existing approaches that are limited to noiseless linear ones. We illustrate the empirical effectiveness of ΠGDM on several image restoration tasks, including super-resolution, inpainting and JPEG restoration. On ImageNet, ΠGDM is competitive with state-of-the-art diffusion models trained on specific tasks, and is the first to achieve this with problem-agnostic diffusion models. ΠGDM can also solve a wider set of inverse problems where the measurement processes are composed of several simpler ones.

1 Introduction

扩散模型 (Sohl-Dickstein 等, 2015)、(Ho 等, 2020)、(Song 等, 2021c) 已被成功应用于诸多领域，如文本到图像生成 (Rombach 等, 2022)、(Saharia 等, 2022b)，自然语言生成 (Li 等, 2022)，音频合成 (Kong 等, 2020) 以及时间序列建模 (Tashiro 等, 2021)。对复杂高维分布的建模能力，也使扩散模型成为求解逆问题的强有力候选——逆问题的目标正是从测量中推断潜在信号 (Bora 等, 2017)、(Daras 等, 2021)、(Ongie 等, 2020)、(Kadkhodaie & Simoncelli, 2021)。

English original
Diffusion models (Sohl-Dickstein et al., 2015; Ho et al., 2020; Song et al., 2021c) have been successfully applied to various applications such as text-to-image generation (Rombach et al., 2022; Saharia et al., 2022b), natural language generation (Li et al., 2022), audio synthesis (Kong et al., 2020), and time series modeling (Tashiro et al., 2021). The ability to model complex, high-dimensional distributions also makes diffusion models strong candidates for solving inverse problems, where the goal is to infer the underlying signal from measurements (Bora et al., 2017; Daras et al., 2021; Ongie et al., 2020; Kadkhodaie & Simoncelli, 2021).

用扩散模型求解逆问题的大多数方法可归入两种范式。第一种范式训练一个针对特定问题、有条件的扩散模型，它被限制在特定逆问题上，例如超分辨率 (Saharia 等, 2021)、(Whang 等, 2021)、(Saharia 等, 2022a)。第二种范式使用与问题无关的扩散模型——它们只为生成建模而训练，不在任何具体逆问题上训练；其解通过"即插即用"的方式获得，把扩散模型与测量过程结合起来，例如借助贝叶斯法则 (Venkatakrishnan 等, 2013a)、(Bardsley, 2012)、(Laumont 等, 2022)、(Choi 等, 2021)、(Song 等, 2021b)、(Jalal 等, 2021)、(Chung 等, 2021)、(Kawar 等, 2021)、(Kawar 等, 2022a)、(Chung 等, 2022b)、(Daras 等, 2022a)。这些方法不必重新训练扩散模型就能轻松迁移到不同任务，但性能往往不如问题专用的扩散模型。

English original
Most methods that solve inverse problems with diffusion models fall into one of the two paradigms. In the first paradigm, one trains a problem-specific, conditional diffusion model that is limited to specific inverse problems, such as super-resolution (Saharia et al., 2021; Whang et al., 2021; Saharia et al., 2022a). In the second paradigm, one uses problem-agnostic diffusion models that are trained for generative modeling but not train on any specific inverse problem; solutions are obtained via a "plug-and-play" approach that combines the diffusion model and the measurement process, e.g., via Bayes' rule (Venkatakrishnan et al., 2013a; Bardsley, 2012; Laumont et al., 2022; Choi et al., 2021; Song et al., 2021b; Jalal et al., 2021; Chung et al., 2021; Kawar et al., 2021; 2022a; Chung et al., 2022b; Daras et al., 2022a). These methods can easily adapt to different tasks without re-training the diffusion model but tend to perform worse than problem-specific diffusion models.

为了两全其美，我们提出伪逆引导（pseudoinverse guidance，ΠG）：用与问题无关的扩散模型达到问题专用模型的经验性能。ΠG 以测量和显式的测量模型为条件，通过贝叶斯法则估计问题相关的分数函数，再用这些分数采样。但与分类器引导／无分类器引导 (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021)、(Ho & Salimans, 2022) 不同，ΠG 直接由已知测量模型得到问题相关的分数，不需要训练额外模型。直观地说，ΠG 把一步去噪解与真实测量分别经过测量模型的"伪逆"变换，再让两者匹配，以此引导扩散过程（见图 1）。这一视角使 ΠG 成为第一个基于引导、既能处理带高斯噪声的测量、又能处理某些非线性甚至不可微测量模型（如 JPEG 压缩 (Kawar 等, 2022b)）的逆问题求解方法。

English original
To achieve the best of both worlds, we introduce pseudoinverse guidance (ΠG), which uses problem-agnostic diffusion models to reach the empirical performance of problem-specific ones. Conditioned on the measurements and an explicit measurement model, ΠG estimates the problem-specific score function via Bayes' rule and uses these scores to draw samples. However, unlike classifier/classifier-free guidance (Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021; Ho & Salimans, 2022), ΠG obtains the problem-specific score directly via the known measurement model, without training additional models. Intuitively, ΠG guides the diffusion process by matching the one-step denoising solution and the ground-truth measurements, after transforming both via a "pseudoinverse" of the measurement model (see Fig. 1). This perspective allows ΠG to be the first guidance-based approach for inverse problem solving that handles measurements with Gaussian noise, as well as some non-linear, non-differentiable measurement models, such as JPEG compression (Kawar et al., 2022b).

我们在 ImageNet 验证图像上的多种逆问题上评估该方法，称之为伪逆引导扩散模型（Pseudoinverse-Guided Diffusion Models，ΠGDM），任务包括超分辨率、修补与 JPEG 复原，并表明其性能与当前最优的任务专用扩散模型 (Saharia 等, 2021)、(Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021)、(Saharia 等, 2022a) 相当。据我们所知，ΠGDM 是首个在 ImageNet 上达到这一质量的、基于与问题无关模型的方法。我们进一步把 ΠGDM 应用到更广泛的一类逆问题：测量过程由不同类型的测量复合而成。这使我们能轻松求解范围大得多的问题，包括此前从未用扩散模型求解过的问题（见图 2），例如低分辨率 + JPEG 压缩 + 掩膜。

English original
We evaluate our method, termed Pseudoinverse-Guided Diffusion Models (ΠGDM), on various inverse problems, such as super-resolution, inpainting, and JPEG restoration over ImageNet validation images, and show that it achieves similar performance when compared against state-of-the-art task-specific diffusion models (Saharia et al., 2021; Dhariwal & Nichol, 2021; Saharia et al., 2022a). To the best of our knowledge, ΠGDM is the first approach based on problem-agnostic models to achieve this quality on ImageNet. We further apply ΠGDM to a wider range of inverse problems, where the measurement process is composed of different types of measurements. This allows us to easily solve a much wider set of problems, including ones have never been solved with diffusion models (see Fig. 2), such as low-resolution + JPEG compression + masking.

深度神经网络已被广泛用作求解逆问题的先验 (Venkatakrishnan 等, 2013b)。这里我们关注这样一种设定：可以基于干净数据训练模型，但不能基于问题训练——问题只在推理时才已知。这在许多现实应用中都是合理的，例如医学成像 (Jalal 等, 2021)、(Chung & Ye, 2022) 与 JPEG 复原 (Ehrlich 等, 2020)。这类逆问题求解器可以使用不同类型的神经网络，例如随机初始化的网络 (Ulyanov 等, 2018)、去噪器 (Romano 等, 2016)、鲁棒分类器 (Santurkar 等, 2019) 与生成模型 (Bora 等, 2017)。基于生成对抗网络（GAN，Goodfellow 等 (2014)）的方法搜索潜变量和／或生成器参数，使产生的图像与测量相符 (Bora 等, 2017)、(Pan 等, 2021)、(Menon 等, 2020)；这类方法往往需要数百甚至数千次迭代，尽管近期已有更高效的方法 (Daras 等, 2022a)。

English original
Deep neural networks have been extensively used as priors for solving inverse problems (Venkatakrishnan et al., 2013b). Here, we focus on the setting where we can train models based on clean data but not on the problem, which is only known at inference time. This is reasonable in many real-world applications, such as medical imaging (Jalal et al., 2021; Chung & Ye, 2022) and JPEG restoration (Ehrlich et al., 2020). These inverse problem solvers may use different types of neural networks, such as randomly initialized networks (Ulyanov et al., 2018), denoisers (Romano et al., 2016), robust classifiers (Santurkar et al., 2019), and generative models (Bora et al., 2017). Methods based on generative adversarial networks (GANs, Goodfellow et al. (2014)) search for the latent variables and/or the generator parameters that would produce images aligning with the measurements (Bora et al., 2017; Pan et al., 2021; Menon et al., 2020); these methods often require hundreds if not thousands of iterations, despite recent methods with improved efficiency (Daras et al., 2022a).

作为另一类生成模型，扩散模型也被用作逆问题求解器，相比 GAN 有两个显著优点：(i) 它以含噪数据上的回归目标训练，因此能自然地处理测量噪声，无需像 GAN 那样做反演；(ii) 它与 SDE／ODE 求解器的紧密联系允许使用更高效的迭代更新。特别地，去噪扩散复原模型（DDRM，Kawar 等 (2022a)）同时利用这两点，为带噪与无噪测量推导出高效的逆问题求解器。

English original
As another family of generative models, diffusion models are also used as inverse problem solvers, with two notable advantages over GANs: (i) it is trained with regression objectives over noisy data, so it can naturally deal with measurement noise without having to perform inversion like in GANs; (ii) its close connections to SDE/ODE solvers allow the use of more efficient iterative updates. In particular, Denoising Diffusion Restoration Models (DDRM, Kawar et al. (2022a)) leverage both to derive efficient inverse problem solvers for both noisy and noiseless measurements.

与 DDRM 类似，许多工作采用「替换」思路：把一步去噪器的部分中间预测替换为测量（有时在变换后的空间中进行），以此来强制与测量一致 (Song 等, 2021c)、(Choi 等, 2021)、(Song 等, 2021b)、(Chung 等, 2021)、(Kawar 等, 2021)、(Su 等, 2022)。这些方法虽在许多任务上成功，却难以处理稀疏测量——替换对采样过程的影响较弱。ΠG 通过在扩散模型中额外计算梯度，让测量影响更新中所有被预测的数值，而不论其稀疏与否。这与重建引导类似，后者在更新时也对扩散模型求导 (Ho 等, 2022)、(Ryu & Ye, 2022)、(Chung 等, 2022b)。事实上，若测量矩阵的转置等于其伪逆，ΠG 在无噪声线性情形下与重建引导完全相同（附录 A.2）。不过，如 3.4 节所述，ΠG 给出了处理带噪、非线性乃至不可微测量的有原则的做法。

English original
Similar to DDRM, many works adopt a "replacement" approach, where consistency with the measurements are enforced by replacing parts of its intermediate predictions from the one-step denoiser with the measurements, sometimes in a transformed space (Song et al., 2021c; Choi et al., 2021; Song et al., 2021b; Chung et al., 2021; Kawar et al., 2021; Su et al., 2022). Despite being successful in many tasks, they have trouble dealing with sparse measurements, where the replacements have weaker impact on the sampling process. By computing additional gradients through the diffusion model, ΠG allows the measurements to impact all the predicted values during the updates, regardless of sparsity. This is similar to reconstruction guidance, which also differentiates through the diffusion model during its updates (Ho et al., 2022; Ryu & Ye, 2022; Chung et al., 2022b). In fact, ΠG is identical in the noiseless, linear case if the transpose of the measurement matrix is equal to its pseudoinverse (App. A.2). Nevertheless, ΠG introduces principled ways of dealing with noisy, non-linear, or even non-differentiable measurements, as discussed in Sec. 3.4.
