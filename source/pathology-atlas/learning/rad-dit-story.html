从自然图像到医学扫描：扩散模型用于医学图像生成的可迁移性

From Natural Images to Medical Scans: Transferability of Diffusion Models for Medical Image Generation

Efthymios Georgiou · Ioannis Kakogeorgiou · Sarah Brüningk

医学扩散模型几乎都把去噪器从零训练。「从 ImageNet 初始化」在判别式医学影像里是常规操作，但在生成式建模里到底有没有用、有多大用、迁移的又是什么，一直缺少系统评估。这篇论文固定去噪器这一变量，在潜空间与像素空间两种范式、131M–952M 三档规模、两个放射学数据集上回答四个问题：迁移是否有效、迁移的是什么、自然图像与医学图像在表示上有多接近、以及收益能不能用医学预训练替代。

这一段是译者注，不是原文。论文所有结论都建立在「哪种去噪器、在什么空间里初始化」上。

  1. 像素扩散 vs 潜扩散：像素扩散把去噪器直接作用在含噪像素 F(xt;W)F(\mathbf{x}_t;\mathrm{W})F(xt​;W)；潜扩散先用分词器把图像压成潜变量 z=E(x)\mathbf{z}=\mathcal{E}(\mathbf{x})z=E(x)，再去噪 F(zt;W)F(\mathbf{z}_t;\mathrm{W})F(zt​;W)。前者的全部先验都在去噪器里，后者有相当一部分先验被分词器「预先提供」——这正是本文解释两种范式迁移幅度差异的关键。
  2. IN 初始化 vs 从零：「IN」指加载在 ImageNet 上训好的同一架构去噪器权重再微调；「scratch」是随机初始化从头训练。论文还加入「统计匹配（ST）」：从匹配 IN 权重逐张量均值方差的高斯里重采样，用来看收益是否只来自权重分布。
  3. gFID / rFID：gFID 是生成样本与真实图像的 FID；rFID 是分词器重建图像的 FID，用来衡量分词器本身的上限。表 1 里两者分列，不要把 gFID 的差距全算到去噪器头上。

Abstract

从自然图像进行迁移学习在监督式医学图像分析中已是标准做法，在自监督医学图像分析中也越来越常见，但在生成式建模、尤其是扩散去噪器中的作用仍未被充分探索。医学扩散模型通常从零训练去噪器，没有对自然图像迁移的系统性评估。我们在潜空间与像素两种范式、模型规模（131M–952M）以及两个放射学数据集（RadImageNet、BraTS）上分析这一问题，并追问迁移的哪些方面支撑生成。我们发现：i) 自然图像初始化稳定改善生成，像素空间相对 gFID 提升 21–34%，潜空间为 14%，后者中分词器已提供大部分先验；gFID 增益在病理分类与放射组学特征 FD 上同步体现，二者都是临床保真度的代理。ii) 收益反映空间结构而非语义：语义探针不能预测生成质量，而为去噪器内部表示改造的空间结构指标在 ∣r∣=0.85|r|{=}0.85∣r∣=0.85 上追踪 gFID，说明空间组织是预训练得以体现的一条通道。iii) 混合域去噪器训练显示，自然图像与医学图像在直到后段块之前共享相似的逐层表示，把自然图像混入医学训练可提升保真度。iv) 迁移收益与自然图像初始化绑定，优于域内医学预训练与若干重初始化方案。总体而言，在我们的设定下自然图像去噪器向医学去噪器正向迁移。代码见 CAIROLab-Bern/rad-dit。

English original
Transfer learning from natural images is standard in supervised and increasingly adopted in self-supervised medical image analysis, but its role in generative modelling, and in particular diffusion denoisers, remains underexplored. Medical diffusion models typically train the denoiser from scratch, with no systematic evaluation of natural-image transfer. We analyse this across latent and pixel paradigms, model sizes (131M–952M) and two radiology datasets (RadImageNet, BraTS), and ask which aspects of transfer support generation. We find that: i) Natural-image initialisation consistently improves generation, by 21-34% relative gFID in pixel space against 14% in latent space, where the tokeniser supplies much of the prior. gFID gains mirror downstream pathology classification and radiomic-feature FD, both proxies for clinical fidelity. ii) Benefits reflect spatial structure, not semantics: semantic probing does not predict generation quality, whereas a spatial-structure metric adapted to internal denoiser representations tracks gFID at ∣r∣=0.85|r|{=}0.85∣r∣=0.85, positioning spatial organisation as a channel through which pretraining manifests. iii) Mixed-domain denoiser training shows that natural and medical images share similar layer-wise representations up to the late blocks, and mixing natural images into medical training improves fidelity. iv) Transfer benefit is tied to natural image initialisation, outperforming in-domain medical pretraining and several reinitialisation schemes. Overall, natural image denoisers transfer positively to medical ones in our setup. Code is available at CAIROLab-Bern/rad-dit.

1 Introduction

教学绘图教材把图像创作分解为少量基本元素，例如边缘、轮廓、比例与明暗，复杂场景可由这些元素组合而成。视觉神经科学中有相近的原理：早期视觉皮层的神经元对朝向边缘这类低级特征有响应，而这类特征存在于任何视觉输入中，无论自然照片还是医学扫描。

English original
Pedagogical drawing textbooks decompose image creation into a small set of basic primitives, e.g., edges, contours, proportions, and light-and-shade, from which complex scenes can be composed. A related principle appears in visual neuroscience, where neurons in early visual cortex respond to low-level features such as oriented edges, which are present in any visual input, whether a natural photograph or a medical scan.

领域专长建立在这一共享感知基础之上。放射科医生通过大量接触医学图像获得领域特异的模式识别能力，但这种优势一般不会延伸到非放射学的视觉搜索任务。这提示应区分可广泛复用的视觉基本元素与习得的领域专长。

English original
Domain expertise is built on top of this shared perceptual basis. Radiologists, for example, acquire domain-specific pattern-recognition skills through extensive exposure to medical images, but this advantage does not generally extend to non-radiological visual search tasks. This suggests a distinction between broadly reusable visual primitives and learned domain-specific expertise.

计算机视觉中的迁移学习由类似现象驱动。从 ImageNet（IN）初始化是监督式医学影像的标准做法。早期卷积模型研究发现迁移收益有限，主要体现为更快收敛与低级（通用）特征复用。Mustafa 等指出尺度是可迁移性的关键因素。Matsoukas 等把分析扩展到卷积与 Transformer 模型，发现迁移有效时主要来源是特征复用，对较小数据集与更接近的域尤其如此。有意思的是，Transformer 比卷积网络更依赖特征复用。在自监督（SSL）医学影像中，自然图像编码器越来越多被用作初始化或直接使用，有时可与领域特定模型匹敌。然而在生成式建模、尤其是扩散去噪器中，迁移的作用仍未被充分探索。

English original
A similar pattern motivates transfer learning in computer vision. Initialising from ImageNet (IN) is the standard approach in supervised medical imaging. Early works on convolutional models find that transfer benefits are modest, mostly faster convergence and reuse of low-level (generic) features. Mustafa et al. identify scale as a crucial factor for transferability. Matsoukas et al. extend their analysis to convolutional and transformer models and find feature reuse to be the main source when transfer helps, especially for smaller datasets and closer domains. Interestingly, transformers rely on feature reuse more than convolutional networks. In self-supervised (SSL) medical imaging, natural-image encoders are increasingly used as initialisation or off-the-shelf, at times matching domain-specific models. For generative modelling, however, and diffusion denoisers in particular, the role of transfer remains underexplored.

最先进的扩散生成模型分为两大类：像素扩散与潜扩散。像素扩散把去噪器直接作用于像素。潜扩散把分词器与在其潜空间中工作的去噪器配对。在潜扩散中，自然图像先验可以通过分词器、去噪器或两者迁移。在医学领域，自然图像分词器已被复用，少数工作微调预训练的文本到图像去噪器。但去噪器迁移仍不常见，且被视为实现细节而非被研究的现象。自然图像去噪器迁移对医学图像生成是否有益、预训练模型的哪些方面解释观察到的收益，仍是开放问题。

English original
State-of-the-art diffusion generative models fall into two broad paradigms: pixel diffusion and latent diffusion. Pixel diffusion applies the denoiser directly to pixels. Latent diffusion pairs a tokeniser with a denoiser that operates in its latent space. In latent diffusion, natural-image priors can be transferred through the tokeniser, the denoiser, or both. In the medical domain, natural-image tokenisers have been reused, and a few works fine-tune pretrained text-to-image denoisers. Yet denoiser transfer remains uncommon and is treated as an implementation detail rather than a studied phenomenon. Whether natural-image denoiser transfer is beneficial for medical image generation, and which aspects of the pretrained model account for any observed benefit, remain open questions.

本文研究面向医学图像生成的自然图像去噪器迁移。分析围绕两个问题：自然去噪器是否迁移到医学域；若迁移，迁移的是什么、如何迁移。我们在潜空间与像素空间、模型规模（131M–952M）以及两个放射学数据集（RadIN；BraTS）上展开。结果显示正向迁移，并提示收益与自然图像初始化绑定，表现为去噪器内部表示具有更好的空间组织。分析以像素去噪器为中心，潜空间给出平行结果。

English original
In this work, we study natural-image denoiser transfer for medical image generation. Our analysis revolves around two broad questions: do natural denoisers transfer to the medical domain, and if so, what is it that transfers and how? We address these across latent and pixel-space, model sizes (131M–952M), and two radiology datasets (RadIN; BraTS). Our results show a positive transfer, and suggest that the benefit is tied to natural-image initialisation and manifests as better spatial organisation of the internal denoiser representations. We centre our analysis on pixel denoisers, with parallel results for latent ones.

我们的主要发现是：

  1. 自然图像初始化在两种范式中都改善生成。像素空间受益最大，gFID 提升 21–34%，潜空间为 14%，后者中分词器承载了大部分先验。FID 增益在放射组学 Fréchet 距离（FD-Rad）与病理分类 macroF1 上同步体现，二者都是临床保真度的代理。

  3. 混合域实验显示，去噪器在网络的大部分层对自然与医学输入计算相似表示，把自然数据混入医学训练可提升医学保真度。
  4. 收益与自然图像初始化绑定。医学预训练与若干重初始化方案都无法恢复；示意性外推表明弥合差距需要多得多的医学数据。
English original
Our main findings are: (1) Natural-image initialisation improves generation in both paradigms. Pixel-space models benefit most, improving by 21–34% gFID against 14% in latent space, where the tokeniser carries much of the prior. FID gains are mirrored in radiomic Fréchet distance (FD-Rad) and pathology classification macroF1, both proxies for clinical fidelity. (2) Transfer benefits reflect spatial structure. Semantic probing shows that linear separability does not predict generation quality, while a modified spatial structure metric for diffusion representations, LDSC, tracks gFID at ∣r∣=0.85|r|{=}0.85∣r∣=0.85, positioning spatial organisation as a channel through which pretraining manifests. (4) The benefit is tied to natural-image initialisation. It cannot be recovered by medical pretraining or several re-initialisation schemes. Illustrative extrapolation suggests far more medical data would be needed to close the gap.

2.1 扩散模型与流模型

English original
2.1 Diffusion and Flow Models

扩散模型与流模型从噪声生成数据。为此，它们被训练去逆转从数据到噪声的前向加噪过程。近似这一去噪过程的网络就是去噪器。

English original
Diffusion and flow models generate data from noise. To do so, they are trained to reverse a forward noising process of data towards noise. The networks that approximate this denoising process are denoisers.

潜空间去噪。潜去噪器在视觉分词器的潜空间中工作，分词器通常是变分自编码器（VAE），相对像素扩散降低了空间维度与计算成本。Esser 等提出 VQ-GAN，把图像空间压缩为离散码本，并用额外的感知损失与 patch 判别器损失训练。Rombach 等使用连续、KL 正则化的 VAE 潜空间与 U-Net 去噪器。VA-VAE 通过把潜空间与 DINOv2 特征对齐，在改善潜空间结构的同时实现更大的空间压缩（16×）。

English original
Latent-space denoising. Latent denoisers operate in the latent space of a visual tokeniser, typically a Variational Autoencoder (VAE), which reduces spatial dimensionality and computational cost relative to pixel-space diffusion. Esser et al. propose a VQ-GAN to compress the image space into a discrete codebook, trained with additional perceptual and patch discriminator loss. Rombach et al. use a continuous, KL-regularised VAE latent space with a U-Net denoiser. VA-VAE improves the latent space structure while achieving larger spatial compression (16×), by aligning it with DINOv2 features.

在去噪器一侧，Peebles 等用扩散 Transformer（DiT）替换 U-Net 骨干，SiT 把它扩展到流目标。LightningDiT（LDiT）在原始 DiT 架构之上提出训练与结构上的改进。本文把 LDiT 与不同 VAE 组合，研究去噪器是否能从自然图像迁移中获得分词器已提供先验之外的收益。

English original
On the denoiser side, Peebles et al. replace the U-Net backbone with a Diffusion Transformer (DiT), and SiT extends it to flow objectives. LightningDiT (LDiT) proposes training and architectural advancements on top of the vanilla DiT architecture. Here, we couple LDiT with different VAEs, and study whether the denoiser benefits from natural-image transfer beyond the prior already provided by the tokeniser.

像素空间去噪。像素扩散把去噪器直接作用于像素，避开了 VAE 重建（解码器）瓶颈。早期扩散模型在像素空间工作，但高维导致潜范式流行。基于像素的方法也被用作提升分辨率的多阶段级联。

English original
Pixel-space denoising. Pixel diffusion applies the denoiser directly to pixels, avoiding the VAE reconstruction (decoder) bottleneck. Early diffusion models operate in pixel space, but the high dimensionality led to the popularisation of the latent paradigm. Pixel-based approaches have also been used as multi-stage cascades for increasing resolution.

直到最近，JiT 用普通 Transformer 去噪大像素块（16–32），继承了 DiT 的进展与 x\mathbf{x}x-预测目标，复兴了单阶段像素扩散。我们用 JiT 作为像素空间去噪器，据我们所知首次把它迁移到医学域。

English original
Only recently, JiT revived single-stage pixel diffusion with a plain transformer that denoises large pixel patches (16–32), carrying over advances from DiT and an x\mathbf{x}x-prediction objective. We use JiT as our pixel-space denoiser, and, to the best of our knowledge, are the first to transfer it to the medical domain.

表示对齐与联合去噪。REPA 把扩散特征与预训练编码器对齐，iREPA 发现驱动对齐收益的是空间结构而非语义。我们在自然图像初始化上观察到类似模式。联合去噪方法把图像与特征放在一起建模。ReDi 把 VAE 潜变量与 PCA 后的 DINOv2 特征耦合，REGLUE 去噪与 VAE 潜变量拼接的压缩 DINOv2 特征。Latent Forcing 把联合去噪带到像素空间，V-Co 用 MMDiT 架构联合去噪像素与特征。我们把 V-Co 作为联合图像-特征去噪基线，据我们所知首次为医学图像生成研究这一范式。

English original
Representation Alignment & Joint denoising. REPA aligns diffusion features to a pretrained encoder, and iREPA finds that spatial structure, rather than semantics, drives alignment gains. We observe an analogous pattern for natural-image initialisation. Joint-denoising methods model images and features together. ReDi couples VAE latents with PCA DINOv2 features, and REGLUE denoises compressed DINOv2 features concatenated with VAE latents. Latent Forcing brings joint denoising to pixel space, and V-Co jointly denoises pixels and features with an MMDiT architecture. We employ V-Co as a joint image-feature denoising baseline and, to the best of our knowledge, are the first to study this paradigm for medical image generation.

2.2 医学领域的生成与迁移

English original
2.2 Medical-domain Generation and Transfer

医学生成建模。扩散与流模型被广泛用于医学图像生成。3D 中，U-Net 去噪器通常在 3D VQ-GAN（离散）或 VAE（连续）潜空间中训练，也有直接在体素空间、或在小波空间训练的。规模化方面，MAISI 用潜 U-Net 生成 3D CT，MAISIv2 复用 MAISI 的 VAE 与 U-Net，但把目标换成 rectified flow。3D MedDiffusion 把自定义 VAE 与在医学数据上从零训练的潜去噪器配对。2D 切片也可以堆叠成 3D 体数据。2D 中，多数工作从零训练去噪器，但少数微调预训练的文本到图像模型，例如 RoentGen、MediSyn 与 Med-Art。在组织病理学中，PathLDM 用 IN 权重初始化去噪器并改善生成，ZoomLDM 沿用类似配方。多数工作把迁移当作实现细节。我们则隔离出去噪器，跨规模与范式系统研究它。

English original
Medical generative modelling. Diffusion and flow models are widely used for medical image generation. In 3D, a U-Net denoiser is typically trained in 3D VQ-GAN (discrete) or VAE (continuous) latents, directly in voxel space, or in wavelet space. At scale, MAISI generates 3D CT with a latent U-Net, and MAISIv2 reuses MAISI's VAE and U-Net, yet replaces the objective with rectified flow. 3D MedDiffusion pairs a custom VAE with a latent denoiser trained from scratch on medical data. 2D slices can also be stacked into 3D volumes. In 2D, most works train the denoiser from scratch, yet a few fine-tune a pretrained text-to-image model, e.g., RoentGen, MediSyn, and Med-Art, fine-tune pretrained text-to-image models. In histopathology, PathLDM initialises its denoiser from IN weights, improving generation, and ZoomLDM follows a similar recipe. Most works treat transfer as an implementation detail. We instead isolate the denoiser and study it systematically across size and paradigms.

7 结论与讨论

English original
7 Conclusion & Discussion

我们对面向医学图像生成的自然图像去噪器迁移做了系统研究。IN 迁移在潜空间与像素空间、模型规模以及两个放射学数据集上稳定改善生成性能。增益也体现在放射组学距离与病理分类上。从零训练的模型达到相近的语义探针精度却生成更差的样本，而空间组织（以 LDSC 度量）与 gFID 在 ∣r∣=0.85|r|{=}0.85∣r∣=0.85 上相关，说明空间结构是预训练发挥作用的通道。尽管自然图像与医学图像在高层内容上不同，混合域去噪器在网络的大部分层对两者计算相似表示，把自然图像混入医学训练可提升保真度。收益与自然图像初始化绑定，我们测试的替代方案都无法复现，包括域内医学预训练。从生成式建模的角度看，自然图像对医学图像生成非常有用。

English original
We present a systematic study of natural-image denoiser transfer for medical image generation. IN transfer consistently improves generation performance across latent and pixel space, model scales, and two radiology datasets. Gains are also mirrored in radiomic distance and pathology classification. From a generative modelling lens, natural images are very useful for medical image generation.
