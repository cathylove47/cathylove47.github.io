可调控条件扩散：医学图像重建中的分布外适配

Steerable Conditional Diffusion for Out-of-Distribution Adaptation in Medical Image Reconstruction

Riccardo Barbano* · Alexander Denker* · Hyungjin Chung* · Tae Hoon Roh · Simon Arridge · Peter Maass · Bangti Jin · Jong Chul Ye（*共同一作）

扩散模型做医学重建，一旦测试分布偏离训练分布，重建图就会长出只在训练集里出现过的东西——合成椭圆、脑回纹理、别的解剖结构。SCD 不换网络、也不需要新域的配对数据：它只用手上这一条测量 yyy，在反向采样的每一步顺手把一层低秩残差调到与 yyy 一致，让去噪器朝正确的方向偏一点。

摘要

English original
Abstract

去噪扩散模型已经成为求解成像逆问题的首选生成式框架。围绕这类模型的一个关键顾虑是它们在分布外任务上的表现，而这仍是一个研究不足的挑战。把一个扩散模型用在分布外数据集上，可以生成看起来很真实的重建，但会带有只存在于训练数据集中的幻觉性图像特征。为处理这一偏差并提高重建精度，我们提出一种新的测试时适配采样框架，称为可调控条件扩散（Steerable Conditional Diffusion）。具体而言，该框架仅依据现有测量所提供的信息，在图像重建的同时对扩散模型进行适配。利用所提方法，我们在多种成像模态的分布外性能上取得显著提升，推动了去噪扩散模型在真实应用中的稳健部署。

English original
Denoising diffusion models have emerged as the go-to generative framework for solving inverse problems in imaging. A critical concern regarding these models is their performance on out-of-distribution tasks, which remains an under-explored challenge. Using a diffusion model on an out-of-distribution dataset, realistic reconstructions can be generated, but with hallucinating image features that are uniquely present in the training dataset. To address this discrepancy and improve reconstruction accuracy, we introduce a novel test-time adaptation sampling framework called Steerable Conditional Diffusion. Specifically, this framework adapts the diffusion model, concurrently with image reconstruction, based solely on the information provided by the available measurement. Utilising the proposed method, we achieve substantial enhancements in out-of-distribution performance across diverse imaging modalities, advancing the robust deployment of denoising diffusion models in real-world applications.

A. 相关工作

English original
A. Related Work

已有充分记录表明，深度学习模型在与训练集不同的数据上评估时通常给出更差的结果。这在图像分类 [21] 与图像重建 [6]、[7] 等计算机视觉任务中都观察到了。然而，高质量配对数据集的稀缺，常常使得必须在 OOD 场景中部署深度学习模型。把模型适配到 OOD 数据，有时被表述为对分布偏移的鲁棒性 [13]。针对监督式深度重建器在分布偏移下缩小性能差距，已有 [8] 与 [22] 的研究。测试时训练（test-time-training）框架 [8] 研究与本文相似的设定，基于单条可用测量来适配深度重建器的参数；但我们的重点是扩散模型的适配，而测试时训练并不能直接套用。

English original
It has been well documented that deep learning models typically provide worse results if they are evaluated on data that differs from the training set. This has been observed in computer vision tasks such as classification [21] and in image reconstruction [6], [7]. However, the scarcity of high-quality paired datasets often necessitates deploying deep learning models in OOD settings. Model adaptation to OOD data is sometimes framed as robustness against distribution shifts [13]. Closing the performance gap under distribution shifts for supervised deep reconstructors was studied by [8] and [22]. The framework of test-time-training [8] studies a similar setting to us and adapts the parameters of a deep reconstructor based on a single available measurement. However, we focus on the adaptation of diffusion models, where test-time training is not directly applicable.

近期提升扩散模型 OOD 性能的尝试，是用新目标域的一小份真值图像数据集去微调预训练扩散模型 [23]–[26]。在保持整体模型固定的前提下使用低秩残差通路进行微调的策略，已在 [26]、[25] 中探索过。然而，这些方法都要求有图像 x\mathbf{x}x 与测量 y\mathbf{y}y 的配对数据集。

English original
Recent attempts to enhance the OOD performance of diffusion models, fine-tune a pre-trained diffusion model using a small dataset of ground truth images from the new target domain [23]–[26]. Fine-tuning strategies using low-rank residual pathways while keeping the overall model fixed, were explored in [26], [25]. However, all of these methods require a paired dataset of images x\mathbf{x}x and measurements y\mathbf{y}y.

此外，还有方法试图在只有受损测量数据的情况下从零训练扩散模型 [27]、[28]。

不过这些方法需要两个条件：i) 由不同采集协议得到的大量测量数据集合；ii) 成像算子满足满秩条件 [28]，或能计算其 Moore–Penrose 伪逆 [27]。实践中这两个条件往往都不容易满足。

English original
Furthermore, methods have been proposed that aim to train a diffusion model from scratch given only corrupted measured data [27], [28].
Nevertheless, these methods require two conditions: i) a large collection of data measurements taken under different acquisition protocols; and ii) a full rank condition on the imaging operator [28] or computing its Moore-Penrose pseudo-inverse [27]. In practice, both conditions are often non-trivial to satisfy.

同一时期，一个类似的计算框架 P2L 被提出，用于学习文生图扩散模型的提示词 [29]。但 P2L 并不调整模型参数，因此无法适配训练时与测试时不同的分布。而且在撰写本文时，医学图像领域还不存在大规模开源的文生图扩散模型。

English original
Concurrently, a similar computational framework, P2L, was proposed to learn the prompt for text-to-image diffusion models [29]. However, P2L does not adjust the model parameters and hence is incapable of adapting to distinct train-test time distributions. Also, no large-scale open-source text-to-image diffusion models for medical images exist at the time of writing this paper.

VI. 讨论

English original
VI. Discussion

关于实用性的一大顾虑是 SCD 计算成本的增加。在第 V-B2 节中我们给出了初步实验：只每 kkk 个采样步适配一次以加速 SCD。近期工作 [69] 把扩散过程分为三个阶段，即混沌阶段、语义阶段与精修阶段，并论证混沌阶段的引导并不必要、大多数特征在语义阶段生成。因此，我们可以只在后两个阶段应用 SCD。提升扩散模型的采样速度是一个研究方向，我们预计该方向上很多未来改进都可以用到 SCD 上。

English original
One concern for the practicability is the increase in the computational cost for SCD. In V-B2 we have provided first experiments to speed-up SCD by adapting the parameters only every kkkth sampling step. Recent work [69] divides the diffusion process into three stages, i.e., chaotic, semantic and refinement stage, and argues that guidance in the chaotic stage is not necessary and most features are generated in the semantic stage. Thus, we could apply SCD only during the latter two stages. Improving the sampling speed of diffusion models is one research focus and we expect that a lot of the future improvements in this direction can be applied to SCD.

SCD 依据测量数据的似然来适配参数，具体来说我们需要前向算子及其伴随。与其它基于似然的方法（例如 DIP [61]）类似，这会带来过拟合风险。我们通过三点缓解：1) 参数高效的适配；2) 引入额外正则项；3) 采用早停。对所有仅靠测量数据拟合参数的方法，性能都有上限。特别是当似然没有信息量时，适配可能失败。图 4 说明了这一点：SCD 的性能随 CT 角度减少而退化，这体现了基于似然的方法所面临的挑战。

English original
SCD adapts the parameters based on the likelihood of the measured data, in particular we require access to the forward operator and its adjoint. Similar to other likelihood-based methods, e.g., DIP [61], this poses the risk of over-fitting. We mitigate this risk by 1) a parameter-efficient adaptation, 2) incorporation of an additional regulariser and 3) applying early stopping. There are performance limits for all approaches based on fitting parameters using the measured data alone. In particular, for an uninformative likelihood, adaptation may fail. This is illustrated in Fig. 4: SCD’s performance deteriorates with fewer CT angles, which shows the challenge for likelihood-based methods.

VII. 结论

English original
VII. Conclusion

本文提出可调控条件扩散，一种在反向采样过程中仅依赖单条测量数据来适配扩散模型的方法。

我们在多种成像模态上的实验揭示：当用于 OOD 重建任务时，扩散模型会生成来自训练数据集的幻觉性特征。

通过所提方法，我们证明适配扩散模型能大幅缓解这些伪影。

此外，我们展示了 LoRA 方法不仅提供了一种内存高效的微调方案，也可用于反向采样中求解成像逆问题的扩散模型。作为未来研究，把我们的方法扩展到涉及更大量测量数据集合的场景很有前景。

English original
In this work, we propose Steerable Conditional Diffusion, a method that adapts diffusion models during reverse sampling, relying solely on a single measured data.
Our experiments across diverse imaging modalities reveal that when applied to OOD reconstruction tasks, diffusion models can generate hallucinatory features from the training dataset.
Through the proposed approach, we demonstrate that adapting diffusion models drastically mitigates these artefacts.
Furthermore, we showcase that the LoRA approach not only provides a memory-efficient fine-tuning solution but is also applicable to diffusion models in reverse sampling for solving imaging inverse problems. As future research, extending our approach to scenarios involving a more extensive collection of measured data holds promise.
