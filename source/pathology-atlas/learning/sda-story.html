CVPR 2025 · TEST-TIME ADAPTATION / DIFFUSION SYNTHETIC DATA

一切交给合成域：用合成域对齐做扩散驱动的测试时适配

Everything to the Synthetic: Diffusion-driven Test-time Adaptation via Synthetic-Domain Alignment

Jiayi Guo1,2 · Junhao Zhao1 · Chaoqun Du1 · Yulin Wang1 · Chunjiang Ge1 · Zanlin Ni1 · Shiji Song1 · Humphrey Shi2,* · Gao Huang1

扩散驱动的测试时适配（TTA）把目标图像「投影回源域」，但投影出来的其实是扩散模型的合成域。这篇论文指出：肉眼看着和源图没差别，深度网络却明显不认它。既然数据落进了合成域，那就把模型也调到合成域去——用条件扩散模型造带标签的合成数据，再用无条件扩散模型把数据对齐到测试时真正使用的那个合成域，最后在同一个域里做域内预测。

摘要

English original
Abstract

测试时适配（TTA）的目标是提升在源域上预训练的模型在先前未见、发生偏移的目标域上的性能。传统 TTA 方法主要依据目标数据流来适配模型权重，这使得模型性能对目标数据的数量与顺序很敏感。近期提出的扩散驱动 TTA 方法通过适配模型输入而非权重来缓解这一问题：在源域上训练的无条件扩散模型把目标域数据变换到一个合成域，并期望这个合成域能近似源域。然而，本文揭示：尽管扩散驱动 TTA 中的合成数据看起来与源数据难以区分，但对深度网络而言，它与源数据并不对齐，甚至显著不同。为解决这一问题，我们提出合成域对齐（Synthetic-Domain Alignment，SDA）框架。我们的核心洞见是用合成数据微调源模型，以保证更好的对齐。具体而言，我们先使用条件扩散模型生成带标签的样本，构建一个合成数据集；随后，在微调之前，用前述无条件扩散模型对每个样本加噪再去噪。这个「混合扩散」（Mix of Diffusion，MoD）过程缓解了条件扩散模型与无条件扩散模型之间潜在的域失配。在分类器、分割器和多模态大语言模型（MLLM，例如 LLaVA）上的大量实验表明，SDA 实现了更优的域对齐，并稳定超过现有的扩散驱动 TTA 方法。代码见 https://github.com/SHI-Labs/Diffusion-Driven-Test-Time-Adaptation-via-Synthetic-Domain-Alignment 。

English original
Test-time adaptation (TTA) aims to improve the performance of source-domain pre-trained models on previously unseen, shifted target domains. Traditional TTA methods primarily adapt model weights based on target data streams, making model performance sensitive to the amount and order of target data. The recently proposed diffusion-driven TTA methods mitigate this by adapting model inputs instead of weights, where an unconditional diffusion model, trained on the source domain, transforms target-domain data into a synthetic domain that is expected to approximate the source domain. However, in this paper, we reveal that although the synthetic data in diffusion-driven TTA seems indistinguishable from the source data, it is unaligned with, or even markedly different from the latter for deep networks. To address this issue, we propose a Synthetic-Domain Alignment (SDA) framework. Our key insight is to fine-tune the source model with synthetic data to ensure better alignment. Specifically, we first employ a conditional diffusion model to generate labeled samples, creating a synthetic dataset. Subsequently, we use the aforementioned unconditional diffusion model to add noise to and denoise each sample before fine-tuning. This Mix of Diffusion (MoD) process mitigates the potential domain misalignment between the conditional and unconditional models. Extensive experiments across classifiers, segmenters, and multimodal large language models (MLLMs, e.g., LLaVA) demonstrate that SDA achieves superior domain alignment and consistently outperforms existing diffusion-driven TTA methods.

1 Introduction

测试时适配（TTA）[26], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [38], [15], [54] 是一个新兴研究方向，处理的是源模型在被偏移的目标数据上评估时出现的域失配问题。与传统域适应（DA）[14], [46], [32] 和无源适配（SFA）[28], [23], [25] 不同，TTA 面向更实际的场景：源数据与完整目标数据都不可得，适配只依赖流式到达的目标数据批。

English original
Test-Time Adaptation (TTA) [26], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [38], [15], [54] is an emerging research field that tackles domain misalignment when source models are evaluated on shifted target data. Unlike traditional domain adaptation (DA) [14], [46], [32] and source-free adaptation (SFA) [28], [23], [25], TTA addresses more practical scenarios where neither source data nor complete target data are accessible. Instead, the adaptation relies solely on streaming batches of target data.

传统 TTA 方法（图 1a）[26], [45], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [41] 通常采用源到目标（source-to-target）的模型适配框架：通过处理目标数据批持续更新源模型权重。在没有标注的情况下，适配过程要么依赖批级的模型统计量更新 [26], [45], [55], [56]，要么依赖无监督或自监督辅助任务 [50], [13], [41]。然而，小规模或不平衡的批次可能无法很好地代表目标域，使这些方法对数据流的数量与顺序很敏感 [55], [15], [10]。例如，用只含单一类别数据的批去适配模型，会导致过拟合。

English original
Traditional TTA methods (Fig. 1a) [26], [45], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [41] typically employ a source-to-target model adaptation framework. These approaches continuously update the source model weights by processing target data batches. Without annotations, the adaptation process relies either on batch-wise updates of model statistics [26], [45], [55], [56], or unsupervised or self-supervised auxiliary tasks [50], [13], [41]. However, small or imbalanced batches may poorly represent the target domain, making these approaches sensitive to the amount and order of the data stream [55], [15], [10]. For instance, adapting the model with batches containing data from only a single category can lead to overfitting.

近来，扩散模型 [21], [42], [39] 强大的生成能力催生了扩散驱动的 TTA 方法（图 1b）[38], [15], [54]，它们采用目标到源（target-to-source）的框架：用一个在源域预训练的无条件扩散模型，把每个目标样本独立地投影到源域。这样一来，源模型无需修改权重即可做预测。作为早期工作，DiffPure [38] 通过先施加前向扩散过程给目标数据加入少量噪声、再用反向扩散过程恢复出干净图像来逼近源域，从而应对对抗扰动。沿着这个思路，更近的研究 [15], [54] 通过引入目标数据的额外结构引导来处理困难的域偏移——例如严重的数据损坏——以保持语义并提升性能。

English original
Recently, the impressive generation capabilities of diffusion models [21], [42], [39] have sparked the development of diffusion-driven TTA methods (Fig. 1b) [38], [15], [54], leveraging a target-to-source framework. These approaches employ an unconditional diffusion model, pretrained on the source domain, aiming to project each target sample to the source domain independently. This enables the source model to make predictions without modifying its weights. As a preliminary work, DiffPure [38] addresses adversarial perturbations by first applying a forward diffusion process, introducing a small amount of noise to the target data, followed by a reverse diffusion process to restore a clean image to approach the source domain. Building on this concept, more recent studies [15], [54] tackle challenging domain shifts—such as severe data corruption—by incorporating additional structural guidance from the target data, helping preserve semantics and improve performance.

本文发现：扩散驱动的 TTA 方法虽然想把目标数据投影回源域，但投影后的目标数据始终局限在无条件扩散模型的合成域之内。而合成域数据最终仍要交给源域模型处理，这种域失配限制了最终性能。为解决该问题，我们提出合成域对齐（SDA，图 1c），这是一类新的 TTA 框架，它让源模型与目标数据同时对齐到扩散模型的同一个合成域（表 1）。

English original
In this paper, we uncover that while diffusion-driven TTA methods aim to project target data back to the source domain, the projected target data remains confined within the synthetic domain of the unconditional diffusion model. As the synthetic-domain data are ultimately processed by the source-domain model, this domain misalignment limits the final performance. To address this issue, we propose Synthetic-Domain Alignment (SDA) (Fig. 1c), a new category of framework for TTA tasks which simultaneously aligns the domains of the source model and target data with the same synthetic domain of a diffusion model (Tab. 1).

SDA 与现有扩散驱动 TTA 方法 [38], [15], [54] 的区别在于：在用适配后的目标数据测试之前，额外引入一个源到合成域（source-to-synthetic）的模型适配阶段（图 2）。既然适配后的目标数据与无条件扩散模型生成的合成域对齐，SDA 就把源模型也适配到这个相同的合成域。具体而言，SDA 采用「混合扩散」（Mix-of-Diffusion，MoD）技术生成用于模型适配的合成数据。考虑到预训练之后源数据已不可得，MoD 先用条件扩散模型以域无关的标签为条件生成样本，构成带标签的合成数据集；随后，MoD 用前述无条件扩散模型对这些样本加噪再去噪，以处理条件模型与无条件模型之间潜在的域失配。当合成数据集足够大时，微调后的模型在合成域内具有很强的判别能力。于是，SDA 通过让源模型与目标数据同时对齐到同一个合成域，把跨域的 TTA 任务转化为域内的预测任务。

English original
SDA distinguishes itself from existing diffusion-driven TTA methods [38], [15], [54] by introducing an additional source-to-synthetic model adaptation phase before testing on adapted target data (Fig. 2). Since the adapted target data aligns with the synthetic domain generated by an unconditional diffusion model, SDA aims to adapt the source model to this same synthetic domain. Specifically, SDA employs a Mix-of-Diffusion (MoD) technique to generate synthetic data for model adaptation. Given that the source data is inaccessible after pretraining, MoD first uses a conditional diffusion model to generate samples conditioned on domain-agnostic labels, creating a labeled synthetic dataset. Subsequently, the aforementioned unconditional diffusion model is leveraged by MoD add noise to and denoise these samples, addressing potential domain misalignment between the conditional and unconditional models. With a sufficiently large synthetic dataset, the fine-tuned model becomes highly effective at discriminating within the synthetic domain. Thus, the SDA framework transforms the cross-domain TTA task into an in-domain prediction task by aligning both the source model and target data with the same synthetic domain.

SDA 是一个通用框架，不局限于特定的微调技术或扩散驱动的数据适配方法。这种灵活性意味着这些方向的未来进展都能进一步扩展它的适用范围。在分类器、分割器和多模态大语言模型（MLLM，例如 LLaVA）上的大量实验表明，SDA 稳定优于现有方法。此外，可视化分析与消融研究进一步印证了该方法的有效性。

English original
SDA is a general framework, not limited to specific fine-tuning techniques or diffusion-driven data adaptation methods. This flexibility allows future advancements in these areas to further broaden its applicability. Extensive experiments across classifiers, segmenters, and multimodal large language models (MLLM, e.g., LLaVA) demonstrate that SDA consistently outperforms existing methods. Moreover, the effectiveness of our approach is reinforced through visualization analysis and ablation studies.

2 Related Work

测试时适配（TTA）是一个新兴研究方向，通过在流式目标数据批上评估时适配模型 [26], [45], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [41] 或数据 [38], [15], [54] 来应对域偏移。早期的模型适配方法更新批归一化统计量以匹配目标分布 [26], [45], [55], [59]，另一些则利用旋转预测 [50] 或图像复原 [13], [41] 这类自监督任务来调整模型权重。然而，这些方法高度依赖持续的权重更新，因而对目标数据的数量、顺序与多样性都很敏感。相比之下，扩散驱动的 TTA 方法 [38], [15], [54] 着眼于数据适配，把每个目标样本投影回源域，在不做在线模型更新的情况下取得稳定性能。DiffPure [38] 用扩散模型净化对抗样本，而 DDA [15] 与 GDA [54] 用结构引导在严重损坏下保持图像内容。在此基础上，本文研究扩散驱动 TTA 中源模型域与合成图像域之间的失配问题，并提出一个新的合成域对齐 TTA 框架。

English original
Test-time adaptation (TTA) is an emerging research area that addresses domain shifts by adapting either models [26], [45], [55], [56], [59], [50], [13], [41] or data [38], [15], [54] during evaluation on streaming target data batches. Early model adaptation methods update batch normalization statistics to match the target distribution [26], [45], [55], [59], while others leverage self-supervised tasks like rotation prediction [50] or image restoration [13], [41] to adjust model weights. However, these approaches rely heavily on continuous weight updates, making them sensitive to the amount, order, and diversity of target data. In contrast, diffusion-driven TTA methods [38], [15], [54] focus on data adaptation by projecting each target sample back into the source domain, achieving stable performance without online model updates. DiffPure [38] purifies adversarial samples with diffusion models, while DDA [15] and GDA [54] use structural guidance to preserve image content under severe corruption. Building on this, our work investigates the issue of misalignment between domains of the source model and synthetic images in diffusion-driven TTA and proposes a new synthetic-domain alignment TTA framework.

Synthetic data for discriminative tasks. Synthetic data, generated by models rather than collected from the real world, has shown significant potential in enhancing visual representations for various discriminative tasks [51], [52], [12]. It has been effectively applied in areas such as visual recognition [3], [52], object detection [44], [40], semantic segmentation [43], [6], [47], image assessment [17], autonomous driving [1], and robotics [34], [57]. In this work, we explore the potential of leveraging synthetic data, generated by diffusion models, for domain alignment in TTA tasks.

5 结论

English original
5 Conclusion

本文提出合成域对齐（SDA），一个新式的测试时适配（TTA）框架，它把源模型与目标数据同时对齐到扩散模型的合成域。对源模型，SDA 引入「混合扩散」（MoD）技术：用条件扩散模型生成合成数据，用无条件扩散模型做数据对齐，以此把源模型适配成合成域模型。对目标数据，SDA 用前述无条件扩散模型把目标数据投影为合成数据。由于模型与数据处在同一个域内，SDA 把跨域的 TTA 任务转化成了更简单的域内预测任务。与现有扩散驱动 TTA 方法相比，SDA 显著缓解了源—合成域失配问题；与传统 TTA 方法相比，SDA 保持了对不同数据流的不敏感性。在分类器、分割器与 MLLM 上的大量实验表明，SDA 实现了更好的域对齐与更优的性能。

English original
In this paper, we proposed Synthetic-Domain Alignment (SDA), a novel test-time adaptation (TTA) framework that simultaneously aligns the domains of the source model and target data with the synthetic domain of a diffusion model. For the source model, SDA introduces a Mix of Diffusion (MoD) technique, which generates synthetic data to adapt the source model to a synthetic-domain model. MoD involves a conditional diffusion model for data generation and an unconditional diffusion model for data alignment. For the target data, SDA utilizes the aforementioned unconditional diffusion model to project the target data to synthetic data. As the domains of the model and data are aligned, SDA converts the cross-domain TTA task into an easier in-domain prediction task. Compared to existing diffusion-driven TTA methods, SDA significantly mitigates the source-synthetic domain misalignment issue. Compared to traditional TTA methods, SDA maintains insensitivity to different data streams. Extensive experiments across classifiers, segmenters, and MLLMs indicate that SDA achieves enhanced domain alignment and superior performance.
