Tent：只用测试数据的熵最小化适配

Tent: Fully Test-Time Adaptation by Entropy Minimization

Dequan Wang* · Evan Shelhamer*†,3 · Shaoteng Liu · Bruno Olshausen · Trevor Darrell（UC Berkeley / Adobe Research）

部署之后分布变了，而源数据、标签、甚至重新训练的时间都没有。这篇论文只承认测试数据和模型自己的参数，用「让预测更自信」这一件事去适配：估计归一化统计量、优化逐通道仿射参数，每个 batch 在线更新一次。它在损坏的 ImageNet 与 CIFAR-10/100 上降低了误差，还刷新了 ImageNet-C 的最好成绩。

这一段是译者注，不是原文。它只做一件事：让你在读到第 2、3 节时，知道每个量在做什么。

  1. 设定：模型 fθf_\thetafθ​ 已经在源数据上训完，测试时只剩下目标数据 xtx^txt（无标签）和模型参数。既不能回看源数据，也不能用目标标签——这就是 fully test-time adaptation。
  2. 目标：对预测 y^=fθ(xt)\hat{y} = f_\theta(x^t)y^​=fθ​(xt) 最小化香农熵 H(y^)H(\hat{y})H(y^​)。熵低 = 预测尖锐 = 更自信。单条预测的熵最小化有平凡解（把所有概率压到一个类），所以必须对同一 batch 内的多条预测联合优化、共享参数。
  3. 优化什么：不改 θ\thetaθ，只改每个归一化层的 γ,β\gamma, \betaγ,β（逐通道的缩放与平移）以及由当前 batch 现算的 μ,σ\mu, \sigmaμ,σ。参数量不到全模型的 1%。
  4. 怎么看结果：所有表格都是错误率（%），越低越好；ImageNet-C 的对比对象是「改训练」的鲁棒训练方法（ANT、AugMix、SIN），Tent 不改训练。

摘要

English original
Abstract

模型必须在测试期间适配自身，才能泛化到新的、不同的数据。在这种「完全测试时适配」的设定下，模型只有测试数据和自己的参数。我们提出用测试熵最小化来做适配（tent[1]）：以预测熵衡量的置信度为目标来优化模型。我们的方法估计归一化统计量，并优化逐通道仿射变换，从而在每一个 batch 上在线更新。Tent 降低了损坏的 ImageNet 与 CIFAR-10/100 上图像分类的泛化误差，并在 ImageNet-C 上刷新了当前最好的错误率。Tent 也能处理无源域适配：SVHN 到 MNIST/MNIST-M/USPS 的数字识别、GTA 到 Cityscapes 的语义分割，以及 VisDA-C 基准。这些结果是在一个 epoch 的测试时优化内、且不改变训练的情况下取得的。

English original
A model must adapt itself to generalize to new and different data during testing. In this setting of fully test-time adaptation the model has only the test data and its own parameters. We propose to adapt by test entropy minimization (tent[1]): we optimize the model for confidence as measured by the entropy of its predictions. Our method estimates normalization statistics and optimizes channel-wise affine transformations to update online on each batch. Tent reduces generalization error for image classification on corrupted ImageNet and CIFAR-10/100 and reaches a new state-of-the-art error on ImageNet-C. Tent handles source-free domain adaptation on digit recognition from SVHN to MNIST/MNIST-M/USPS, on semantic segmentation from GTA to Cityscapes, and on the VisDA-C benchmark. These results are achieved in one epoch of test-time optimization without altering training.

1 引言

English original
1 Introduction

深度网络在来自同一分布的训练与测试数据上可以达到很高的准确率，基准测试上的巨大进步已经证明了这一点 (Krizhevsky 等, 2012)、(Simonyan & Zisserman, 2015)、(He 等, 2016)。

English original
Deep networks can achieve high accuracy on training and testing data from the same distribution, as evidenced by tremendous benchmark progress (Krizhevsky et al., 2012; Simonyan & Zisserman, 2015; He et al., 2016).

然而，对新数据与不同数据的泛化能力是有限的 (Hendrycks & Dietterich, 2019)、(Recht 等, 2019)、(Geirhos 等, 2018)。当训练（源）数据与测试（目标）数据不一致时，准确率就会下降，这种情形称为数据集偏移（dataset shift）(Quionero-Candela 等, 2009)。模型可能对训练时未知的测试期变化很敏感，无论是自然变化还是损坏，例如意料之外的天气或传感器退化。尽管如此，把模型部署到不同的数据分布上有时是必要的，因此需要适配。

English original
However, generalization to new and different data is limited (Hendrycks & Dietterich, 2019; Recht et al., 2019; Geirhos et al., 2018). Accuracy suffers when the training (source) data differ from the testing (target) data, a condition known as dataset shift (Quionero-Candela et al., 2009). Models can be sensitive to shifts during testing that were not known during training, whether natural variations or corruptions, such as unexpected weather or sensor degradation. Nevertheless, it can be necessary to deploy a model on different data distributions, so adaptation is needed.

在测试期间，模型必须只凭自己的参数和目标数据来适配。这种完全测试时适配设定不能依赖源数据或监督信息。当模型第一次遇到新的测试数据、尚来不及收集与标注时，这两者都不可行，因为推理必须继续下去。现实使用从数据、计算与任务三方面对完全测试时适配提出了要求：

  1. 可用性。模型可能出于带宽、隐私或商业考虑，在不附带源数据的情况下被分发。
  2. 效率。在测试期间（重新）处理源数据在计算上可能并不现实。
  3. 准确率。不经适配的模型可能精度不足，无法完成它的用途。
English original
During testing, the model must adapt given only its parameters and the target data. This fully test-time adaptation setting cannot rely on source data or supervision. Neither is practical when the model first encounters new testing data, before it can be collected and annotated, as inference must go on. Real-world usage motivates fully test-time adaptation by data, computation, and task needs: (1) Availability. A model might be distributed without source data for bandwidth, privacy, or profit. (2) Efficiency. It might not be computationally practical to (re-)process source data during testing. (3) Accuracy. A model might be too inaccurate without adaptation to serve its purpose.

为了在测试期间适配，我们最小化模型预测的熵。我们把这个目标称为测试熵，并据此把方法命名为 tent。选择熵，是因为它与误差、与偏移都有联系。熵与误差相关：更有把握的预测总体上更正确（图 1）。熵也与损坏带来的偏移相关：损坏越重，熵越大，而且随着损坏程度增加，熵与图像分类损失的秩相关性很强（图 2）。

English original
To adapt during testing we minimize the entropy of model predictions. We call this objective the test entropy and name our method tent after it. We choose entropy for its connections to error and shift. Entropy is related to error, as more confident predictions are all-in-all more correct (Fig. 1). Entropy is related to shifts due to corruption, as more corruption results in more entropy, with a strong rank correlation to the loss for image classification as the level of corruption increases (Fig. 2).

为了最小化熵，tent 对目标数据上的推理做归一化与变换：逐个 batch 地估计统计量并优化仿射参数。这种低维、逐通道的特征调制在测试期间适配起来很高效，即使做在线更新也是如此。Tent 不限制也不改变模型训练：给定模型参数，它与源数据无关。只要能跑模型，就能做适配。最重要的一点是，tent 有效降低的不只是熵，还有误差。

English original
To minimize entropy, tent normalizes and transforms inference on target data by estimating statistics and optimizing affine parameters batch-by-batch. This choice of low-dimensional, channel-wise feature modulation is efficient to adapt during testing, even for online updates. Tent does not restrict or alter model training: it is independent of the source data given the model parameters. If the model can be run, it can be adapted. Most importantly, tent effectively reduces not just entropy but error.

我们的实验结果评估了三类泛化：图像分类对损坏的泛化、数字识别对域偏移的泛化、语义分割对仿真到真实偏移的泛化。为了在有更多数据与优化的条件下作对照，我们也评估了在给定带标签源数据时的鲁棒训练、域适配与自监督学习方法。Tent 仅凭目标数据就能取得更低的误差，并改进了 ImageNet-C 基准上的最好成绩。分析实验支持了我们的熵目标，检查了方法对数据量和适配参数选择的敏感性，并验证了 tent 跨架构的通用性。

English original
Our results evaluate generalization to corruptions for image classification, to domain shift for digit recognition, and to simulation-to-real shift for semantic segmentation. For context with more data and optimization, we evaluate methods for robust training, domain adaptation, and self-supervised learning given the labeled source data. Tent can achieve less error given only the target data, and it improves on the state-of-the-art for the ImageNet-C benchmark. Analysis experiments support our entropy objective, check sensitivity to the amount of data and the choice of parameters for adaptation, and back the generality of tent across architectures.

Proxy tasks derive a self-supervised label y′y'y′ from the input xtx_txt​ without the task label yyy. Examples of these proxies include rotation prediction (Gidaris et al., 2018), context prediction (Doersch et al., 2015), and cross-channel auto-encoding (Zhang et al., 2020). Too much progress on a proxy task could interfere with performance on the supervised task, and self-supervised adaptation methods have to limit or mix updates accordingly (Sun et al., 2019b; Sun et al., 2019a). As such, care is needed to choose a proxy compatible with the domain and task, to design the architecture for the proxy model, and to balance optimization between the task and proxy objectives. Our entropy objective does not need such efforts.

Tent 可扩展到 VisDA-C 挑战赛。为了在更难的基准上展示适配，我们在 VisDA-C 挑战赛上评估 (Peng 等, 2017)。任务是对 12 个类别的物体识别，源数据由渲染 3D 模型合成，目标数据采集自真实场景。我们源模型（ResNet-50，在 ImageNet 上预训练）的验证错误率为 56.1%，而 tent 达到 45.6%，并按 Liang 等 (2020) 的做法更新除最后分类器之外的所有层后，进一步改进到 39.6%。虽然通过模型适配 (Li 等, 2020) 或 SHOT (Liang 等, 2020) 的离线无源适配可以用更多计算与调参达到更低错误率，但 tent 能在测试期间在线适配。

English original
Tent scales to the VisDA-C challenge. To show adaptation on a more difficult benchmark, we evaluate on the VisDA-C challenge (Peng et al., 2017). The task is object recognition for 12 classes where the source data is synthesized by rendering 3D models and the target data is collected from real scenes. The validation error for our source model (ResNet-50, pretrained on ImageNet) is 56.1%, while tent reaches 45.6%, and improves to 39.6% by updating all layers except for the final classifier as done by Liang et al. (2020). Although offline source-free adaptation by model adaptation (Li et al., 2020) or SHOT (Liang et al., 2020) can reach lower error with more computation and tuning, tent can adapt online during testing.

4.3 Analysis

Tent reduces entropy and error. Figure 6 verifies tent does indeed reduce the entropy and the task loss (softmax cross-entropy). We plot changes in entropy and loss on CIFAR-100-C for all 75 corruption type/level combinations. Tent 需要特征调制。我们对特征调制的归一化与变换两步做消融。不更新归一化会增加错误，而且可能无法优于 BN 与 PL。不更新变换参数，方法就退化为测试时归一化。只更新模型的最后一层可以先有改进，但继续优化就会退化。更新全模型参数 θ\thetaθ 从不优于未适配的源模型。

English original
Tent needs feature modulation. We ablate the normalization and transformation steps of feature modulation. Not updating normalization increases errors, and can fail to improve over BN and PL. Not updating transformation parameters reduces the method to test-time normalization. Updating only the last layer of the model can improve but then degrades with further optimization. Updating the full model parameters θ\thetaθ never improves over the unadapted source model.

Tent 跨目标数据泛化。适配可能只对用于更新的那些点有效。我们检查适配是否跨点泛化：在目标训练集上适配，而不在目标测试集上适配。测试错误下降：CIFAR-100-C 的错误率从 37.3% 降到 34.2%，SVHN 到 MNIST 的错误率从 8.2% 降到 6.5%。（训练集比测试集大；当子采样到相同规模时，错误率相差不到 0.1%。）因此适配后的调制不是逐点特定的，而是通用的。

English original
Tent generalizes across target data. Adaptation could be limited to the points used for updates. We check that adaptation generalizes across points by adapting on target train and not target test. Test errors drop: CIFAR-100-C error goes from 37.3% to 34.2% and SVHN-to-MNIST error goes from 8.2% to 6.5%. (Train is larger than test; when subsampling to the same size errors differ by <0.1%.) Therefore the adapted modulation is not point specific but general.

Tent 的调制不同于归一化。调制会归一化并变换特征。我们考察它的综合效果。图 7 把损坏数据上适配后的特征与未损坏数据上的参考特征作对比。我们画出 source 模型、归一化、tent，以及一个在目标标签上优化的 oracle 的特征。归一化让特征更像参考，tent 则不然。Tent 反而让特征更像 oracle。这提示了一种不同的、与任务相关的效果。更多层的可视化见附录 C。

Tent modulation differs from normalization. Modulation normalizes and transforms features. We examine the combined effect. Figure 7 contrasts adapted features on corrupted data against reference features on uncorrupted data. We plot features from the source model, normalization, tent, and an oracle that optimizes on the target labels. Normalization makes features more like the reference, but tent does not. Instead, tent makes features more like the oracle. This suggests a different and task-specific effect. See the appendix for visualizations of more layers (Appendix C).

Tent 适配其他架构。原则上 tent 与架构无关。为了衡量它在实践中的通用性，我们在 CIFAR-100-C 上评估基于自注意力（SAN）(Zhao 等, 2020) 与平衡求解（MDEQ）(Bai 等, 2020) 的新架构的损坏鲁棒性。表 4 显示，tent 用与卷积残差网络相同的设置就降低了错误。

English original
5 Related Work

我们把 tent 与已有的适配、熵最小化和特征调制方法联系起来。

English original
We relate tent to existing adaptation, entropy minimization, and feature modulation methods.

训练期适配。域适配通过跨域损失 L(xs,xt)L(x^s, x^t)L(xs,xt) 在源与目标上联合优化，以缓解偏移。这类损失优化的是特征对齐 (Gretton 等, 2009)、(Sun 等, 2017)，对抗不变性 (Ganin & Lempitsky, 2015)、(Tzeng 等, 2017)，或共享的代理任务 (Sun 等, 2019a)。直推式学习（transduction）(Gammerman 等, 1998)、(Joachims, 1999)、(Zhou 等, 2004) 在训练与测试上联合优化，以更好地拟合特定的测试实例。这些方法在各自的设定中有效，但当不允许把源／训练与目标／测试联合使用时，两者都不适用。Tent 只在目标上适配。

English original
Train-Time Adaptation. Domain adaptation jointly optimizes on source and target by cross-domain losses L(xs,xt)L(x^s, x^t)L(xs,xt) to mitigate shift. These losses optimize feature alignment (Gretton et al., 2009; Sun et al., 2017), adversarial invariance (Ganin & Lempitsky, 2015; Tzeng et al., 2017), or shared proxy tasks (Sun et al., 2019a). Transduction (Gammerman et al., 1998; Joachims, 1999; Zhou et al., 2004) jointly optimizes on train and test to better fit specific test instances. While effective in their settings, neither applies when joint use of source/train and target/test is denied. Tent adapts on target alone.

近来的「无源」方法 (Li 等, 2020)、(Kundu 等, 2020)、(Liang 等, 2020) 同样不使用源数据来适配。Li 等 (2020) 与 Kundu 等 (2020) 依赖生成建模，并用多个损失优化多个模型。Kundu 等 (2020) 与 Liang 等 (2020) 还改变了训练。Tent 既不需要生成建模，也不改变训练，因此可以更普遍地部署、以高得多的计算效率做在线适配。SHOT (Liang 等, 2020) 通过信息最大化（熵最小化加多样性正则）适配，但在其他损失与参数化方式上不同。这些无源方法用多个损失离线优化多个 epoch，比 tent 需要更多调参与计算，但可能用更多计算换来更高准确率。Tent 用单个损失在线优化，并采用高效的调制参数化，以突出推理期的完全测试时适配。我们鼓励对「不使用源数据的适配」这一前沿上的每一项工作进行考察。

English original
Recent “source-free” methods (Li et al., 2020; Kundu et al., 2020; Liang et al., 2020) also adapt without source data. Li et al. (2020; Kundu et al., 2020) rely on generative modeling and optimize multiple models with multiple losses. Kundu et al. (2020; Liang et al., 2020) also alter training. Tent does not need generative modeling, nor does it alter training, and so it can deployed more generally to adapt online with much more computational efficiency. SHOT (Liang et al., 2020) adapts by information maximization (entropy minimization and diversity regularization), but differs in its other losses and its parameterization. These source-free methods optimize offline with multiple losses for multiple epochs, which requires more tuning and computation than tent, but may achieve more accuracy with more computation. Tent optimizes online with just one loss and an efficient parameterization of modulation to emphasize fully test-time adaptation during inference. We encourage examination of each of these works on the frontier of adaptation without source data.

Chidlovskii 等 (2016) 最早出于法律、商业或技术上的考虑，论证了不使用源数据做适配的动机。他们通过应用去噪自编码器来适配预测，而我们通过熵最小化来适配模型。我们与他们的动机相同，但方法与实验不同。

English original
Chidlovskii et al. (2016) are the first to motivate adaptation without source data for legal, commercial, or technical concerns. They adapt predictions by applying denoising auto-encoders while we adapt models by entropy minimization. We share their motivations, but the methods and experiments differ.

测试时适配。Tent 通过测试时优化与归一化来更新模型。对预测做测试时适配——根据容易且确定的样本去调整困难和不确定的样本 (Jain & Learned-Miller, 2011)——为我们这类基于确定性的模型适配方案提供了启发。

English original
Test-Time Adaptation. Tent adapts by test-time optimization and normalization to update the model. Test-time adaptation of predictions, through which harder and uncertain cases are adjusted based on easier and certain cases (Jain & Learned-Miller, 2011), provides inspiration for certainty-based model adaptation schemes like our own.

测试时训练（TTT）(Sun 等, 2019b) 也在测试期间优化，但损失不同，而且必须改变训练。TTT 依赖一个代理任务，例如识别图像的旋转，因此它的损失取决于代理的选择。（事实上，其作者也提醒，代理必须「在新域中既定义良好又非平凡」。）TTT 改变训练，以便在适配到目标之前在源上优化这个代理损失。Tent 既不需要代理任务，也不改变训练。

English original
Test-time training (TTT) (Sun et al., 2019b) also optimizes during testing, but differs in its loss and must alter training. TTT relies on a proxy task, such as recognizing rotations of an image, and so its loss depends on the choice of proxy. (Indeed, its authors caution that the proxy must be “both well-defined and non-trivial in the new domain”.) TTT alters training to optimize this proxy loss on source before adapting to target. Tent adapts without proxy tasks and without altering training.

归一化特征统计量在域适配中很常见 (Gretton 等, 2009)、(Sun 等, 2017)。批归一化方面，Li 等 (2017) 与 Carlucci 等 (2017) 在训练时区分源与目标的统计量。Schneider 等 (2020) 与 Nado 等 (2020) 在测试期间估计目标统计量以改进泛化。Tent 建立在测试时归一化之上，进一步降低泛化误差。

English original
Normalizing feature statistics is common for domain adaptation (Gretton et al., 2009; Sun et al., 2017). For batch normalization Li et al. (2017; Carlucci et al., 2017) separate source and target statistics during training. Schneider et al. (2020; Nado et al., 2020) estimate target statistics during testing to improve generalization. Tent builds on test-time normalization to further reduce generalization error.

熵最小化。熵最小化是域适配 (Carlucci 等, 2017)、(Shu 等, 2018)、(Saito 等, 2019)、(Roy 等, 2019)、半监督学习 (Grandvalet & Bengio, 2005)、(Lee, 2013)、(Berthelot 等, 2019) 以及少样本学习 (Dhillon 等, 2020) 的重要正则项。对熵做正则，会惩罚数据分布中高密度处的决策，从而提高对易混淆类别的准确率 (Grandvalet & Bengio, 2005)。这些方法在训练期间把熵正则与额外数据上的其他监督、无监督损失一起使用。Tent 首次在测试期间最小化熵，用于适配数据集偏移，而不需要其他损失或数据。熵损失本身很常见；我们的贡献在于展示熵作为唯一损失用于完全测试时适配。

English original
Entropy Minimization. Entropy minimization is a key regularizer for domain adaptation (Carlucci et al., 2017; Shu et al., 2018; Saito et al., 2019; Roy et al., 2019), semi-supervised learning (Grandvalet & Bengio, 2005; Lee, 2013; Berthelot et al., 2019), and few-shot learning (Dhillon et al., 2020). Regularizing entropy penalizes decisions at high densities in the data distribution to improve accuracy for distinct classes (Grandvalet & Bengio, 2005). These methods regularize entropy during training in concert with other supervised and unsupervised losses on additional data. Tent is the first to minimize entropy during testing, for adaptation to dataset shifts, without other losses or data. Entropic losses are common; our contribution is to exhibit entropy as the sole loss for fully test-time adaptation.

特征调制。调制让模型随输入而变化。为稳定、高效地适配，我们优化的调制比整个模型更简单。我们调制逐通道的仿射变换，因为它在与归一化配合时有效 (Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015)、(Wu & He, 2018)，也因为它在不同任务的条件化上很灵活 (Perez 等, 2018)。这些归一化与条件化方法在训练期间用监督损失优化调制，但在测试期间保持固定。我们在测试期间用无监督损失优化调制，使它能够适配不同的目标数据。

English original
Feature Modulation. Modulation makes a model vary with its input. We optimize modulations that are simpler than the full model for stable and efficient adaptation. We modulate channel-wise affine transformations, for their effectiveness in tandem with normalization (Ioffe & Szegedy, 2015; Wu & He, 2018), and for their flexibility in conditioning for different tasks (Perez et al., 2018). These normalization and conditioning methods optimize the modulation during training by a supervised loss, but keep it fixed during testing. We optimize the modulation during testing by an unsupervised loss, so that it can adapt to different target data.

6 讨论

English original
6 Discussion

Tent 通过测试时熵最小化降低了偏移数据上的泛化误差。在最小化熵的过程中，模型根据自己预测的反馈来适配自身。这是真正的自监督式自我改进。这类自监督完全由监督任务定义，不同于为了从数据中提取更多监督而设计的代理任务，然而令人惊讶的是它仍然降低了误差。不过，由损坏和其他偏移引起的误差依然存在，因此还需要更多适配。下一步应当追求在更多、更难的偏移类型上，在更一般的参数上，以及用更有效、更高效的损失来做测试时适配。

English original
Tent reduces generalization error on shifted data by test-time entropy minimization. In minimizing entropy, the model adapts itself to feedback from its own predictions. This is truly self-supervised self-improvement. Self-supervision of this sort is totally defined by the supervised task, unlike proxy tasks designed to extract more supervision from the data, and yet it remarkably still reduces error. Nevertheless, errors due to corruption and other shifts remain, and therefore more adaptation is needed. Next steps should pursue test-time adaptation on more and harder types of shift, over more general parameters, and by more effective and efficient losses.

偏移。Tent 降低了多种偏移下的误差，包括图像损坏、数字外观的简单变化，以及仿真到真实的差异。这些偏移作为标准化基准很流行，但现实世界中还有别的偏移。例如，CIFAR 10.1 与 ImageNetV2 测试集 (Recht 等, 2018)、(Recht 等, 2019) 是通过复制数据集收集流程得到的，带来自然的、未知的偏移。虽然两个集合上的误差都更高（说明存在偏移），tent 并未改进泛化。对抗偏移 (Szegedy 等, 2014) 同样威胁现实使用，而且攻击者会不断适应防御。虽然对抗训练 (Madry 等, 2018) 能起作用，测试时适配也可能有助于对抗这类测试时攻击。

English original
Shifts. Tent reduces error for a variety of shifts including image corruptions, simple changes in appearance for digits, and simulation-to-real discrepancies. These shifts are popular as standardized benchmarks, but other real-world shifts exist. For instance, the CIFAR 10.1 and ImageNetV2 test sets (Recht et al., 2018; Recht et al., 2019), made by reproducing the dataset collection procedures, entail natural but unknown shifts. Although error is higher on both sets, indicating the presence of shift, tent does not improve generalization. Adversarial shifts (Szegedy et al., 2014) also threaten real-world usage, and attackers keep adapting to defenses. While adversarial training (Madry et al., 2018) makes a difference, test-time adaptation could help counter such test-time attacks.

参数。Tent 通过归一化与变换来调制模型，但模型的大部分保持不变。测试时适配可以更新模型的更多部分，问题在于找出既具表达力又可靠的参数，而这可能与损失的选择相互作用。TTT 适配由监督模型与自监督模型共享的多层特征，SHOT 适配除最后一层（或几层）之外的全部模型。这些选择取决于模型架构、损失与调参。对 tent 而言调制是可靠的，但 VisDA 上更大的偏移更适合用 SHOT 的参数化来处理。联合适配输入可能是一种更通用的替代方案。如果模型能在目标上适配自身，那么或许可以用它的输入梯度来优化空间变换或图像平移，从而在不使用源数据的情况下减小偏移。

English original
Parameters. Tent modulates the model by normalization and transformation, but much of the model stays fixed. Test-time adaptation could update more of the model, but the issue is to identify parameters that are both expressive and reliable, and this may interact with the choice of loss. TTT adapts multiple layers of features shared by supervised and self-supervised models and SHOT adapts all but the last layer(s) of the model. These choices depend on the model architecture, the loss, and tuning. For tent modulation is reliable, but the larger shift on VisDA is better addressed by the SHOT parameterization. Jointly adapting the input could be a more general alternative. If a model can adapt itself on target, then perhaps its input gradients might optimize spatial transformations or image translations to reduce shift without source data.

损失。Tent 最小化熵。为了更强的适配，是否存在一个对一般化但逐例（episodic）的测试时优化有效的损失？熵跨任务通用，但作用范围有限。它需要 batch 才能优化，无法一次一个点地做逐例更新。TTT 可以这样做，但前提是选对了代理任务。为了更少的计算，是否存在一个适合更局部优化的高效损失？Tent 与 TTT 都需要为更新而完整（重新）计算模型，因为它们依赖模型的预测。如果损失改为定义在表示上，更新所需的前向与反向计算就会更少。回到熵本身：这个损失可能与校准 (Guo 等, 2017) 相互作用，因为更好的不确定性估计可能带来更好的适配。

English original
Losses. Tent minimizes entropy. For more adaptation, is there an effective loss for general but episodic test-time optimization? Entropy is general across tasks but limited in scope. It needs batches for optimization, and cannot update episodically on one point at a time. TTT can do so, but only with the right proxy task. For less computation, is there an efficient loss for more local optimization? Tent and TTT both require full (re-)computation of the model for updates because they depend on its predictions. If the loss were instead defined on the representation, then updates would require less forward and backward computation. Returning to entropy specifically, this loss may interact with calibration (Guo et al., 2017), as better uncertainty estimation could drive better adaptation.

我们希望完全测试时适配这一设定能推动新方法，让模型具备自我适配的能力——正如 tent 在每一次更新中都会产生一个新模型。

English original
We hope that the fully test-time adaptation setting can promote new methods for equipping a model to adapt itself, just as tent yields a new model with every update.
